Nov 23 (Reuters) - Nvidia on Monday said it was reinstating Jensen Huang as CEO, effective immediately, an extraordinary reversal that followed calls from US President Donald Trump for his return after Huang resigned over revelations of extramarital affairs, including one with an alleged Chinese spy. The reversal, which sent shares soaring 33%, came after the board said it saw no national security risk in the return of Huang, who had also allegedly carried on affairs with at least two Nvidia employees. Multiple investigations of the company are underway, including by the US SEC and the Department of Justice, related to everything from potential accounting issues to alleged chip smuggling.
11月23日（路透社）——英伟达周一宣布，将恢复黄仁勋的首席执行官职务，立即生效。这一不同寻常的决定逆转发生在美国总统唐纳德·特朗普呼吁黄仁勋回归之后。此前，黄仁勋因婚外情被曝光而辞职，其中包括一段据称与一名涉嫌为中国从事间谍活动的女子之间的关系。这一决定使英伟达股价飙升33%。此前，董事会表示，黄仁勋的回归不会构成国家安全风险。据称，黄仁勋还曾与至少两名英伟达员工发生婚外情。目前，英伟达正面临多项调查，包括美国证券交易委员会和司法部开展的调查，涉及的问题从潜在的会计问题到涉嫌芯片走私等多个方面。

参考答案

Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia's CEO after reports of extramarital affairs, including one involving an alleged Chinese spy. Although the board initially ruled out his return, President Trump urged the company to reinstate him. A petition also attracted more than 180,000 signatures. In a dramatic reversal, the board unanimously approved his reinstatement, saying it was satisfied with his candor and leadership ability. Nvidia's shares soared following the announcement. However, several investigations are still ongoing, and the company continues to face questions about national security, compliance and its dependence on Huang.

I believe business success should not come at the cost of ethical responsibility. A talented founder can bring enormous value to a company, but no individual should be above its rules. If a company depends too heavily on one leader, it may face serious risks when that person makes mistakes. True corporate success requires not only strong leadership and financial growth, but also transparency, accountability and public trust.
我认为，商业上的成功不应该以牺牲道德责任为代价。一位才华出众的创始人能够为公司带来巨大的价值，但任何个人都不应该凌驾于公司的规则之上。如果一家公司过度依赖某一位领导者，那么当这个人犯错时，公司就可能面临严重的风险。真正的企业成功不仅需要强大的领导力和财务增长，还需要透明度、问责机制以及公众的信任。
