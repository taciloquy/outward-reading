A hurricane is coming to tax systems

一场飓风正扑向税收体系

THE YIELD on ten-year US Treasuries has charged through the 5% barrier. The spread between French government bonds and German Bunds is at levels not seen since the euro-zone crisis in 2012.

十年期美国国债收益率已冲破 5% 关口。法国政府债券与德国国债之间的利差，达到 2012 年欧元区危机以来未见的水平。

Britain's newish prime minister has this week set out a plan for spending bolstered by a taboo-busting promise to curb state-pension increases—and yet is still in a fiscal pickle.

英国那位上任不久的首相本周公布了一项支出计划，靠的是一项打破禁忌的承诺——限制国家养老金上调——可即便如此，她仍陷在财政困境里。

Across the rich world, countries are struggling to coax more growth out of their economies. And that's not the worst of it.

在整个富裕世界，各国都在艰难地想从自家经济里多挤出一点增长。而最糟的还不是这个。

As we explain this week, the tax base in all countries, which depends heavily on taxing labour, may be upended by artificial intelligence.

正如我们本周所解释的，所有国家的税基都严重依赖对劳动征税，而它可能被人工智能颠覆。

Add these problems together, and it is as if a mighty hurricane were about to hit the rich world's tax systems—which are hopelessly unprepared. The way the state raises money is wasteful, unfair and perverse.

把这些问题加在一起，就好像一场强飓风即将袭击富裕世界的税收体系——而它们毫无准备。国家筹钱的方式既浪费、又不公平，还充满扭曲。

You would have thought governments would be scrambling to strengthen the system before the hurricane makes landfall, but as ever they are running away from tax reform instead.

你本以为各国政府会在飓风登陆前抢着加固体系，但像往常一样，它们在逃避税制改革。

The accumulated flaws of modern tax systems are so ubiquitous that they have become part of the economic backdrop. Taxes are full of needless complexity.

现代税制日积月累的缺陷无处不在，以至于已成为经济背景的一部分。税收里塞满了不必要的复杂性。

In Germany the rate of value-added tax (VAT) on oat milk is more than double that on cow's milk, for example. Such widespread special treatment has ruinous fiscal consequences.

例如在德国，燕麦奶的增值税率比牛奶高出一倍多。这种普遍存在的特殊待遇，带来了灾难性的财政后果。

Across the OECD, a club of mostly rich countries, consumption taxes raise only 58% of what they could if there were no carve-outs and no evasion. Tax systems also create perverse incentives.

在主要由富裕国家组成的 OECD，消费税只筹集到"若无减免、也无逃税"时本可筹到的 58%。税制还制造了扭曲的激励。

When a parent in Britain increases their net income from £99,999 ($131,500) to £100,000, they automatically lose free child care worth thousands of pounds. This can prompt some of Britain's most productive workers to cut their hours or overstuff their pensions.

在英国，一位家长把净收入从 99999 英镑（13.15 万美元）提高到 10 万英镑时，会自动失去价值数千英镑的免费托育。这可能促使英国一些生产率最高的劳动者减少工时，或往养老金里猛塞钱。

And taxes are widely seen as unfair. In Britain the median employee pays 55% more tax than their self-employed counterparts. Many people feel the rich aren't doing their bit.

而税收被广泛视为不公平。在英国，普通雇员缴纳的税比自雇者高出 55%。许多人觉得富人们没有尽自己的一份力。

In California voters will decide in November whether to charge a one-off 5% levy on residents with wealth of over $1bn. No one knows when the fiscal weather will turn. But rapid advances in AI could accelerate matters.

在加利福尼亚，选民将在 11 月决定是否对财富超过 10 亿美元的居民征收一次性 5% 的税。没人知道财政天气何时变脸。但人工智能的飞速进步可能让事态加速。

Taxes on labour are the workhorse of most tax systems worldwide, raising around half of all revenue in the OECD.

对劳动征税是全球大多数税制的"主力马"，在 OECD 贡献了约一半的收入。

In the coming years AI could well displace workers or cut their wages even as corporate profits rise, shrinking labour's share of GDP. This in turn could reduce revenues and increase benefits payments, making deficits even harder to support.

未来几年，即便企业利润上升，AI 也可能取代劳动者或压低其工资，压缩劳动在 GDP 中的份额。这反过来会减少收入、增加福利支出，让赤字更难支撑。

Optimists may hope that superfast growth will make the maths easier to handle. Yet The Economist has looked at what would happen in rich countries if labour's share of national income fell by ten points.

乐观者或许指望超高速增长能让这笔账更好算。但《经济学人》研究了富裕国家若劳动在国民收入中的份额下降 10 个百分点会发生什么。

By our calculations, Italy's budget deficit would double as a share of GDP and Germany and France would not fare much better. In addition, the capacity for tax avoidance would no longer be limited by human ingenuity.

按我们的计算，意大利的预算赤字占 GDP 之比会翻倍，德国和法国也好不了多少。此外，避税的能力将不再受人类才智的限制。

Point countless AI agents at Britain's 23,000-page tax code and hundreds of new tax-avoidance strategies are likely to emerge—and don't bet on the Treasury to keep up. What to do?

把无数 AI 智能体对准英国长达 23000 页的税法，很可能冒出数百种新的避税策略——别指望财政部能跟得上。怎么办？

Given that AI is likely to mint vast fortunes for a select few, countries may be inclined to follow California's proposal and introduce wealth taxes.

鉴于 AI 很可能为少数人铸造巨额财富，各国可能倾向于效仿加州的提议，开征财富税。

Yet such taxes will probably backfire, either because they are too difficult to administer or because they cause rich people to flee. Previous attempts have proved unworkable. In 1990, 12 rich countries taxed fortunes; today just four do.

然而这类税很可能适得其反——要么因为太难征管，要么因为逼得富人跑路。此前的尝试已被证明行不通。1990 年有 12 个富裕国家对财富征税，今天只剩 4 个。

A more fundamental overhaul is needed. Wealth should be taxed more via capital-gains taxes, which currently overpenalise normal returns and undertax supernormal ones. A start would be to introduce a higher headline rate and allowances that exempt normal returns.

需要的是更根本的改造。应当更多通过资本利得税来课税——目前它对正常回报惩罚过重，对超额回报课税不足。起步可以是设定更高的名义税率，并给正常回报以免税额。

At the same time, the burden on labour should lighten. That would be a hedge against disruption from AI, and remove an obstacle to employment in countries where labour taxes are crushing.

与此同时，应减轻劳动身上的负担。这既是对冲 AI 冲击的手段，也能在劳动税沉重的国家消除就业障碍。

In Belgium they constitute 52% of the costs of employing a person. Consumption should be taxed more heavily, with a focus on ending the litany of exemptions. That would have the useful side-effect of taxing the spending of the very rich.

在比利时，劳动税占到雇佣一个人成本的 52%。消费应当被课得更重，重点是终结那一长串豁免。这还有个有用的副作用：能征到最富有者消费的税。

This might not curb the influence Elon Musk can buy, but it would ensure the state gets a bigger cut when Mr Musk buys another plane. Finally, codes should be radically simplified. None of this will be easy.

这也许遏制不了马斯克能买到的影响力，但能确保他在再买一架飞机时，国家能分到更大一块。最后，税法应当被彻底简化。这些都来之不易。

The losers will hiss more loudly than the winners will cheer. But change is coming, like it or not. The country that gets ahead of the hurricane will one day thank the politicians who had the foresight to act.

输家的嘘声会盖过赢家的欢呼。但不管你喜不喜欢，变革正在到来。那个跑在飓风前面的国家，终有一天会感谢当初有远见采取行动的政治人物。
