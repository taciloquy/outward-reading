October 17 French protests: Crowds marched through the streets before violence, looting and chaos broke out

10 月 17 日法国抗议：游行之后，暴力、抢劫与混乱爆发

On Saturday, 800,000 people took to the streets according to organizers, while Interior Minister Laurent Nuñez put the number at 550,000.

周六，据组织者称有 80 万人走上街头，而内政部长洛朗·努涅斯给出的数字是 55 万人。

The same pattern played out in many cities: On one side, a crowd of peaceful protesters; on the other, independent groups determined to clash with the police.

同样的模式在许多城市上演：一边是一群和平示威者；另一边是决意与警察冲突的独立团体。

While turnout across the country was massive, the protests were also marred by unprecedented property damage, violence and looting. In France, several worlds seemed to have collided over one long day.

全国参与人数固然庞大，但抗议也被空前的财产损失、暴力和趁乱打劫所破坏。在法国，似乎有几个世界在漫长的一天里撞在了一起。

The roots of the protests stretched back to September, when calls for demonstrations against rising fuel prices began circulating on social media. Meanwhile, high school students were protesting teacher shortages, overcrowded classrooms and poor school conditions.

抗议的根源可以追溯到 9 月——当时反对燃油价格上涨的示威号召开始在社交媒体上流传。与此同时，高中生们也在抗议教师短缺、教室过度拥挤和糟糕的学校条件。

The unrest also revived memories of the Yellow Vest movement, which began in 2018 as a protest against fuel taxes before growing into a broader rebellion against economic inequality and President Emmanuel Macron's government.

这场骚乱也唤起了人们对"黄马甲"运动的记忆——它始于 2018 年反对燃油税的抗议，后来演变成一场针对经济不平等和马克龙总统政府的更广泛反抗。

Behind these different grievances lay a deeper frustration: many French people felt squeezed by rising living costs, disappointed by public services and increasingly distrustful of political leaders.

在这些各自不同的不满背后，是更深的挫败感：许多法国人感到被上涨的生活成本挤压，对公共服务失望，并越来越不信任政治领导人。

Students, workers and Yellow Vest veterans saw an opportunity to unite their separate struggles.

学生、工人和黄马甲运动的老将们，看到了把各自斗争联合起来的机会。

Responding to a call to action that notably cited the rising cost of living, but also building on the momentum of the high school student protest movement that began in late September, Saturday's demonstrations aimed to create a "convergence of struggles" between various groups, ranging from "Yellow Vests" to high school students, several professional sectors, left-wing political leaders and trade unions.

响应一份特别提到生活成本上涨的行动号召，同时也借势 9 月下旬开始的高中生抗议运动，周六的示威力图在各类群体之间实现"斗争的汇合"——从"黄马甲"到高中生、若干行业、左翼政治领袖和工会。

At exactly 10 am, outside Gare Saint-Lazare train station, one Yellow Vest summed up his twofold ambition for the day: "First, the revolution, but also to sleep in Macron's bed." He said this with a mixture of humor and seriousness, going on to denounce capitalism, the bourgeoisie, the police and the cost of living.

上午 10 点整，在圣拉扎尔火车站外，一位黄马甲这样概括他当天的双重抱负："第一，革命；还有就是睡到马克龙的床上去。"他说这话时半开玩笑半认真，接着又痛斥资本主义、资产阶级、警察和生活成本。

Everyone in his entourage chimed in relentlessly, listing their demands, frustrations and anger, until their conversation was abruptly cut short when a BRAV-M motorcycle police unit suddenly barged in and descended on the group.

他身边的人都跟着不停附和，一一列举诉求、沮丧和愤怒，直到一支 BRAV-M 摩托车警察小队突然冲进来扑向这群人，谈话戛然而止。

Several small autonomous gatherings started forming in the morning. They aimed to head toward the Élysée Palace, the Place de la Concorde or the Opéra – basically, to push as far as possible.

上午开始形成了几支自发的小型集会。他们打算前往爱丽舍宫、协和广场或歌剧院——总之，能推多远就推多远。

There was no official procession, no leader, no truly defined objective, just packs of individuals on the move, light on their feet. "Strike, blockade, wild protest!" mask-clad people shouted as they sprinted toward Avenue de l'Opéra.

没有官方队伍，没有领袖，也没有真正明确的目标，只有一群群在移动的人，脚步轻快。"罢工、封锁、野蛮抗议！"戴面具的人一边喊着，一边朝歌剧院大道冲刺。

In Paris, the situation seemed to at least partially slip out of control from the early afternoon, while, at the same time, taking on several different forms.

在巴黎，从午后不久开始，局势似乎至少部分失控，同时又呈现出几种不同的形态。

Place de la République, the starting point for the official demonstration was packed to bursting.

官方示威的起点共和国广场，挤得水泄不通。

Some violent and organized protesters began by attacking street furniture, then tried to break into the Republican Guard barracks, set a car on fire and smashed things left, right and center.

一些有组织的暴力抗议者先是攻击街道设施，随后试图闯入共和国卫队军营，点燃一辆汽车，四处砸东西。

While it might not exactly have been the much-heralded "convergence of struggles," the march still brought together people from all walks of life.

虽然这未必就是那场被大肆宣扬的"斗争的汇合"，但这场游行确实汇聚了各行各业的人。

Lucie and Thomas, who both just graduated from the prestigious HEC business school, proclaimed their support for the movement: "We are aware that we are privileged students and we have a duty of solidarity." The same pattern also played out in several other cities.

刚刚从名校 HEC 商学院毕业的露西和托马斯公开表示支持这场运动："我们清楚自己是享有特权的学生，我们有团结的义务。"同样的模式也在其他几个城市上演。

On one side, large groups of demonstrators marched in dozens of peaceful processions from Rennes (Brittany) to Toulouse (south), despite the tensions. On the other, independent groups were determined to clash with the police.

一边是大批示威者组成数十支和平队伍，从雷恩（布列塔尼）一路行进到图卢兹（南部），尽管气氛紧张。另一边则是决意与警察冲突的独立团体。

In the streets, groups of university and high school students, toughened up by weeks of protest, mingled with small groups that organized to confront law enforcement units.

街头，经过数周抗议历练的大学和高中生群体，与那些组织起来对抗执法部门的小团体混在一起。

Protesters from working-class neighborhoods were there, as well as a good number of looters who could hardly believe their eyes: All the opulence of Paris's wealthy districts was suddenly within reach, whether by hand or by hammer.

来自工薪阶层社区的抗议者也在其中，还有不少几乎不敢相信自己眼睛的抢劫者：巴黎富裕街区的一切奢华，突然之间伸手可及——用手，或者用锤子。

"These neighborhoods are rich here. It's all from the profits made off the suburbs," shouted a very young man in the middle of a small group, as they made their way near Rue Lafayette.

"这一带才叫富。全是从郊区身上赚来的利润，"一个小团体中间一个很年轻的男人喊道，他们正朝拉法耶特街附近走去。

On several of Paris's major right bank streets, the same scenes, with the same violent acts, played out again and again. Paving stones were thrown. Cars, trucks and construction vehicles were set on fire.

在巴黎右岸的几条主要街道上，同样的场景、同样的暴力行为一再上演。铺路石被扔了出去。轿车、卡车和工程车被点燃。

Starting from Madeleine, Kenzo, Salomon and Arc'teryx boutiques had their windows smashed and shelves ransacked. The Céline store on Avenue Montaigne was pillaged.

从玛德莲教堂一带开始，Kenzo、Salomon 和 Arc'teryx 的精品店橱窗被砸、货架被洗劫。蒙田大道上的 Céline 门店被抢掠一空。

In nearby streets, one guy and his friends were asking, "Where is Chanel?" On Saturday evening, Lecornu announced that 1,200 people had been arrested and 150 police officers and gendarmes injured.

在附近的街上，一个男人和朋友们在问："香奈儿在哪？"周六晚，勒科尔尼宣布已有 1200 人被捕、150 名警察和宪兵受伤。

"We will all stand united against the sowers of chaos, who seek only to bring down our institutions and prevent those who peacefully express their demands from doing so," he said.

"我们将团结一致，对抗那些播撒混乱的人——他们只想搞垮我们的制度，阻止那些和平表达诉求的人发声，"他说。
