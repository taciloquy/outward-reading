Singapore goes after companies over toxic haze

新加坡追查涉事企业，剑指跨境“毒烟雾”

Singapore is trying to hold companies to account as it grapples with its worst air pollution in a decade, but the smog-choked city-state is running into the limits of prosecuting cross-border environmental laws.

新加坡正试图追究企业的责任，以应对十年来最严重的一次空气污染；但这座被烟雾笼罩的城邦国家，正在撞上跨境环境诉讼的边界。

The government has in recent days threatened legal action against 10 businesses, several with deep ties to Singapore, that it says are behind wildfires in neighbouring Indonesia that have produced the haze.

政府近几日威胁对 10 家企业采取法律行动，其中数家与新加坡关系深厚；政府称这些企业是邻国印度尼西亚野火的幕后推手，正是这些火情造成了烟雾。

It has also raised questions about whether other companies listed on the local bourse may be involved, and has threatened to target company profits, access to financing and standing with investors.

这也引发了疑问：本地交易所上市的其他公司是否也可能牵涉其中。政府还威胁要针对企业利润、融资渠道以及它们在投资者心中的地位下手。

"Singapore — which wants to continue being an international finance centre — gets a lot of business from these companies," said Ivan Png at the National University of Singapore, who has campaigned for anti-haze legislation.

"新加坡——它想继续做国际金融中心——从这些公司身上拿到很多生意，"新加坡国立大学的伊万·彭（Ivan Png）说。他一直在推动反烟雾立法。

He added that the companies "depend on Singapore for equity and financing — you would think that would make them want to pay attention to what the government is saying".

他补充说，这些公司"依赖新加坡提供股权和融资——你会以为这会让它们愿意重视政府说的话"。

The issue has also threatened a rift with Indonesia, where the fires are blamed on slash-and-burn practices by paper and palm oil producers.

这一问题也威胁到与印尼的关系——那里的火灾被归咎于纸浆和棕榈油生产商的刀耕火种式烧荒。

This year's fires have been exacerbated by a "super" El Niño, a natural weather phenomenon that has raised temperatures and reduced rainfall across Indonesia.

今年的火情因"超级"厄尔尼诺现象而加剧；这是一种自然天气现象，推高了整个印尼的气温、减少了降雨。

Singapore's air quality has been classified by the government as "very unhealthy" this month, at times ranking the worst in the world, according to monitoring platform IQAir.

据监测平台 IQAir 的数据，新加坡政府本月已将空气质量定为"非常不健康"，有时还排到全球最差。

Outdoor activities and sports events have been cancelled and hospital admissions for respiratory complaints have surged. The smog cast a pall over this month's Singapore Grand Prix.

户外活动和体育赛事被取消，因呼吸系统疾病住院的人数激增。烟雾也给本月的 F1 新加坡大奖赛蒙上阴影。

Public frustration is mounting with what has become a regular nuisance in the city-state that generally prides itself on effective governance.

在这座一向以治理高效自豪的城邦国家，这种已成常态的麻烦正让民众的不满不断累积。

Singapore has taken steps to try to bring companies suspected of field burning — which is also illegal in Indonesia — to book.

新加坡已采取措施，试图追究那些涉嫌田间焚烧的企业的责任——田间焚烧在印尼同样违法。

In 2014, it introduced a Transboundary Haze Pollution Act, which empowered the government to pursue criminal charges against companies found to have contributed to haze-producing fires and levy fines as high as S$100,000 (US$78,000) a day, capped at S$2mn (US$1.6mn) per conviction.

2014 年，新加坡出台《跨境烟雾污染法》，授权政府对企业提起刑事指控——只要认定其助长了产生烟雾的火情——并可处以每日最高 10 万新元（7.8 万美元）的罚款，单次定罪上限为 200 万新元（160 万美元）。

But initial attempts to enforce the law stalled, with the environment ministry complaining that its Indonesian counterpart did not provide the necessary information to pursue charges. No company has been fined under the law.

但最初的执法尝试停滞不前：环境部抱怨说，印尼对口部门没有提供起诉所需的信息。至今没有一家公司依据该法被罚款。

This year, Singapore has vowed to toughen its stance. In recent days, the government has served legal notices on 10 companies — identified using maps, meteorological data and satellite imagery — to demand they stop burning.

今年，新加坡誓言加严立场。近几日，政府向 10 家公司送达法律通知——这些公司是通过地图、气象数据和卫星图像锁定的——要求它们停止焚烧。

Three of the businesses were served similar notices in 2015.

其中三家企业在 2015 年就收到过类似通知。

"If companies refuse to co-operate, regardless of whether they have a presence in Singapore... we will use every legal and regulatory lever at our disposal to exert pressure where it matters." At least four of the 10 companies are suppliers to Asia Pulp and Paper, which is headquartered in Singapore and part of Indonesian conglomerate Sinar Mas.

"如果企业拒绝合作，无论它们在新加坡是否有实体存在……我们都将动用一切可用的法律和监管手段，在真正要紧的地方施加压力。"这 10 家公司中至少四家是亚洲纸浆纸业（APP）的供应商；该公司总部位于新加坡，隶属印尼金光集团（Sinar Mas）。

Two others are run by permanent Singapore residents. Shanmugam said the government would investigate APP and several other companies listed in Singapore, where many Indonesian conglomerates access global capital.

另外两家由新加坡永久居民经营。尚穆根（Shanmugam）表示，政府将调查 APP 以及在新加坡上市的其他几家公司——许多印尼企业集团正是通过这里获取全球资本。

"Some of the fires or smoke-source points seem to be associated with Singapore-listed First Resources and Bumitama Agri, as well as First Borneo Plantations," he said.

"一些火点或烟源点似乎与在新加坡上市的第一资源（First Resources）、布米塔玛农业（Bumitama Agri）以及 First Borneo Plantations 有关，"他说。

"We will be asking more questions of these companies." Shanmugam added that banks that lent to the businesses had also questioned them, and the Monetary Authority of Singapore, the city-state's financial regulator, would track lenders' risk exposure.

"我们会向这些公司提出更多问题。"尚穆根补充说，曾向这些企业放贷的银行也已向它们提出质询；作为城邦国家的金融监管机构，新加坡金融管理局将跟踪银行的风险敞口。

First Resources said it welcomed the investigation, adding it was "confident that an objective review of the available evidence will prove that it does not engage in any land clearing by fire, whether directly or indirectly".

第一资源表示欢迎此次调查，并称"相信对现有证据的客观审查将证明，本公司无论直接还是间接，都没有从事任何以火清地"。

APP, Bumitama Agri and First Borneo Plantations did not respond to requests for comment. SGX, the local bourse, has separately asked seven listed companies — including Golden-Agri Resources, Wilmar and Kencana Agri — about possible links to the haze.

APP、布米塔玛农业和 First Borneo Plantations 未回应置评请求。本地交易所新加坡交易所（SGX）已另行询问七家上市公司——包括金光农业资源（Golden-Agri Resources）、丰益国际（Wilmar）和 Kencana Agri——是否可能与烟雾有关。

Indonesia's police chief said this week that authorities had named 11 corporations, three of them foreign, as suspects in connection with the fires.

印尼警察总长本周表示，当局已将 11 家企业列为与火灾有关的嫌疑人，其中三家为外资企业。

"Some countries are mocking, insulting us... when many of their companies [operating] here are responsible," Indonesia's President Prabowo Subianto said in a speech on Thursday, adding: "We will announce those names when the time comes." He did not identify the countries.

"有些国家在嘲讽、侮辱我们……而它们许多在我们这里经营的公司才是责任方，"印尼总统普拉博沃·苏比安托周四在讲话中说，并补充："到时候我们会公布这些名字。"他没有点名是哪些国家。

Indonesia's government communication agency said: "Indonesia and Singapore maintain channels of engagement on environmental issues, including transboundary pollution." It added that it prioritises "the protection of affected communities, the containment of fires, and effective enforcement of environmental regulations".

印尼政府通信机构表示："印尼和新加坡在环境问题上保持着沟通渠道，包括跨境污染。"该机构还称，其优先事项是"保护受影响社区、控制火情，以及有效执行环境法规"。

NUS professor Png called for stronger laws to provide an incentive to whistleblowers, pointing to successful efforts in the US to combat bribery under the Foreign Corrupt Practices Act. "It is very hard to collect evidence," he said.

新加坡国立大学教授彭呼吁出台更有力的法律，为举报人提供激励，并援引美国依据《反海外腐败法》打击贿赂的成功经验。"收集证据非常困难，"他说。

"But when you offer incentives, whistleblowers will come out."

"但只要你提供激励，举报人就会站出来。"
