全球都在抢矿，中国却开始抢“垃圾”：一块废电池，为什么成了战略资源？

日期：2026-10-15 分类：英国《经济学人》外刊讲解 第1198天 阅读时长：约 18 分钟
正文 词汇 专有名词 练习
The Economist · 标题
China is turning trash into treasure on a vast scale — EV batteries, especially, will help it secure supplies of critical minerals
中国正以惊人的规模，把垃圾变成宝物——尤其是电动车电池，它将帮助中国把关键矿产的供应牢牢锁定在自己手里。
trash /træʃ/ n.
英文释义：Trash means things that are no longer wanted or useful, especially household waste.
中文释义：垃圾，破烂
文中意：标题用 trash 不用 waste，要的是“破烂”的粗粝感——变废为宝说的就是最不起眼的东西也能翻身。
例句一：Don't just throw it away as trash. 别把它当垃圾扔了。
例句二：One man's trash is another man's treasure. 一个人的垃圾，是另一个人的宝贝。
treasure /ˈtreʒə(r)/ n./v.
英文释义：Treasure means a collection of valuable things such as gold or jewels; as a verb, to value something highly.
中文释义：宝物；珍视
文中意：与 trash 押头韵，凑成“变废为宝”的英文对句，暗指废电池就是埋在城里的宝藏。
例句一：We treasure these memories. 我们珍视这些记忆。
例句二：The city is digging treasure out of its own rubbish. 这座城市正在从自己的垃圾里挖宝。
vast /vɑːst/ adj.
英文释义：Vast means extremely large in size, amount, or degree.
中文释义：巨大的，广袤的
文中意：on a vast scale＝大规模地，作者借它写出国家级体量，而不是几家工厂的生意。
例句一：China is turning trash into treasure on a vast scale. 中国正在大规模地变废为宝。
例句二：The scheme operates on a vast scale. 这个计划规模空前。
critical /ˈkrɪtɪkl/ adj.
英文释义：Critical means extremely important, or at a point of crisis; it can also mean judging or analytical.
中文释义：关键的；危急的；批判性的
文中意：critical minerals＝关键矿产，标题用它点出废电池里那点金属何以成了战略资源。
例句一：Lithium and cobalt are critical minerals. 锂和钴是关键矿产。
mineral /ˈmɪnərəl/ n.
英文释义：A mineral is a natural substance in the earth, such as coal, salt, or metal ore.
中文释义：矿物，矿产
文中意：与第六段的 ore 是“成品对原料”：中国精炼 minerals，却进口 ores，这一对词是全文暗线。
例句一：The country lacks mineral resources. 该国矿产资源匮乏。
Paragraph 1
A fast-growing number of EVs will soon be scrapped and replaced. Shredding their batteries yields “black mass”, a powder from which valuable metals such as lithium, cobalt and nickel can be recovered and reused. From next month the EU will make it harder to export this waste. The aim is to encourage more processing of black mass close to home (at the moment most is shipped to Asia). In August America, which has banned Chinese EVs but still imports lots of Chinese batteries, blocked all black-mass exports.
越来越多的电动车，很快就会报废、被换掉。把这些车的电池撕碎，就能产出“黑粉”——一种粉末，从中可以回收并再利用锂、钴、镍这些贵重的金属。从下个月起，欧盟会让这种废物的出口变得更难，目的是鼓励更多黑粉在本地加工（而目前，绝大部分都被运去了亚洲）。8月，美国封锁了所有黑粉的出口——有意思的是，这个国家禁掉了中国的电动车，却还在大量进口中国的电池。
scrap /skræp/ v./n.
英文释义：To scrap something means to get rid of it because it is no longer useful; as a noun, it is waste material, especially metal, that can be reused.
中文释义：报废，废弃；废料
文中意：第一段作动词“报废”，第九段作名词“废铜废铝”，一个词两副面孔。
例句一：A growing number of EVs will soon be scrapped. 越来越多的电动车很快就要报废。
例句二：The plant imports scrap copper and aluminium. 这家工厂进口废铜和废铝。
yield /jiːld/ v./n.
英文释义：If something yields a result or a substance, it produces it; to yield can also mean to give way to pressure.
中文释义：产出，出产；屈服
文中意：宾语决定词义：yield black mass 是“产出”，作者借此把处理垃圾悄悄写成了开采矿山。
例句一：Shredding their batteries yields “black mass”. 粉碎这些电池会产出“黑粉”。
例句二：The mine yields 10 tonnes of copper a day. 这座矿每天产出10吨铜。
black mass /ˌblæk ˈmæs/ n.
英文释义：Black mass is the powder left after lithium-ion batteries are shredded, containing valuable metals such as lithium, cobalt and nickel.
中文释义：黑粉（电池粉碎后的黑色粉末）
文中意：全文主角：电池撕碎后的黑粉，富含锂钴镍，等于“城市里的矿”。
例句一：Black mass might make up 20-30% of global demand for battery metals by 2050. 到2050年，黑粉可能占全球电池金属需求的20%至30%。
ban / block /bæn/ /blɒk/ v.
英文释义：To ban means to officially forbid something; to block means to stop something from moving or happening.
中文释义：禁止；封锁，拦下
文中意：ban 是立法层面的“禁”，block 是动作层面的“拦”：禁了中国的车，却照样买中国的电池。
例句一：America has banned Chinese EVs but still imports lots of Chinese batteries. 美国禁了中国的电动车，却仍在大量进口中国的电池。
Paragraph 2
China, however, is unlikely to suffer. Black mass might make up 20-30% of global demand for battery metals by 2050, says the International Energy Agency, a forecaster. So China is supercharging its recycling industries: not just to shore up supplies of battery metals, but also rare-earths and much else. The goal is to make its economy less “linear” (where raw materials are made into products which are then thrown away) and more “circular” (closing the loop so recycled waste replaces raw materials).
不过，中国不太可能吃亏。国际能源署——一家预测机构——认为，到2050年，黑粉可能占到全球电池金属需求的二到三成。所以，中国正在给自己的回收产业猛踩油门：目标不只是稳住电池金属的供应，还有稀土，以及其他很多东西。它的目标，是让本国经济少一点“线性”（原材料被制成产品，随后被丢弃），多一点“循环”（闭合回路，让回收来的废物顶替原材料）。
supercharge /ˈsuːpətʃɑːdʒ/ v.
英文释义：To supercharge something means to give it much more power or energy than usual.
中文释义：给……增压；使猛增
文中意：不是“发展”，是“踩油门”，一个动词把国家意志的力度写了出来。
例句一：China is supercharging its recycling industries. 中国正在给它的回收产业猛踩油门。
shore up /ʃɔː(r)/ phr. v.
英文释义：To shore up something means to support or strengthen it, especially when it is weak.
中文释义：支撑，巩固
文中意：shore up supplies＝把电池金属的供应“顶住”，暗示这份供应本来有垮塌风险。
例句一：The move aims to shore up supplies of battery metals. 此举意在巩固电池金属的供应。
rare earth /ˌreə ˈɜːθ/ n.
英文释义：Rare earths are a group of 17 chemical elements used in magnets, batteries and electronics.
中文释义：稀土
文中意：稀土其实不“稀”，难的是把 17 个化学性质相近的元素分开，中国的优势正在分离环节。
例句一：China supercharges recycling of rare earths too. 中国也在加码稀土回收。
linear / circular /ˈlɪniə(r)/ /ˈsɜːkjələ(r)/ adj.
英文释义：A linear economy takes raw materials, makes products, and throws them away; a circular one closes the loop so recycled waste replaces raw materials.
中文释义：线性的；循环的
文中意：全文骨架：linear 是拿料—造—扔，circular 是让回收料顶替原料。
例句一：The goal is to make its economy less linear and more circular. 目标是让经济更少“线性”、更多“循环”。
replace A with B /rɪˈpleɪs/ phr.
英文释义：To replace A with B means to put B in the place of A; to substitute A for B means to use A in place of B.
中文释义：用 B 替换 A
文中意：closing the loop so recycled waste replaces raw materials——用再生废料顶替原材料，正是“循环”的定义。
例句一：Recycled waste replaces raw materials. 再生废料顶替了原材料。
Paragraph 3
Once recycling efforts were driven by concerns about pollution. Now, amid a global scramble for the materials needed to build a high-tech economy, security has become a primary motive.
曾经，回收这件事是由“对污染的担忧”推动的。而现在，在一场为打造高科技经济所需的原材料而展开的全球争夺中，安全已经成为首要的动机。
drive /draɪv/ v.
英文释义：To drive something means to cause it to happen or to force it in a particular direction.
中文释义：驱动，促使；驱使
文中意：efforts were driven by concerns——被动语态暗示回收是被外部推着走的。
例句一：Once recycling efforts were driven by concerns about pollution. 过去，回收的努力是由对污染的担忧推动的。
scramble /ˈskræmbl/ n./v.
英文释义：A scramble is a hurried and competitive struggle to get something; to scramble means to climb or move quickly using hands and feet.
中文释义：争抢，抢夺
文中意：不用 competition 而用 scramble，作者要说的是：这不是比赛，是抢。
例句一：There is a global scramble for these materials. 全世界都在抢这些材料。
primary /ˈpraɪməri/ adj.
英文释义：Primary means main, most important, or happening first.
中文释义：首要的，主要的
文中意：a primary motive＝首要动机，比 main 更有书面语的正式感。
例句一：Security has become a primary motive. 安全已成为首要动机。
motive /ˈməʊtɪv/ n.
英文释义：A motive is a reason for doing something, especially one that is hidden.
中文释义：动机
文中意：用 motive 不用 reason，暗含“真正推动事情的那只手未必明说”。
例句一：Security has become a primary motive. 安全已成为首要动机。
Paragraph 4
In the 1980s China started to import waste, taking the hardware, paper and plastic that richer countries did not want. A sprawling informal recycling-sector emerged, led by scavengers picking through rubbish piles. Much ended up in landfills or burnt in the open air, leaching toxic chemicals and creating poisonous smog.
20 世纪80 年代，中国开始进口废物，接收那些富裕国家不要的金属器件、纸张和塑料。一个杂乱蔓延的非正规回收行业应运而生，主角是在垃圾堆里翻捡的拾荒者。这些废物中的大部分，最终进了填埋场，或者索性被露天焚烧，渗出有毒的化学物质，制造出有毒的烟雾。
hardware /ˈhɑːdweə(r)/ n.
英文释义：Hardware means tools and equipment made of metal; in computing, it means the physical parts of a computer.
中文释义：五金器具；（电脑）硬件
文中意：熟词僻义：这里指一船一船的金属器件，不是电脑硬件。
例句一：They took the hardware, paper and plastic that richer countries did not want. 他们把富国不要的五金件、纸张和塑料拉了回来。
sprawling /ˈsprɔːlɪŋ/ adj.
英文释义：Sprawling means spreading out over a large area in an untidy or irregular way.
中文释义：杂乱蔓延的，无序扩张的
文中意：不是普通的“大”，是“大得没规矩、东一块西一块”。
例句一：A sprawling informal recycling sector emerged. 一个杂乱蔓延的非正规回收业冒了出来。
scavenger /ˈskævɪndʒə(r)/ n.
英文释义：A scavenger is a person or animal that collects things that have been discarded, or feeds on dead animals.
中文释义：拾荒者；食腐动物
文中意：不用 worker、不用 recycler，一个词写尽这条链上“我们翻剩下的”位置。
例句一：… led by scavengers picking through rubbish piles. ……带头的是在垃圾堆里翻找的拾荒者。
landfill /ˈlændfɪl/ n.
英文释义：A landfill is a large place where waste is buried under the ground.
中文释义：垃圾填埋场
文中意：全文“三种去向”之一：填埋、焚烧、回收，后面每段都会回指一次。
例句一：Much of it ended up in landfills. 其中大部分进了填埋场。
leach /liːtʃ/ v.
英文释义：If a liquid leaches, it slowly passes through soil or waste, carrying chemicals with it.
中文释义：沥滤，渗滤
文中意：现在分词作结果状语：一埋一烧，结果就是渗毒、冒烟。
例句一：… leaching toxic chemicals and creating poisonous smog. ……沥滤出有毒化学品，制造出有毒的烟雾。
smog /smɒɡ/ n.
英文释义：Smog is a form of air pollution that looks like thick smoke mixed with fog.
中文释义：雾霾，烟雾
文中意：smoke+fog 的混成词，把八十年代遮天蔽日的画面直接压在读者眼前。
例句一：Rubbish burnt in the open air created poisonous smog. 露天焚烧垃圾制造出有毒的烟雾。
Paragraph 5
China is still well behind squeaky-clean European countries when it comes to waste management (though it is hard to say how far, as it does not publish detailed national data for recycling). Its poorer rural areas are still often strewn with rubbish. But officials have made China’s big cities, at least, cleaner and more pleasant to live in. Now their focus is shifting to the use of recycling for the sake of security. In July China released a plan for the development of the circular economy until 2030. “The importance of resource security has become increasingly prominent, while geopolitical dynamics…have grown complex and severe,” the plan noted.
在废物管理这件事上，中国仍然远远落后于那些一尘不染的欧洲国家（不过到底落后多少，很难说清，因为中国并不公布关于回收的详细全国数据）。它较为贫困的农村地区，至今仍常常垃圾遍地。但至少，官员们让中国的大城市变得更干净、也更宜居了。而现在，他们的重心正在转移——为了安全而利用回收。今年 7 月，中国发布了一份面向2030 年的循环经济发展规划。该规划指出：“资源安全的重要性日益凸显，而地缘政治的……已经变得复杂而严峻。”
well behind /wel bɪˈhaɪnd/ phr.
英文释义：Well behind means much further back than something — here “well” is a degree adverb meaning much.
中文释义：远远落后于
文中意：well 这里是程度副词＝much，不是“好”；同一串还有 well ahead、well above。
例句一：China is still well behind European countries. 中国仍然远远落后于欧洲国家。
squeaky-clean /ˌskwiːki ˈkliːn/ adj.
英文释义：Squeaky-clean means very clean; it can also mean having a spotless reputation.
中文释义：干净得发亮的；名声清白的
文中意：与 well behind 形成反差：中国远远落后于“擦得吱吱响”的欧洲。
例句一：… behind squeaky-clean European countries. ……落后于干净得吱吱响的欧洲国家。
strew / strewn with /struː/ v./adj.
英文释义：To strew means to scatter things over a surface; strewn with means covered with things scattered around.
中文释义：散落，撒满
文中意：作者写的不是“脏”，是“乱”——东西东一堆西一摊地撒在那里。
例句一：Rural areas are still strewn with rubbish. 农村地区仍然散落着垃圾。

中文释义：为了……的缘故
文中意：for the sake of security——回收的目的被改写成“安全”，这是全文的转折。

prominent /ˈprɒmɪnənt/ adj.

Paragraph 6
That judgment rests on two considerations. First, China wants to dominate high-tech manufacturing in everything from robotics to wind turbines. And second, to achieve this, it must secure supplies of raw materials, connect more power lines and build more data-centres. Massive amounts of aluminium, copper, battery metals and rare earths are needed. While China controls the bulk of the global refining capacity for these materials, it relies on imports for most of the ores. Meanwhile many Western countries want to reduce reliance on China’s factories. Officials worry they might try to squeeze its supplies. Last year a researcher at a state-owned research facility said that without recycling China would “keep facing strangleholds in the future”.
这个判断建立在两个考量之上。第一，中国想要主导高科技制造业——从机器人技术到风力发电机，无所不包。第二，为了实现这一点，它必须确保原材料的供应、接入更多电力线路、并建造更多数据中心；而这需要大量的铝、铜、电池金属和稀土。中国虽然掌控着这些材料全球精炼产能的大部分，但它的矿石却大部分依赖进口。与此同时，许多西方国家想要减少对中国工厂的依赖。官员们担心，他们可能会设法挤压中国的供应。去年，一家国有研究机构的一位研究者说，如果没有回收，中国未来将“持续被卡脖子”。

Paragraph 7
That China already makes vast quantities of these goods should make recycling simpler to do. Factories can tweak designs so that products are easier to dismantle later. And many battery-producing techniques (such as using acids and heat to isolate valuable metals) are also used in recycling them. Take CATL, for example, the world’s largest battery-maker. Most of the Chinese firm’s sales are domestic, and will end up being processed by a CATL subsidiary called Brunp, which is the world’s biggest battery-recycler. Such arrangements have helped China build 85% of global battery-recycling capacity.
中国已经在大量生产这类产品——这一点，本应让回收变得更容易操作。工厂可以对设计稍作调整，好让产品在日后更容易拆解。而且，许多制造电池的技术（比如用酸和高温把有价值的金属分离出来），同样被用于回收电池。以宁德时代为例——它是全世界最大的电池制造商。这家中国公司的销售大部分在国内，而这些电池最终会被一家名叫邦普的宁德时代子公司处理，邦普是全球最大的电池回收商。正是这样的安排，帮助中国建起了全球85% 的电池回收产能。
