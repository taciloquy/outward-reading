The New York Times · 标题
El Niño Threatens Global Sugar Supply — A poor sugarcane crop in India, the world's second-largest producer, is hurting consumers and farmers — plus undermining the country's energy security goals.
厄尔尼诺发威，全球糖供应告急——印度甘蔗歉收，正在让农民和消费者一起受伤，还把这个国家的能源安全目标，从底下一点点掏空。
threaten /ˈθretn/ v.
英文释义：threaten (v.) means to be likely to cause harm or damage to something
中文释义：大祸临头但尚未落地的悬置感
文中意：糖还没断供，刀已经举起来了——作者不用 hurt 或 damage，因为它们表示“已经发生”，threaten 才是“即将发生”。
例句一：The drought threatens the harvest of more than two million farmers. 这场干旱威胁着两百多万农民的收成。
例句二：Warming seas threaten to push the fish stocks past the point of recovery. 海水变暖，可能会把鱼类资源推向无法恢复的临界点。
supply /səˈplaɪ/ n.
英文释义：supply (n.) means an amount of something that is available to be used
中文释义：全球糖的总盘子
文中意：标题用 Global Sugar Supply，把一场天气事件直接接到了全世界的糖罐子上——五个词就交代了完整的因果链。
例句一：Global oil supply remains tight despite record output in the United States. 尽管美国产量创纪录，全球石油供应依然吃紧。
例句二：Fresh vegetables were in short supply after the floods. 洪水过后，新鲜蔬菜供应短缺。
poor /pɔː(r)/ adj.
英文释义：poor (adj.) means smaller in amount, or lower in quality, than is normal or expected
中文释义：歉收的
文中意：a poor sugarcane crop 不是“一株穷甘蔗”，是“一场收成不好的甘蔗”；外刊经济类文章中 poor harvest 是固定高频搭配——典型熟词僻义。
例句一：Poor harvests across the region have pushed bread prices to a record high. 整个地区歉收，把面包价格推到了历史新高。
例句二：Investors were rattled by the company's poor results in the first quarter. 公司第一季度糟糕的业绩让投资者慌了神。
undermine /ˌʌndəˈmaɪn/ v.
英文释义：undermine (v.) means to make something weaker at its most basic level, usually in a way that is not immediately noticeable
中文释义：悄悄掏空
文中意：糖价一涨，印度能源安全目标的底座被一点点挖掉；作者不用 destroy——那是一下子炸掉，undermine 是你看不见的慢动作。
例句一：Constant leaks undermined public confidence in the government's handling of the crisis. 不断出现的泄密，悄悄掏空了公众对政府处理这场危机的信任。
例句二：Years of underfunding had undermined the foundations of the research system. 长年经费不足，已经把这套研究体系的根基掏空了。
Paragraph 1
Conflict. Climate change. A withering El Niño. All are conspiring to spoil the world's sweet tooth. Global sugar prices have soared, as a hot, dry summer scorched much of the sugar-producing parts of the world, nudging overall food prices upward. Nowhere is that felt as acutely as it is in India, the biggest sugar consumer in the world and the second-biggest producer. The worst monsoon in 11 years and fears of a poor sugarcane harvest have spiked prices amid the Hindu festival season, when gods and mortals alike are feted with sweets.
冲突。气候变化。一场正在让万物枯萎的厄尔尼诺。这三样东西像是商量好了似的，一起来毁掉全世界那份嗜甜的快乐。全球糖价一路飙升——一个又热又干的夏天，把世界上大片产糖区烤得焦头烂额，顺手把整体食品价格也往上顶了一顶。而这份痛，没有哪个地方感受得比印度更深切：它是全球最大的食糖消费国，也是第二大生产国。十一年来最糟糕的一场季风，加上对甘蔗歉收的恐惧，把糖价猛地戳了上去——偏偏这一切，就发生在印度教的节日季里；在这个时节，神明和凡人，都要用甜食供着。

例句一：Farmers across the country wait for the monsoon before planting. 全国农民都等季风到了才下种。
例句二：A weak monsoon means a poor harvest for millions of small farmers. 季风一弱，就意味着数百万小农要歉收。

例句一：In the old stories, gods and mortals shared the same table. 在古老的故事里，神明和凡人同桌共食。

文中意：gods and mortals alike are feted with sweets——它是被动态，主语是神和凡人，动作是“被甜食招待”；外刊里这个词常用被动式。

例句二：Visitors are feted with tea and homemade cakes in every village. 每座村子里，来客都会用茶和自制糕点招待。

Making matters worse, high sugar prices are also complicating India's energy security goals. Sugarcane is a key crop for the country's biofuels push, all the more so as India seeks to curb oil imports while the waterways of the Middle East are choked by war. This year's El Niño has left many of the country's sugarcane fields parched, just a year after they were drenched by excess rains and devoured by pests.
更糟的是，高糖价还在把印度的能源安全目标搅成一团乱麻。甘蔗正是这个国家生物燃料计划的关键作物——这一点尤其要命，因为印度正想方设法压住石油进口，偏偏中东的航道又被战争掐住了。今年的厄尔尼诺，把印度大片甘蔗地晒得干裂；而就在一年前，这些地才刚被过多的雨水泡透、被害虫啃光。

Paragraph 3
Protests have broken out over rising prices. The government has warned sugar traders against hoarding. And a country that usually exports sugar has taken the extraordinary step of opening the doors to imports this year, further raising global sugar prices. "This crop is gone," Morarji Patil, a 30-year-old farmer, said as he stood beside his cane field one sweaty September morning in the Marathwada region of western India. Like other farmers here, Mr. Patil was already crushed by debt and high fertilizer prices before the blistering heat and scant rains. His cane stalks were thin and short, stunted by a lack of water this summer when they needed it most. Mr. Patil expected his yields to be 70 percent lower than normal.
抗议因涨价而爆发了。政府已经警告糖商不许囤积。而一个平时出口糖的国家，今年破天荒地迈出一步——向进口打开了大门，这反过来又把全球糖价往上推了一把。“这批庄稼完了。”三十岁的农民莫拉尔吉·帕蒂尔说。那是一个出汗的九月早晨，他站在印度西部马拉特瓦达地区自家的甘蔗地边。跟这里其他农民一样，在酷热和少雨到来之前，他就已经被债务和高昂的化肥价格压垮了。他的甘蔗秆又细又矮——在最需要水的那个夏天，水没来。帕蒂尔预计，今年的收成会比正常年份低七成。

Paragraph 4
India represents an object lesson on the far-reaching effects of conflict and extreme weather on a nation's economy. Food prices are rising month by month. Key crops remain at risk as the long tail of El Niño drags out through next year, heightening the likelihood of extreme drought and flood. On top of that, India is extremely vulnerable to global oil supply shocks. It imports the vast majority of its crude oil. It's not just India.
