China is turning trash into treasure on a vast scale

中国正在把垃圾变成宝藏，规模惊人

A fast-growing number of EVs will soon be scrapped and replaced. Shredding their batteries yields "black mass", a powder from which valuable metals such as lithium, cobalt and nickel can be recovered and reused.

快速增长的大量电动汽车很快就要报废更新。把它们的电池粉碎后得到"黑粉"——一种可以从中回收并再利用锂、钴、镍等贵重金属的粉末。

From next month the EU will make it harder to export this waste. The aim is to encourage more processing of black mass close to home (at the moment most is shipped to Asia).

从下个月起，欧盟将让这类废料的出口变得更难。目的是鼓励在本土就近加工更多"黑粉"（目前大部分被运往亚洲）。

In August America, which has banned Chinese EVs but still imports lots of Chinese batteries, blocked all black-mass exports. China, however, is unlikely to suffer.

今年 8 月，美国——它禁了中国电动车，却仍大量进口中国电池——封锁了所有"黑粉"出口。但中国不太可能因此受损。

Black mass might make up 20-30% of global demand for battery metals by 2050, says the International Energy Agency, a forecaster.

据预测机构国际能源署（IEA）估计，到 2050 年，"黑粉"可能占到全球电池金属需求的 20% 至 30%。

So China is supercharging its recycling industries: not just to shore up supplies of battery metals, but also rare earths and much else.

所以中国正在给自己的回收产业"开足马力"：不仅是为了稳住电池金属的供应，也包括稀土和许多其他材料。

The goal is to make its economy less "linear" (where raw materials are made into products which are then thrown away) and more "circular" (closing the loop so recycled waste replaces raw materials).

目标是让经济少一点"线性"（即原材料被制成产品、然后被丢弃），多一点"循环"（即闭合环路，用回收废料替代原材料）。

Once recycling efforts were driven by concerns about pollution. Now, amid a global scramble for the materials needed to build a high-tech economy, security has become a primary motive.

曾几何时，回收的动力来自对污染的担忧。如今，在全球争抢建设高科技经济所需材料的大背景下，"安全"成了首要动机。

In the 1980s China started to import waste, taking the hardware, paper and plastic that richer countries did not want. A sprawling informal recycling-sector emerged, led by scavengers picking through rubbish piles.

1980 年代，中国开始进口废料，接手富裕国家不想要的五金、纸张和塑料。一个庞杂的非正规回收行业随之兴起，领头的是在垃圾堆里翻捡的拾荒者。

Much ended up in landfills or burnt in the open air, leaching toxic chemicals and creating poisonous smog.

其中很多最终进了填埋场，或被露天焚烧，沥出有毒化学物质，制造出有毒烟雾。

China is still well behind squeaky-clean European countries when it comes to waste management (though it is hard to say how far, as it does not publish detailed national data for recycling).

在废弃物管理上，中国仍远远落后于那些一尘不染的欧洲国家（不过很难说到底差多远，因为中国不公布详细的全国回收数据）。

Its poorer rural areas are still often strewn with rubbish. But officials have made China's big cities, at least, cleaner and more pleasant to live in.

它较贫困的农村地区依然常常垃圾遍地。但至少，官员们已让中国的大城市变得更干净、更适合居住。

Now their focus is shifting to the use of recycling for the sake of security. In July China released a plan for the development of the circular economy until 2030.

如今他们的重心转向：为了安全而利用回收。7 月，中国发布了到 2030 年的循环经济发展规划。

"The importance of resource security has become increasingly prominent, while geopolitical dynamics...have grown complex and severe," the plan noted. First, China wants to dominate high-tech manufacturing in everything from robotics to wind turbines.

规划指出："资源安全的重要性日益凸显，而地缘政治动向……变得复杂而严峻。"其一，中国想在从机器人到风力涡轮机的一切领域主导高科技制造。

And second, to achieve this, it must secure supplies of raw materials, connect more power lines and build more data-centres. Massive amounts of aluminium, copper, battery metals and rare earths are needed.

其二，要做到这一点，它必须保障原材料供应、架设更多输电线路、建设更多数据中心。这需要大量的铝、铜、电池金属和稀土。

While China controls the bulk of the global refining capacity for these materials, it relies on imports for most of the ores. Meanwhile many Western countries want to reduce reliance on China's factories.

虽然中国掌握着这些材料的全球精炼产能的大头，但它所需的矿石大部分依赖进口。与此同时，许多西方国家希望减少对"中国工厂"的依赖。

Officials worry they might try to squeeze its supplies. Last year a researcher at a state-owned research facility said that without recycling China would "keep facing strangleholds in the future".

官员们担心它们可能试图卡住中国的供应。去年，一家国有研究机构的研究人员说，如果不搞回收，中国将"在未来持续面临被扼住咽喉的局面"。

That China already makes vast quantities of these goods should make recycling simpler to do. Factories can tweak designs so that products are easier to dismantle later.

中国本就在大量生产这些产品，这应该让回收变得更容易做。工厂可以微调设计，让产品日后更容易拆解。

And many battery-producing techniques (such as using acids and heat to isolate valuable metals) are also used in recycling them. Take CATL, for example, the world's largest battery-maker.

而且许多电池制造工艺（比如用酸和热来分离贵重金属）也被用在回收环节。以全球最大电池制造商宁德时代（CATL）为例。

Most of the Chinese firm's sales are domestic, and will end up being processed by a CATL subsidiary called Brunp, which is the world's biggest battery-recycler. Such arrangements have helped China build 85% of global battery recycling capacity.

这家中国企业的销售大部分在国内，最终会由宁德时代旗下名为邦普（Brunp）的子公司来处理——后者是全球最大的电池回收商。这样的安排帮助中国建成了全球 85% 的电池回收产能。

China is trying to make the recycling of rare earths (which it claims now supplies 10% of them) more efficient. At present it is far more costly than extracting them from raw materials.

中国正努力提高稀土回收（它称目前已供应其中 10%）的效率。眼下，回收的成本远高于从原材料中提取。

Still, Chinese rare-earth companies, which are almost all state-owned, "do not rely on economic viability in all cases", notes Corby Anderson.

不过，科尔比·安德森指出，中国的稀土企业几乎全是国企，它们"并非在所有情况下都依赖经济可行性"。

And to make sure its recycling plants are fed, China is also quietly reassuming its old role in the waste trade. It is selectively rolling back import restrictions on some foreign waste.

而为了确保回收工厂有料可吃，中国也在悄悄重拾它在废料贸易中的旧角色。它有选择地放宽了对某些外国废料的进口限制。

In 2024 it eased curbs on imports of some scrap copper and aluminum. Last year it started allowing imports of black mass. In January it slashed import tariffs on the stuff from 6.5% to 3%.

2024 年，它放宽了对部分废铜、废铝进口的限制。去年，它开始允许进口"黑粉"。今年 1 月，它把这类东西的进口关税从 6.5% 砍到 3%。

Officials have pledged to continue to increase imports of "high-quality" recycled materials over the next five years. Still, now that America and Europe want to keep their valuable waste, could they eat into China's recycling dominance?

官员们已承诺，未来五年将继续增加"高质量"回收材料的进口。然而，既然美国和欧洲想留住自己的宝贵废料，它们能蚕食中国在回收上的主导地位吗？

Zhang Yuping, the deputy general manager of GEM, a big Shenzhen-listed battery-recycler, thinks not. "From the perspective of national security, they might have some justification," he says.

深圳上市的大型电池回收商格林美（GEM）副总经理张玉平认为不能。"从国家安全的角度看，他们或许有些道理，"他说。

But the cost of recycling outside China would be high and scaling up difficult. Europe, at least, can't digest such a challenge, reckons Mr Zhang. "They would pay a heavy cost. " ■

但在中国境外做回收成本会很高，扩大规模也难。张玉平认为，至少欧洲"消化不了"这样的挑战。"他们会付出沉重的代价。" ■
