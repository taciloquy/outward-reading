Don't expect supersize profits from Anthropic soon

Anthropic准备上市：一家AI公司为什么可能创造史上最大IPO之一？

Breakneck growth is commonplace in Silicon Valley. The rise of Anthropic is something else. In the first quarter of last year—when Claude Code, its hit programming tool, was first released—the artificial-intelligence lab pulled in $376m of revenue.

在硅谷，爆发式增长是常态。但 Anthropic 的崛起非同寻常。去年第一季度——其爆款编程工具 Claude Code 首次发布之时——这家人工智能实验室创下了 3.76 亿美元营收。

By the second quarter of this year that figure had risen 30-fold, to $11.6bn, exceeding corporate mainstays such as McDonald's.

到今年第二季度，这一数字增长了三十倍，达到 116 亿美元，超过了麦当劳这样的老牌企业。

In a few weeks this darling of the AI revolution will undergo an initial public offering (IPO) that will be, if not the largest of all time, then certainly close to it.

几周之后，这家 AI 革命的宠儿将进行首次公开募股（IPO），即便不是史上最大，也必定与之接近。

On October 26th it publicly filed supporting paperwork that gave the outside world a closer look at its finances. Anthropic's growth is all the more remarkable because, unlike many zippy startups, it has already reached profitability.

10 月 26 日，它公开提交了支撑性文件，让外界得以更近距离审视其财务状况。Anthropic 的增长格外引人注目，因为与许多活跃的初创公司不同，它已经实现了盈利。

In the second quarter it eked out an operating profit of just under $1bn, having lost $1.7bn in the preceding three months.

第二季度，它勉强实现了略低于 10 亿美元的营业利润，而此前三个月还亏损了 17 亿美元。

The company is particularly eager to draw attention to its spending on computing power, which fell from 251% of sales in the first quarter of 2025 to just 48% in the second quarter of 2026.

公司特别急于让人关注它在算力上的支出——这部分支出占销售额的比例，已从 2025 年第一季度的 251% 骤降至 2026 年第二季度的 48%。

Anthropic claims that improvements in its AI models have made it faster and more efficient to develop and serve its technology, allowing the company to make the most of its limited resources.

Anthropic 声称，其 AI 模型的改进让技术研发与服务变得更快速、更高效，使公司得以充分利用有限的资源。

But can Anthropic's stunning performance continue?

但 Anthropic 惊人的表现能否持续？

A closer look at its filing suggests caution is warranted: growth is slowing; the company is paying higher prices to access scarce computing power; and a resulting shift towards directly purchasing AI chips will guzzle up much of the cashflow Anthropic would otherwise be on course to generate.

细看其申报文件便会发现，谨慎是必要的：增长正在放缓；公司为获取稀缺算力支付着更高的价格；由此转向直接采购 AI 芯片，将吞噬掉 Anthropic 原本有望产生的大部分现金流。

Earlier investors who had hoped for a public valuation as high as $2trn may thus be disappointed. Anthropic's scorching revenue growth has put a target on its back.

因此，早前那些期望公开估值高达 2 万亿美元的投资者可能会感到失望。Anthropic 迅猛的营收增长，已经让它成了众矢之的。

Claude Code made it an early leader in the race to automate programming. Tech giants such as Meta and Microsoft became huge customers, spending billions of dollars on Anthropic's technology.

Claude Code 让它早早成为编程自动化竞赛的领跑者。Meta 和微软等科技巨头成了它的大客户，在 Anthropic 的技术上投入了数十亿美元。

More recently those customers have been pushing staff to use internally developed alternatives.

但最近，这些客户一直在推动员工使用内部开发的替代产品。

At the same time, OpenAI, Anthropic's arch-rival, has been gaining ground with its own offering, Codex, the daily usage of which has risen ten fold since early August.

与此同时，Anthropic 的主要竞争对手 OpenAI 凭借自家产品 Codex 抢占阵地，其日活用量自 8 月初以来增长了十倍。

And after the short-lived craze for "tokenmaxxing" earlier this year, companies of all stripes have been more closely monitoring their spending on AI and imposing limits on their engineers.

而在今年早些时候"极致刷 token"的短暂狂热过后，各行各业的公司都更密切地监控着自己在 AI 上的开支，并对工程师设定了限制。

This month Anthropic told shareholders during a private gathering that revenue in the third quarter was up by about 45% from the preceding three months, a third of the rate it achieved in the second quarter.

本月，Anthropic 在一次私下聚会上告诉股东，第三季度营收较前三个月增长约 45%，仅为第二季度增速的三分之一。

In its IPO prospectus Anthropic warns that, as the market matures, growth could slow "more rapidly than we anticipate". Moreover, it has become increasingly reliant on the tech giants to push its products.

在 IPO 招股书中，Anthropic 警告称，随着市场成熟，增长可能"比我们预期的更快"放缓。此外，它对科技巨头推广其产品的依赖也日益加深。

Almost half of Anthropic's revenue in the first six months of this year came from sales made through the cloud businesses of Alphabet, Amazon and Microsoft.

今年上半年，Anthropic 近一半的营收来自通过 Alphabet、亚马逊和微软的云业务达成的销售。

Anthropic's spending on sales, marketing and distribution partnerships such as these chewed up 19% of revenue in the first half of 2026, up from 10% in the same period a year before.

Anthropic 在销售、营销和分销合作上的支出，在 2026 年上半年吞掉了营收的 19%，高于一年前同期的 10%。

Another problem Anthropic faces is that, like other AI developers, it is struggling to secure the computing power it needs.

Anthropic 面临的另一个问题是，与其他 AI 开发者一样，它正艰难地争取所需的算力。

In its prospectus it warns investors that its use of "short-term compute" could go up in the near term, weighing on margins. For example, Anthropic has agreed to pay SpaceX about $3.1bn per month to use its Nvidia chips into 2029.

在招股书中，它警告投资者，其对"短期算力"的使用可能在短期内上升，拖累利润率。例如，Anthropic 已同意向 SpaceX 支付约每月 31 亿美元，以使用其英伟达芯片直至 2029 年。

(Anthropic is able to back out from early next year with 90 days' notice.) That explains why Anthropic is starting to develop more of its own computing infrastructure, rather than relying solely on cloud providers.

（Anthropic 可从明年初起提前 90 天通知退出。）这也解释了为何 Anthropic 开始更多地自建算力基础设施，而非仅仅依赖云服务商。

This strategy should help keep operating costs under control, but also provides forewarning that the lab will become a more capital-intensive business in the long run.

这一策略应有助于控制运营成本，但也预示着这家实验室长远来看将成为一门更资本密集的业务。

The initiative, called "Anthropic Direct", involves procuring chips from providers such as Broadcom, which develops custom silicon, and installing them in rented data centres.

这项名为"Anthropic Direct"的举措，涉及从博通等供应商处采购芯片（博通开发定制芯片），并将其部署在租赁的数据中心里。

That could make it harder to start throwing off the kind of free cashflow investors might hope for, and require the business to raise more capital.

这可能使其更难开始释放投资者所期望的那种自由现金流，并需要公司募集更多资本。

In Anthropic's prospectus, its founders argue that its spending plans "may seem irresponsibly large at first glance", but are justified by the exponential trajectory that AI is on. "We will aim to be bold but sane," they write.

在 Anthropic 的招股书中，其创始人辩称，其支出计划"乍看可能显得不负责任地庞大"，但鉴于 AI 所处的指数级发展轨迹，这是合理的。"我们将力求大胆而清醒，"他们写道。

With a technology so mind-bending, striking that balance may prove surprisingly difficult. ■

面对如此令人难以想象的技术，把握好这种平衡或许会被证明异常困难。■
