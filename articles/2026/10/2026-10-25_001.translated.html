China has captured the market for hobbyist 3D printers

从19%到96%！中国3D打印机十年屠榜全球，西方巨头彻底出局

The fastest-growing industrial product in China today is not the electric vehicle, lithium-ion battery or even humanoid robot. It is the humble 3D printer for hobbyists.

今天中国增长最快的工业品，不是电动汽车、锂电池，甚至也不是人形机器人，而是不起眼的爱好者级 3D 打印机。

Once geeky curios, 3D printers have quietly become one of China's most successful consumer electronics. Exports of the machines grew by 90% in the first half of this year.

3D 打印机曾是极客圈的稀罕玩意儿，如今已悄然成为中国最成功的消费电子产品之一。今年上半年，这类机器的出口增长了 90%。

In the past decade China's global market share has risen from 19% to 96%, ending the dominance of Western firms.

过去十年，中国的全球市场份额从 19% 升至 96%，终结了西方企业的主导地位。

The personal 3D-printing market is now split among the "four little dragons of Shenzhen", says Chris Connery of Context, a London-based market-intelligence firm. Creality, Anycubic, Elegoo and the industry's leader, Bambu Lab, reign supreme globally (see chart).

伦敦市场情报公司 Context 的克里斯·康纳里（Chris Connery）说，个人 3D 打印市场如今由"深圳四小龙"瓜分：创想三维、纵维立方、智能派，以及行业龙头拓竹科技，它们在全球称雄（见图表）。

Their success shows, yet again, how fierce competition in China combined with generous government support can help improve quality and drive down export prices, making Chinese products hard to beat.

它们的成功再次说明：中国激烈的竞争加上慷慨的政府支持，能提升质量、压低出口价格，让中国产品难以被超越。

The printers work by building objects layer by layer, usually by extruding molten plastic. Invented by Chuck Hull, an American, in 1983, they long remained expensive, unreliable and difficult to use.

这类打印机靠逐层堆叠物体工作，通常是把熔化的塑料挤出成型。它由美国人查克·赫尔（Chuck Hull）于 1983 年发明，长期以来价格昂贵、性能不稳、难以上手。

Today they routinely sell for a little more than $500 and have the sleek aluminium finish of an Apple computer. Comparable entry-level printers from Western firms start at more than double the price.

如今它们通常只卖 500 美元出头，外观是苹果电脑那种光洁的铝质感。西方企业的同类入门机型，起价要高一倍多。

Artificial intelligence allows users to create print designs for such things as tools, parts or toys by describing them in natural language rather than using complicated design-software.

人工智能让用户只要用自然语言描述，就能生成工具、零件或玩具的打印设计，不必再用复杂的设计软件。

Just as Chinese factories helped to turn drones into recreational devices (as well as game-changers in warfare) by achieving large-scale production at low cost, so they are now doing with 3D printers.

正如中国工厂靠低成本大规模生产，把无人机变成了娱乐设备（也成了战场上的颠覆者），如今它们正用同样的方式做 3D 打印机。

For most of its history, consumer 3D printing had an unspoken cost of entry: patience. Getting a printer to actually work often took longer than using it.

在大部分历史里，消费级 3D 打印有一项心照不宣的入门代价：耐心。让一台打印机真正跑起来，往往比用它本身更费时间。

Bed leveling, calibration, and trial and error troubleshooting weren't occasional annoyances; they were simply part of owning one. That was the deal, and for years, nobody in the industry seriously challenged it.

调平台、校准、反复试错排查故障，并不算偶发的烦心事，它们就是拥有这台机器的一部分。这就是规矩，多年来业内没人认真挑战过它。

Part of Bambu Lab's different approach can be traced back to its founding team. Several of its engineers came from DJI, the drone manufacturer, with backgrounds in robotics and consumer electronics.

拓竹科技的不同路线，部分可以追溯到它的创始团队。公司有几位工程师来自无人机制造商大疆，背景是机器人技术和消费电子。

Drones present a similar design challenge to 3D printers: they're mechanically complex, but successful consumer products need to be usable without specialized training. Bambu Lab's founders had already worked through that kind of problem before applying it to printing.

无人机与 3D 打印机面临相似的设计难题：机械结构复杂，但成功的消费产品必须让人无需专门培训就能上手。拓竹的创始人此前已经啃过这类问题，然后才把它用到打印上。

The company also didn't rush its first product. It spent 22 months on research and development before releasing its first printer, the X1, which launched on Kickstarter and raised about HK$55 million, or roughly $7 million, from 5,575 backers.

公司也没有急着推出第一款产品。它花了 22 个月做研发，才发布首款打印机 X1；该机型在 Kickstarter 上众筹，从 5,575 名支持者那里筹到约 5,500 万港元，约合 700 万美元。

The core of Bambu Lab's approach is automating steps that previously required manual adjustment.

拓竹方法的核心，是把过去需要人工调整的步骤自动化。

Its printers include cameras, LiDAR, and gravity sensors that handle bed leveling and calibration automatically on every print, without requiring the user to understand the underlying mechanics.

它的打印机配有摄像头、激光雷达和重力传感器，每次打印都自动完成调平台和校准，用户无需懂背后的机械原理。

LatePost described the result as "a small AI-enabled robot." One Bambu Lab owner on Reddit summarized the difference simply: "Bambu printers just work." Price wars have eaten into the printer dragons' profits even as sales boom.

晚点（LatePost）把结果形容为"一台小型 AI 机器人"。一位拓竹用户在 Reddit 上把差别总结得很简单："拓竹的机器就是能直接用。"即便销量猛增，价格战还是啃掉了这些打印机"小龙"的利润。

But the firms still excite investors. Bambu Lab was founded in 2020 by alumni of another Chinese company, DJI, the world's leading dronemaker. The printer-maker is reported to have a private valuation of about $10bn.

但这些企业依然让投资者兴奋。拓竹科技由另一家中国公司、全球领先的无人机制造商大疆的前员工于 2020 年创立。据报道，这家打印机制造商的私募估值约为 100 亿美元。

Last year DJI itself invested in Elegoo, a Bambu rival. Creality listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in May. It boasts a market capitalisation of HK$12bn ($1.5bn).

去年大疆自己也投资了拓竹的对手智能派。创想三维 5 月在香港交易所上市，市值达 120 亿港元（15 亿美元）。

The government cherishes 3D-printing firms. The business featured in its "Made in China 2025" industrial plan, which was launched in 2015 to ensure domestic strength and self-reliance in high-tech manufacturing (much to the West's concern).

政府珍视 3D 打印企业。这一行业被列入 2015 年启动的"中国制造 2025"产业规划，目标是确保高科技制造领域的国内实力与自力更生（这让西方颇为不安）。

Officials see 3D printers as a way to produce components of aerospace, health care and military equipment, for which China often relies on Western suppliers. Policymakers have directed research institutes and state-run firms to buy them.

官员把 3D 打印机视为生产航空航天、医疗和军事装备零部件的途径——这些领域中国常常依赖西方供应商。政策制定者已指示科研院所和国有企业采购。

Some competitors say Chinese firms have profited from Western open-source printing software. Last month an American court ruled that Bambu Lab had infringed on four patents held by an American company, and ordered it to pay $27.6m in damages.

一些竞争对手称，中国企业从西方的开源打印软件中获利。上个月，美国一家法院裁定拓竹侵犯了一家美国公司持有的四项专利，判其赔偿 2,760 万美元。

Although Chinese firms have captured the consumer market, they have struggled to do so for industrial 3D printers.

尽管中国企业拿下了消费市场，在工业级 3D 打印机上却一直吃力。

Firms are wary of relying on Chinese products if it involves feeding them with sensitive designs (though Britain's defence ministry has been using Bambu printers, its government admits).

如果涉及把敏感设计交给中国产品，企业会保持警惕（不过英国政府承认，其国防部一直在用拓竹打印机）。

Outside China, top-of-the-range machines are still mostly supplied by American and European outfits. They can sell for millions of dollars. Undeterred, Chinese suppliers are creating a new form of manufacturing at home and abroad.

在中国以外，顶配机器仍主要由美国和欧洲厂商供应，售价可达数百万美元。中国供应商并未被吓退，正在国内外开创一种新的制造形态。

They are pioneering "3D-printing farms"—facilities with as many as 5,000 desktop 3D printers that operate like factories. Several have been built in China this year.

它们正在开创"3D 打印农场"——配备多达 5,000 台桌面 3D 打印机、像工厂一样运转的设施。今年中国已建成数座。

Printing farms can mass-produce trinkets for zany consumer trends in short order, replacing expensive and slow injection-moulding techniques. Like the printers themselves, their output will be higher quality and cheaper than what came before.

打印农场能很快为古怪的消费潮流批量生产小玩意儿，取代昂贵而缓慢的注塑工艺。和打印机本身一样，它们的产出质量更高、价格更便宜。
