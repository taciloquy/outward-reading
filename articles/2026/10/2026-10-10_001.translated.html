What Experts Want to Know About the Russia Plague Scare

关于俄罗斯鼠疫恐慌，专家想知道什么

A 28-year-old worker at a research laboratory in Siberia recently died of pneumonia. The facility studies plague, among other pathogens, raising fears that she had been exposed at the laboratory.

西伯利亚一家研究实验室的一名 28 岁工作人员最近死于肺炎。该机构研究鼠疫等多种病原体，引发了人们对她是否在实验室内遭到暴露的担忧。

But Russian health officials so far have not linked the death of the worker, Darya Shipilova, to infection with Yersinia pestis, the bacterium that causes plague.

但俄罗斯卫生官员迄今并未把这位名叫达里娅·希皮洛娃的工作人员的死亡，与鼠疫耶尔森菌（引发鼠疫的细菌）感染联系起来。

And despite early reports that dozens had been quarantined near the research facility, health officials said that there was no risk of an epidemic.

尽管早先有报道称该研究机构附近有数十人被隔离，卫生官员表示不存在疫情风险。

Still, the death has prompted concerns around the world, in part because of the Soviet Union's long history of advanced bioweapons research, which led to outbreaks of anthrax and smallpox.

尽管如此，这起死亡仍引发了全球关注，部分原因在于苏联在先进生物武器研究上的悠久历史——那段历史曾导致炭疽和天花疫情暴发。

After unconfirmed reports of a second unusual case of pneumonia in the region, the World Health Organization sought additional information from Russian officials. President Trump said he planned to speak to President Vladimir V. Putin of Russia "very soon" about the death. Early reports cited local officials saying that Ms. Shipilova was exposed to Y. pestis during her work at the Irkutsk Anti-Plague Research Institute of Siberia and the Far East.

在该地区出现第二例异常肺炎病例的未经证实报道之后，世界卫生组织向俄罗斯官员索取更多信息。特朗普总统表示，他计划"很快"与俄罗斯总统弗拉基米尔·普京谈这起死亡事件。早先的报道援引当地官员的话说，希皮洛娃在伊尔库茨克西伯利亚及远东抗鼠疫研究所工作时接触过鼠疫耶尔森菌。

Y. pestis was responsible for two of history's deadliest pandemics: the Black Death and the Plague of Justinian. As a lung infection, plague is contagious, although not as infectious as some other respiratory infections.

鼠疫耶尔森菌造成了历史上最致命的两次大流行：黑死病和查士丁尼瘟疫。作为一种肺部感染，鼠疫具有传染性，尽管传染力不如其他一些呼吸道感染。

(The bacterium also infects the bloodstream and lymph nodes.) In its early stages, plague can be successfully treated with antibiotics. There are hundreds of cases each year, mostly in Africa, according to the W.H.O..

（这种细菌也会感染血液和淋巴结。）在早期阶段，鼠疫可以用抗生素成功治疗。据世卫组织数据，每年有数百例病例，大多在非洲。

Russian health officials said that Ms. Shipilova had died of pneumonia of unknown cause. While plague can lead to pneumonia, the filling of the lung's air sacs with fluid, it can have many other causes, including seasonal flu.

俄罗斯卫生官员说，希皮洛娃死于病因不明的肺炎。虽然鼠疫可导致肺炎——即肺气囊被液体充满——但肺炎也可能由许多其他原因引起，包括季节性流感。

"Unfortunately, young people can die from pneumonia, so that development alone wouldn't be enough to raise concern," said Dr. Tom Inglesby, an epidemiologist at Johns Hopkins Center for Health Security.

"不幸的是，年轻人也可能死于肺炎，所以单是这一进展还不足以引起担忧，"约翰斯·霍普金斯健康安全中心的流行病学家汤姆·英格尔斯比博士说。

"What I do think is concerning is the Russian reaction so far." More than 200 people who had been in contact with the deceased were quarantined in local hospitals, and Russian officials have not explained why, Dr. Inglesby said.

"我确实认为令人担忧的是俄罗斯迄今的反应。"英格尔斯比说，200 多名与死者有过接触的人在当地医院被隔离，而俄罗斯官员没有解释原因。

Nor have they announced the cause of death, if not plague. Lab accidents can and do happen, even when researchers are following safety protocols.

他们也没有公布死因（如果不是鼠疫的话）。即便研究人员遵守安全规程，实验室事故也可能、而且确实会发生。

But the lack of transparency surrounding the death and the number of people potentially exposed is unusual, other experts said. "Something is not adding up about this situation," said Jennifer Nuzzo, an epidemiologist at Brown University.

但其他专家说，围绕这起死亡以及可能暴露人数的缺乏透明度很不寻常。"这件事什么地方对不上，"布朗大学流行病学家珍妮弗·努佐说。

"You don't quarantine 200 people for one plague case," she added.

"你不会因为一例鼠疫就隔离 200 人，"她补充说。

"You don't close multiple hospital units in response to one infection." After an unusual infection like this, health officials often provide the genome sequence of the virus or bacterium involved.

"你也不会因为一例感染就关闭多个医院病区。"在出现这类异常感染之后，卫生官员通常会公布相关病毒或细菌的基因组序列。

This helps researchers pinpoint exactly which pathogen is to blame and how to fight it.

这有助于研究人员精确锁定罪魁祸首是哪种病原体，以及如何对抗它。

Public health experts suspect that Russian officials have already sequenced the pathogen involved in Ms. Shipilova's death, and they wonder why that information has not been made public.

公共卫生专家怀疑俄罗斯官员已经对希皮洛娃死亡涉及的病原体做了测序，他们想知道为什么这些信息没有公开。

"Without that, the risk is uncertain, and I think we should be putting pressure on Russia to share the genome sequence," said Dr. Raj Panjabi, a physician and former White House biosecurity official.

"没有这些信息，风险就是不确定的；我认为我们应该向俄罗斯施压，要求其分享基因组序列，"内科医生、前白宫生物安全官员拉杰·潘贾比博士说。

"If Y. pestis is confirmed and the presentation was pneumonia, the likely route is inhalation of an infectious aerosol during laboratory work," said Dr. Tom Frieden, a physician and former director of the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention.

"如果确认是鼠疫耶尔森菌，且表现为肺炎，最可能的途径是在实验室工作中吸入了传染性气溶胶，"内科医生、前美国疾病控制与预防中心主任汤姆·弗里登博士说。

But until more information is released, he added, "anyone claiming to know more is guessing." Thanks to safety protocols and modern personal protective equipment, exposures to pathogens in labs are not common.

但他补充说，在更多信息公布之前，"任何声称知道更多的人都是在猜"。得益于安全规程和现代个人防护装备，实验室中接触到病原体的情况并不常见。

Y. pestis is a particular concern, Dr. Frieden noted. It requires that laboratories maintain a certain airflow, HEPA filtration, respiratory protection, precautions against aerosols, trained staff members and health monitoring. "Done right, it's safe," Dr. Frieden said.

弗里登指出，鼠疫耶尔森菌尤其令人担忧。它要求实验室维持特定的气流、高效空气过滤（HEPA）、呼吸防护、防气溶胶措施、受过培训的人员以及健康监测。"做得对，就是安全的，"弗里登说。

"The problem is that 'done right' is not verifiable, and it's not easy." The last time a fatal exposure to Y. pestis occurred in a laboratory environment was in 2009, when Malcolm Casadaban, a biologist at the University of Chicago, died after contact with a weakened form of the bacterium.

"问题在于'做得对'无法验证，而且并不容易。"上一次在实验室环境中发生致命的鼠疫耶尔森菌暴露是在 2009 年：芝加哥大学的生物学家马尔科姆·卡萨达班在接触一种弱化型细菌后死亡。

When accidents do happen, Dr. Panjabi said, it's important to have a system in place to characterize the outbreak, including how fast the threat is moving, who is at risk and what options are available to mitigate that threat.

潘贾比说，当事故真的发生时，重要的是要有一套系统来刻画这起暴发——包括威胁扩散有多快、谁处于风险之中，以及有哪些选项可以缓解这一威胁。

"The larger issue of maintaining biosafety in high consequence labs that study dangerous pathogens is a national security issue in every country," Dr. Panjabi said. "And it needs more attention."

"在那些研究危险病原体的高后果实验室中维持生物安全这一更大的议题，在每个国家都是国家安全问题，"潘贾比说，"它需要更多关注。"
