A Near Disaster on Flydubai

迪拜航空的一次险情

This unbelievable sequence of events has huge implications.

这一连串令人难以置信的事件，影响重大。

Today, somewhere over Saudi Arabia, the co-pilot of an Israel-bound Flydubai Boeing 737 stabbed the other pilot and pitched the nose of the plane toward the desert below.

今天，在沙特阿拉伯某处的上空，一架飞往以色列的迪拜航空（Flydubai）波音 737 的副机长刺伤了另一名飞行员，并把机头压向下方沙漠。

The jet descended 17,000 feet in two minutes, at an acceleration violent enough to make your phone and in-flight snack jump off your tray and be pinned to the ceiling.

这架喷气式客机在两分钟内下降了 17000 英尺，加速度之猛，足以让你的手机和机上零食从餐板上弹起、被压在天花板上。

Other crew members and at least one Israeli passenger broke into the cockpit and subdued the assailant. Relief pilots then took over and landed the plane in Tabuk, Saudi Arabia.

其他机组人员和至少一名以色列乘客冲进驾驶舱制服了袭击者。随后替补飞行员接手，把飞机降落在沙特阿拉伯的塔布克。

After a second Flydubai plane took the 174 passengers to Tel Aviv, one of the passengers who’d stormed the cockpit told Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu that his actions had been effective in part because he had watched the disaster-porn documentary series Mayday, about aviation catastrophes, and had known to pull back on the yoke.

在第二架迪拜航空的飞机把 174 名乘客送往特拉维夫后，其中一名冲进驾驶舱的乘客告诉以色列总理本雅明·内塔尼亚胡：他的举动之所以有效，部分原因在于他看过以空难为主题的"灾难片式"纪录剧集《Mayday》（《空中浩劫》），知道要向后拉操纵杆。

Whether today’s near-disaster was terrorism or merely attempted mass murder depends on the attacker’s motive. Either way, it is a public-relations catastrophe for Flydubai, the budget cousin of the airline Emirates.

今天的这起险情究竟属于恐怖主义，还是仅仅是一起未遂的大规模谋杀，取决于袭击者的动机。无论哪种，对阿联酋航空的廉价子品牌迪拜航空来说，这都是一场公关灾难。

Since the United Arab Emirates opened diplomatic relations with Israel in 2020, the airline has connected the two countries with few interruptions, even for acts of war and terror.

自 2020 年阿联酋与以色列建交以来，这条航线几乎未中断过，即便是在战争和恐怖行为期间。

Keeping that air route open and safe has taken a great deal of effort.

保持这条航线开放且安全，付出了巨大努力。

Last year, I witnessed clever security engineering at Dubai’s terminal 1: placement of the check-in desk for El Al, the Israeli carrier, adjacent to the desk for Iran’s Mahan Air.

去年，我在迪拜 1 号航站楼目睹了巧妙的安全工程设计：以色列航空公司 El Al 的值机柜台，被安排在伊朗马汉航空（Mahan Air）柜台旁边。

Anyone tempted to attack Israeli passengers would hit Iranian ones, and vice versa. Checked baggage costs extra, but the experience of being a human shield while waiting in line is complimentary.

任何想袭击以色列乘客的人都会先打到伊朗人，反之亦然。托运行李要另外收费，但在排队时当"人肉盾牌"的体验则是免费奉送。

Mahan Air no longer flies into Dubai. U.S. sanctions have halted much of its operations, and Dubai had already curtailed its flights, on the grounds that Iran can fly either missiles into Dubai or commercial flights, but not both.

马汉航空如今已不再飞迪拜。美国制裁让其大部分运营停摆，而迪拜此前就已削减其航班，理由是：伊朗要么向迪拜发射导弹，要么飞来商业航班，二者不可兼得。

Israel, by contrast, has emerged from the 2026 Iran war closer to the UAE, which has absorbed more Iranian attacks than any other country.

相比之下，以色列在 2026 年的伊朗战争之后与阿联酋走得更近了——阿联酋承受的伊朗袭击比任何其他国家都多。

Although the Arab countries of the region have been allied as the Gulf Cooperation Council since 1981, that alliance proved useless as soon as the missiles and drones started flying.

虽然该地区的阿拉伯国家自 1981 年起就以海湾合作委员会的形式结盟，但导弹和无人机一开始飞，这个联盟就证明毫无用处。

But Israel responded as an ally, by sending defense systems and advisers. At least one Iranian strike during the early phases of the war hit near the Israeli consulate in Dubai.

而以色列以盟友的方式作出了回应——派出防御系统和顾问。战争初期至少有一次伊朗的袭击落在迪拜以色列领事馆附近。

One possible political motive for the Flydubai incident would be to strain and punish the relationship between the two countries. The relationship between Israel and the UAE is unlikely to snap over a single attack.

迪拜航空这起事件的一个可能的政治动机，是让两国关系承压、受到惩罚。但以色列与阿联酋的关系不太可能因为一次袭击就断裂。

The relationship between the UAE and Netanyahu himself is more delicate.

阿联酋与内塔尼亚胡本人的关系则要微妙得多。

In the past several weeks, the president of the UAE, Mohammed bin Zayed Al Nahyan, was implicated in a report from Haaretz that severely damaged Netanyahu’s chances of remaining in office after next month’s Israeli election.

过去几周，《国土报》的一篇报道把阿联酋总统穆罕默德·本·扎耶德·阿勒纳哈扬牵扯进来，严重损害了内塔尼亚胡在下月以色列大选后继续执政的机会。

The report alleged that days before the massacre of October 7, 2023, MBZ had personally warned Netanyahu that an attack was imminent but Netanyahu had done nothing to act on the warning.

报道称，在 2023 年 10 月 7 日大屠杀前几天，MBZ 曾亲自警告内塔尼亚胡袭击迫在眉睫，但内塔尼亚胡未采取任何行动。

The UAE government at first refused to comment on the story. On Sunday, however, Netanyahu visited Abu Dhabi, and the AP reported that he asked MBZ to deny that he’d warned of an attack.

阿联酋政府起初拒绝置评。但周日，内塔尼亚胡访问了阿布扎比；据美联社报道，他要求 MBZ 否认自己曾发出过袭击警告。

Yesterday, in New York, UAE Foreign Minister Abdullah bin Zayed did exactly that.

昨天，在纽约，阿联酋外长阿卜杜拉·本·扎耶德正是这么做的。

"Sheikh Mohammed did not call Prime Minister Netanyahu and say there is going to be an attack on Israel," Abdullah said in a meeting at the Council on Foreign Relations, calling his denial "categorical." He said that using a leader-to-leader call to pass along secrets was "not our style," and he left open the possibility that a warning had been passed between governments, if not between leaders.

"谢赫穆罕默德没有打电话给内塔尼亚胡总理说以色列将遭到袭击，"阿卜杜拉在对外关系委员会的一场会议上说，并称他的否认是"绝对的"。他说，用领导人之间的通话来传递机密"不是我们的风格"，同时他留有余地：警告可能在政府之间而非领导人之间传递过。

This denial releases some pressure on Netanyahu without absolving his government of its failures. The New York Times and others have reported that the first officer who allegedly tried to bring down the plane is an Omani national.

这一否认缓解了内塔尼亚胡身上的部分压力，却没有免除其政府的失职。《纽约时报》等报道称，涉嫌试图驾机坠毁的那名副驾驶是阿曼国籍。

Both are reportedly still in Saudi Arabia.

据报道，两人目前都仍在沙特阿拉伯。

The next part of the story will involve determining whether the pilot acted alone—not a good look for Flydubai, to have suicidal or terrorist pilots—or at someone’s behest.

故事的下一部分将是判断这名飞行员是单独行动——对迪拜航空来说，出了个自杀式或恐怖分子飞行员可不是什么好看的事——还是受人指使。

The latter possibility could, if confirmed, have serious implications, depending on who directed the attack. Many have a motive: Sunni jihadists (al-Qaeda or the Islamic State), Hamas, and of course Iran and its proxies.

若是后者，一旦得到确认，视乎指使方是谁，可能产生严重影响。很多方都有动机：逊尼派圣战分子（基地组织或"伊斯兰国"）、哈马斯，当然还有伊朗及其代理人。

This Mayday moment will have huge implications if any of these groups are involved.

如果这些组织中的任何一个牵涉其中，这个"Mayday 时刻"都将产生重大影响。
