A SpaceX Starship Rocket Officially Reached Orbit. Why That's So Significant

SpaceX 星舰正式入轨——为什么这事如此重要

For three and a half years, ever since the spring of 2023, when SpaceX launched the first of its massive Starship rockets, the company has been promising that before too long the towering, stainless steel machine would get to orbit.

三年半以来——自 2023 年春天 SpaceX 发射首枚巨型星舰火箭起——该公司就一直承诺，这台高耸的不锈钢机器迟早会进入轨道。

This morning, on the 14th such launch, it made good on that boast. "Starship performs its orbital insertion burn and enters orbit of Earth for the first time," SpaceX announced on X at 9:15 a.m. ET, along with a video of that engine maneuver. It took just 25 minutes and 17 seconds for the spacecraft to travel from its launch pad in Starbase, Texas, to its circular orbit 170 miles high.

今天上午，在第 14 次此类发射中，它兑现了这一豪言。"星舰执行入轨点火，首次进入地球轨道，"SpaceX 于美东时间上午 9 点 15 分在 X 上宣布，并附带了那段发动机机动的视频。飞船从得州星舰基地的发射台到 170 英里高的圆轨道，只用了 25 分 17 秒。

There is no overstating how important today's success was to SpaceX's—and NASA's—long-term plans.

今天的成功对 SpaceX——以及 NASA——的长期计划有多重要，怎么说都不为过。

Starship is pivotal to getting human beings to the moon and Mars, and SpaceX sees it as an ultimate replacement for its workhorse Falcon 9 rocket, which accounted for nearly 51% of the global total of commercial launches— including 85% of all satellites—in 2025.

星舰是把人类送上月球和火星的关键，SpaceX 也把它视为自家主力火箭猎鹰 9 号的终极替代者——2025 年猎鹰 9 号承担了全球商业发射总量的近 51%，其中包括 85% 的所有卫星。

Starship's far larger payload bay can carry a cargo equivalent of nearly five Falcon 9s, ultimately consolidating launches and cutting costs.

星舰大得多的载荷舱可装载相当于近五枚猎鹰 9 号的货物，最终把发射任务合并起来、降低成本。

In 2021, NASA selected Starship as one of two spacecraft that will land astronauts on the moon as early as 2028.(Blue Origin, the aerospace company founded by Amazon boss Jeff Bezos, will provide the other.) In 2027, NASA is planning a crewed test of either or both spacecraft in Earth orbit during the mission of Artemis III.

2021 年，NASA 选定星舰作为最早在 2028 年把宇航员送上月球的两艘飞船之一（另一艘由亚马逊老板杰夫·贝索斯创办的航空航天公司蓝色起源提供）。2027 年，NASA 计划在"阿尔忒弥斯 3 号"任务期间，在地球轨道上对其中一艘或两艘进行载人测试。

Early on, routine was not remotely how Starship launches were being described. Four minutes into the rocket's inaugural launch on April 20, 2023, it exploded spectacularly.

早期，星舰的发射怎么也谈不上"例行"。2023 年 4 月 20 日首飞四分钟后，火箭壮烈爆炸。

Concrete and steel debris rained down in a near-400-acre footprint in the surrounding Boca Chica State Park, and a 3.85-acre fire burned south of the launch site.

混凝土和钢铁碎片散落在周边博卡奇卡州立公园近 400 英亩范围内，发射场以南还燃起了一场 3.85 英亩的大火。

But the flights proceeded apace at a rate of once every three months, none achieving stable orbit— until today. The launch this morning was both imperfect and stripped down to its orbital essentials.

但飞行仍以每三个月一次的节奏推进，没有一次实现稳定入轨——直到今天。今晨这次发射既不完美，也被精简到了入轨所必需的最低限度。

On the way up, one of the Starship's six engines failed to burn properly, requiring the other engines to compensate for the missing thrust to get the ship in orbit.

上升过程中，星舰六台发动机中有一台未能正常燃烧，需要其余发动机补偿损失的推力，才把飞船送入轨道。

In addition, the return to Earth was simplified.

此外，返回地球的过程也被简化了。

Starship's first stage, meantime, performs what has become known as a chopstick recovery, with the booster navigating its way back to the launch tower where two giant metal arms pluck it from the sky.

星舰的第一级 meanwhile 执行所谓的"筷子回收"：助推器自行导航回发射塔，由两只巨大的金属臂从空中把它夹住。

For the current mission, the chopstick recovery was done away with to simplify the flight objectives; instead the first stage made a soft, engine-assisted splashdown in the Gulf of Mexico.

本次任务为简化飞行目标，取消了筷子回收；第一级改为在墨西哥湾以发动机辅助的方式软溅落。

The Starship spacecraft was planned for a six-orbit, 10-hour mission.

星舰飞船原计划执行 6 圈轨道、10 小时的任务。

Today's launch was not just a test flight; it was also an operational mission designed to deliver 26 Starlink satellites to orbit, adding to the whopping 11,000 that already comprise the Starlink constellation—with an eventual goal of 100,000.

今天的发射不只是一次试飞，它也是一次业务发射，任务是把 26 颗星链卫星送入轨道——星链星座已拥有惊人的 11000 颗卫星，最终目标是 10 万颗。

SpaceX released footage of Starship deploying the satellites with what it has dubbed its Pez dispenser system, because that's exactly what the spacecraft resembles as it releases its payload one satellite at a time.

SpaceX 发布了星舰部署卫星的画面，使用的是它称之为"PEZ 糖果盒"的释放系统——因为飞船一颗一颗释放载荷的样子，确实就像那种糖果盒。

Ultimately, the spacecraft was brought down early, after just three orbits, since its satellites had all been deployed and the ship proved its flight worthiness quickly.

最终，飞船被提前带回，只飞了 3 圈就再入——因为卫星已全部部署完毕，飞船也很快证明了自身的适航性。

The launch of Starship 14 is in many ways only a very small—if critical—step. Even the biggest SpaceX supporters don't pretend that Starship is anywhere near ready to land astronauts on the moon in 2028.

星舰第 14 次发射，在很多意义上只是非常小——但关键——的一步。即便是最坚定的 SpaceX 支持者，也不会假装星舰已接近可以在 2028 年把宇航员送上月球。

The spacecraft—which is now essentially a giant, flying, cargo truck—has to be adapted to carry crew, with all of the environmental and habitat modifications that implies.

这台飞船——本质上现在是一辆巨大的飞行卡车——必须被改造以载运人员，包括由此带来的一切环境和居住条件改造。

It will also have to solve a stubborn refueling problem: before heading out for the moon, Starship will need to enter a parking orbit around the Earth where up to a dozen other Starship-sized tankers will top off its tanks.

它还必须解决一个棘手的加注问题：在前往月球之前，星舰需要先进入一条绕地停泊轨道，在那里由多达十几艘星舰大小的油船为其加满燃料。

It will have to fly its Artemis III test flight in 2027, and later, an uncrewed landing on—and liftoff from—the moon before it will be trusted to carry astronauts in as little as two years' time.

它还得在 2027 年完成阿尔忒弥斯 3 号的试飞，之后再进行一次无人月球着陆与起飞，才能被信任在短短两年后载运宇航员。

Still, SpaceX promises that an Earth-orbital launch that today is headline-making could become almost as commonplace as the takeoff of a commercial airliner.

尽管如此，SpaceX 承诺：今天这样上新闻头条的地球轨道发射，未来可能变得几乎像民航客机起飞一样平常。

Elon Musk, the company's founder and CEO, has spoken openly about Starships launching as frequently as once a day—or even once an hour. Maybe.

该公司创始人兼 CEO 埃隆·马斯克曾公开谈到星舰发射频率高达一天一次——甚至一小时一次。也许吧。

For now the company is celebrating one launch on one day, on a mission that didn't even last past east coast lunchtime. But spaceflight is iterative and incremental. And today SpaceX moved one big increment indeed.

眼下，公司正在庆祝某一天的一次发射，而这次任务甚至没撑过美东时间的午餐时分。但航天飞行是迭代的、渐进的。而今天，SpaceX 确实迈出了一大步。
