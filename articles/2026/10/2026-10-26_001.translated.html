US has for years asked Musk to alter Starlink service in Ukraine, Middle East, sources say

多年来，美国一直要求马斯克调整星链在乌克兰及中东的服务

Well before the throttling this month of Ukraine's military access to Elon Musk's satellite communications system, the Biden and Trump administrations had several times asked SpaceX to turn its Starlink service on or off in various countries, according to six former senior US officials familiar with the matter.

本月乌克兰军方使用埃隆·马斯克卫星通信系统的权限遭到限速，而早在这次限速之前，拜登和特朗普两届政府就已多次要求 SpaceX 在多个国家开启或关闭星链服务——据六位了解内情的前美国高级官员说。

During the presidency of Joe Biden, the requests included switching off the service to prevent Iranian proxy militias in Yemen and Lebanon from using Starlink to communicate, and activating the service to provide internet access inside Iran and in Ukraine to support its military.

在乔·拜登任内，这些要求包括：关掉服务，以防伊朗在也门和黎巴嫩的代理民兵用星链通信；以及开通服务，在伊朗和乌克兰境内提供互联网接入，以支持乌军。

Similarly, President Donald Trump's administration asked Musk, who is CEO of SpaceX, to make the service available in Iran following US-Israeli strikes in 2025 and 2026, in hopes it would bolster internet access and communications for anti government protesters.

同样，唐纳德·特朗普政府也曾要求身为 SpaceX 首席执行官的马斯克在伊朗开放服务，那是在 2025 年和 2026 年美以对伊朗发动打击之后，希望借此增强反政府抗议者的网络接入与通信。

The recent blowup over restrictions to Ukraine's use of SpaceX satellite services has raised questions about the ties between the US government and Musk, whose company owns systems vital to the US military and to US allies.

最近围绕乌克兰使用 SpaceX 卫星服务受限的风波，令外界质疑美国政府与马斯克之间的关系——他的公司掌握着对美国军方及其盟友至关重要的系统。

The Biden-era and Trump requests to Musk show a close relationship in which US administrations rely on an unprecedented degree of a private-sector company to help implement policy.

拜登与特朗普两届政府对马斯克的这些要求，显示出一种密切关系：美国政府前所未有地依赖一家私营企业来协助实施外交政策。

The recent limits on Ukraine, despite being a US ally, have also rattled officials in Europe, some of whom argue that Europe must develop its own military satellite communications capabilities because no viable short-term alternative currently exists.

对乌克兰这个美国盟友的限制，也令欧洲官员不安。其中一些人主张，欧洲必须发展自己的军用卫星通信能力，因为目前没有可行的短期替代方案。

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said last week that his country's access to Starshield, the military version of the Starlink system, had been intentionally limited, interfering with the performance of the country's military drones.

乌克兰总统泽连斯基上周表示，该国使用"星盾"（Starshield，星链系统的军用版本）的权限被故意限制，影响了本国军用无人机的性能。

He also said the speeds were reduced for Starlink, which Ukraine uses for some military activities including defense against Russian missile attacks.

他还说，星链的速度被下调，而乌克兰将星链用于部分军事活动，包括防御俄罗斯的导弹袭击。

The White House, contacted for comment, referred Reuters to Trump's remarks on Friday, when he said he had not spoken with Musk about Starlink access issues for Ukraine and that "if there is a problem, I'll get it straightened out." SpaceX did not respond to requests for comment, although Musk said in a social media post earlier this week: "Starshield systems are owned and controlled by the US government.

白宫在被问及置评时，让路透社去问特朗普周五的表态——特朗普称他没有就乌克兰的星链接入问题与马斯克谈过，"如果有问题，我会把它理顺"。SpaceX 未回应置评请求，但马斯克本周早些时候在社交媒体发帖称："星盾系统由美国政府拥有并控制。

Any limits on speed or usage are decided by the US military and ultimately (by the president of the United States)." For Ukraine, the Starlink internet service has become the lifeblood of frontline communication between units, who say there is currently no rival to the capabilities of SpaceX's products.

任何速度或用量上的限制都由美国军方决定，并最终由（美国总统）决定。"对乌克兰而言，星链网络服务已成为各部队之间前线通信的命脉，他们说目前没有任何产品能匹敌 SpaceX 的能力。

Starshield receivers, largely impervious to radio jamming, are used as onboard guidance systems and to provide links to a remote pilot for Ukrainian drones, including deep behind Russian lines inside Ukraine.

星盾接收器基本不受无线电干扰，被用作机载制导系统，并为乌克兰无人机提供与远端操作员的连接，包括深入乌克兰境内俄军防线后方。

Musk's role as the strong-willed CEO of a company with outsized influence over US national security has at times raised questions over who is making decisions about SpaceX's satellite services.

马斯克以强势首席执行官的姿态，掌管着一家对美国国家安全影响过大的公司，这有时会让人质疑：SpaceX 的卫星服务到底由谁拍板。

The Biden administration at times relied on Musk to help implement policy. Biden officials coordinated with SpaceX to thwart US adversaries by cutting the service, and to help allies by expanding Starlink's availability.

拜登政府有时依赖马斯克协助落实政策。拜登的官员与 SpaceX 协调，通过切断服务来阻挠美国的对手，并通过扩大星链的覆盖来帮助盟友。

They added that discussions between the government and SpaceX often focused on preventing groups such as the Iranian-linked Houthis in Yemen and Hezbollah in Lebanon from using Starlink terminals.

他们还说，政府与 SpaceX 的讨论常常聚焦于阻止伊朗支持的也门胡塞武装和黎巴嫩真主党等组织使用星链终端。

In October 2023, during fighting between Israel and Hezbollah militants in southern Lebanon, intelligence officials told the White House that individuals linked to Hezbollah could have gained access to Starlink terminals.

2023 年 10 月，以色列与真主党武装在黎巴嫩南部交火期间，情报官员告知白宫，与真主党有关联的个人可能已获得星链终端的使用权限。

Officials had similar concerns about the Houthis in Yemen in 2024 during a US air campaign against the Iran-linked group. In both cases, the administration contacted SpaceX and asked it to identify the terminals and shut them off.

2024 年美国对伊朗支持的胡塞武装发动空中行动期间，官员们对也门的胡塞武装也有类似担忧。这两次，政府都联系了 SpaceX，要求其识别这些终端并将其关闭。

At least some of the terminals were deactivated.

至少部分终端已被停用。

In a 2024 submission to the US Commerce Department's Bureau of Industry and Security, SpaceX said it works closely with US government agencies to secure export authorizations where required and to safeguard technology in support of US national security objectives and foreign policy goals.

在 2024 年提交给美国商务部工业与安全局的一份材料中，SpaceX 表示，它与美国政府机构密切合作，在需要时取得出口许可，并保护技术，以支持美国国家安全目标和外交政策目标。

Soon after Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022, Biden officials pushed SpaceX to expand Starlink use for the Ukrainian military.

2022 年俄罗斯全面入侵乌克兰后不久，拜登的官员就推动 SpaceX 扩大星链在乌克兰军方的使用。

In a separate instance, the Biden administration asked SpaceX to turn on Starlink in Iran during a protest movement launched in September 2022 after the death of Mahsa Amini, an Iranian arrested for not wearing her hijab properly.

另一起事例中，拜登政府在 2022 年 9 月伊朗爆发抗议运动时要求 SpaceX 在伊朗开启星链；那场抗议的起因是玛莎·阿米尼之死——她是一名因未规范佩戴头巾而被捕的伊朗人。

Starlink terminals were moved into the country with the help of the administration.

在政府的帮助下，星链终端被运入该国。

The Trump administration took a similar step earlier this year and discussed with Musk and SpaceX getting Starlink terminals into Iran and activated following US-Israeli strikes in the country in June 2025 and again in February 2026.

特朗普政府今年早些时候采取了类似步骤，与马斯克和 SpaceX 讨论把星链终端运入伊朗并激活，背景是 2025 年 6 月和 2026 年 2 月美以对伊朗的打击。

Neither the Trump administration nor SpaceX has publicly confirmed that those terminals were turned on. The Wall Street Journal reported in February that the US government had sent thousands of Starlink terminals into Iran.

特朗普政府和 SpaceX 都未公开确认这些终端已被开启。《华尔街日报》2 月报道称，美国政府已向伊朗运送数千台星链终端。
