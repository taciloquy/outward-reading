China has captured the market for hobbyist 3D printers

The fastest-growing industrial product in China today is not the 
electric vehicle, lithium-ion battery or even humanoid robot. It 
is the humble 3D printer for hobbyists.
                             /ˈhɒbiɪsts/爱好者

machines grew by 90% in the first half of this year.
In the past decade China's global market share has risen from 
19% to 96%, ending the dominance of Western firms.
                       /ˈdɒmɪnəns/主导地位
The personal 3D-printing market is now split among the "four

Elegoo and the industry's leader, Bambu Lab, reign supreme

Part of Bambu Lab's different approach can be traced back to its 
founding team. Several of its engineers came from DJI, the drone 
manufacturer, with backgrounds in robotics and consumer 
electronics.
Drones present a similar design challenge to 3D printers: 
they're mechanically complex, but successful consumer products 
need to be usable without specialized training. Bambu Lab's 
founders had already worked through that kind of problem before 
applying it to printing.
The company also didn't rush its first product. It spent 22 
months on research and development before releasing its first 
printer, the X1, which launched on Kickstarter and raised about 
HK$55 million, or roughly $7 million, from 5,575 backers.
The core of Bambu Lab's approach is automating steps that 
previously required manual adjustment.
Its printers include cameras, LiDAR, and gravity sensors that 
handle bed leveling and calibration automatically on every 
print, without requiring the user to understand the underlying 
mechanics.
LatePost described the result as "a small AI-enabled robot." One 
Bambu Lab owner on Reddit summarized the difference simply: 
"Bambu printers just work." Price wars have eaten into the 
printer dragons' profits even as sales boom.
But the firms still excite investors. Bambu Lab was founded in 
2020 by alumni of another Chinese company, DJI, the world's 
leading dronemaker. The printer-maker is reported to have a 
private valuation of about $10bn.
