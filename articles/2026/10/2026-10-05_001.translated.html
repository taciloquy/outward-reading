Investing for babies involves knotty trade-offs

为宝宝投资，充满难解的取舍

Patience, consistency and frugality are fine habits for an investor. They are not, however, common virtues among newborn babies. The little bundles of joy are impulsive short-term thinkers.

耐心、坚持和节俭都是投资者的好习惯。然而这些美德在新生儿身上并不常见。这些小小的"欢乐包袱"是冲动的短期思考者。

They are also some of nature's biggest freeloaders, living entirely beyond their means. All the same, for new parents with a soft spot for the magic of compounding, like your columnist, a baby presents a tantalising investment opportunity.

他们也是自然界最大的"吃白食者"之一，完全过着入不敷出的生活。尽管如此，对像本专栏作者这样迷恋复利魔力的新手父母来说，一个宝宝就代表着一个诱人的投资机会。

Many decades of life and potential returns stretch out into the future: $1,000 invested today with a real annual return of 5.2% (the average for global stocks over the past 125 years) would be worth $58,000 by the time the child is 80.

几十年的寿命和潜在回报在未来铺展开来：今天投入 1000 美元，若实际年回报率为 5.2%（全球股票过去 125 年的均值），到孩子 80 岁时将价值 5.8 万美元。

Yet these juicy financial returns must be considered alongside another sort of investment: in the children themselves. Spending on a child's health, education and skills can yield even higher returns (on top of helping them lead a fulfilling life).

然而这些诱人的财务回报，必须与另一种投资放在一起考量：对孩子们自身的投资。在孩子的健康、教育和技能上花钱，能带来更高的回报（而且还能帮他们过上充实的人生）。

So how should a new parent think about their budding little portfolio? Potential financial investments are manifold. Many countries offer tax-advantaged ways for parents to save for their children.

那么新手父母该如何看待自己这个萌芽中的小投资组合？可能的金融投资五花八门。许多国家都为父母提供储蓄的税收优惠渠道。

American tykes are now eligible for Trump accounts, which start with a $1,000 freebie from the federal government that can be put into mutual funds or index trackers until the child turns 18, while taxes are deferred.

美国的娃娃们现在有资格开设"特朗普账户"（Trump accounts），联邦政府会先给 1000 美元的免费启动金，可投入共同基金或指数基金，直到孩子满 18 岁，期间税收递延。

Earlier options include the even more tax-friendly 529 accounts, but these may be used solely for specified educational purposes. In Britain, the Junior Individual Savings Account, into which up to £9,000 ($11,900) a year can be invested, is tax friendlier still.

更早的选项包括税收更优惠的 529 账户，但这些只能用于指定的教育用途。在英国，青少年个人储蓄账户（Junior ISA）每年最多可投入 9000 英镑（1.19 万美元），税收上更友好。

Investing for babies is a joy even without the tax perks. Long horizons smooth out volatility, allowing for more risk.

即便没有税收优惠，为宝宝投资本身就是一件乐事。漫长的时间跨度熨平了波动，可以承担更多风险。

Ian Ayres and Barry Nalebuff, two economists, propose that young workers should borrow to buy stocks, since the long-term returns to equities are (hopefully) higher than the interest on borrowing.

两位经济学家伊恩·艾尔斯和巴里·纳莱巴夫提出，年轻劳动者应当借钱买股票，因为股票的长期回报（希望）高于借款利息。

If the logic is true for 20-somethings, it is truer still for infants with a horizon a couple of decades longer. Some parents fear that creating a large trust fund will turn their offspring into layabouts.

如果这套逻辑对二十多岁的人成立，对时间跨度还要长几十年的婴儿就更成立了。一些父母担心，设立一大笔信托基金会把子女变成游手好闲的人。

Andrew Carnegie, a 19th-century American steel magnate, gave away most of his wealth out of fear that it would dull the work ethic of his descendants.

19 世纪美国钢铁大亨安德鲁·卡内基捐掉了大部分财富，就是担心财富会磨掉后代的职业道德。

A more recent study of Norwegian inheritances between 2000 and 2004 found that those bequeathed NKr300,000 (some $40,000 at the time) or more reduced their working hours by 7-10% in the six years that followed.

一项更近期的研究考察了 2000 至 2004 年间挪威的遗产继承，发现那些继承 30 万挪威克朗（当时约合 4 万美元）或以上的人，在之后六年里把工作时间减少了 7% 至 10%。

This makes investments in human capital more appealing. In the 1970s and 1980s Gary Becker and Nigel Tomes, another pair of economists, theorised that parents were better off investing in education and skills early on.

这让"人力资本投资"更有吸引力。1970 和 1980 年代，另一对经济学家加里·贝克尔和奈杰尔·托姆斯提出理论：父母最好在早期就投资于教育和技能。

For example, teaching a child to read is not hugely expensive, but opens up huge opportunities for future earnings. Investing in financial assets starts to make sense later, once those human-capital investments encounter diminishing returns.

例如，教孩子读书成本不算高，却能为未来的收入打开巨大空间。等到这些人力资本投资遭遇边际收益递减之后，投资金融资产才开始讲得通。

A wealth of empirical evidence has since backed up the theory. Beyond basic skills, however, investing in human capital gets fraught. Such investments are sunk costs that cannot be liquidated.

此后大量实证证据支持了这一理论。然而在基本技能之外，投资人力资本就变得棘手了。这类投资是沉没成本，无法变现。

They are also infungible: tutoring in advanced maths is not the same as Chinese lessons. Children react differently (and unpredictably) to each. The extraordinarily long timeframes that make investing for babies attractive also raise uncertainty.

它们还不可互换：高等数学的家教和中文课可不是一回事。孩子对它们的反应不同（且不可预测）。让"为宝宝投资"显得诱人的超长时间跨度，同时也增加了不确定性。

Perhaps the skills that are valuable today will be obsolete in an AI future? So what is a new parent to do? The most obvious financial options involve accepting free money.

也许今天有价值的技能，在 AI 的未来会被淘汰？那么新手父母该怎么办？最显眼的财务选项包括：把免费的钱收下。

The $1,000 in a Trump account is a no-brainer. Even rabid anti-Trumpers would be mad not to take it. All parents should also accept similar grants from several American states.

特朗普账户里那 1000 美元是不用想的。即便是狂热的反特朗普者，不拿也是疯了。所有父母也应当接受美国几个州提供的类似补助。

Those who expect to be on the hook for university tuition would be wise to take advantage of 529 accounts or similar vehicles as early as possible, so that compounding can work its magic to the full.

那些预计将来要承担大学学费的人，明智的做法是尽早利用 529 账户或类似工具，好让复利充分发挥魔力。

Any money that children are likely eventually to inherit is better off in tax-friendly shelters than in their parents' brokerage accounts. Investments can misfire: violin lessons may generate little more than screeching and even a modest trust fund could encourage laziness.

孩子最终可能继承的任何钱，放在税收友好的"避税壳"里，都比放在父母的券商账户里更划算。投资也可能失手：小提琴课说不定只换来刺耳的吱吱声，而即便一笔不大的信托基金也可能助长懒惰。

But with a bit of luck, investing in a child's future and on their behalf will pay off handsomely.

但运气好的话，为孩子的未来投资、并以他们的名义投资，会得到丰厚的回报。

Fortunate parents with the ability to do both should approach this like the rest of parenting: try different things and don't agonise too much over your decisions.

有能力两者兼顾的幸运父母，应当像对待育儿的其他方面一样对待这件事：多试几种做法，别在决定上过度纠结。
