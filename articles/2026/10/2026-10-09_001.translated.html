Inside the Nobel Prize in literature

诺贝尔文学奖内幕

Alfred Nobel was a keen but mediocre writer. The Swedish dynamite tycoon wrote unpublished poems and a play featuring a 40-minute torture scene.

阿尔弗雷德·诺贝尔是个热切但平庸的写作者。这位瑞典炸药大亨写过未曾发表的诗，以及一出含有 40 分钟酷刑场景的戏剧。

To the dismay of relatives keen to inherit his fortune, his handwritten will of 1895 endowed five awards, including a prize for literature.

令那些一心想继承他财产的亲属沮丧的是，他 1895 年的手写遗嘱设立了五个奖项，其中包括文学奖。

Nobel instructed "the Academy in Stockholm" to give it to the writer of "the most outstanding work in an ideal [idealisk] direction". The Swedish Academy spent decades puzzling over what "ideal" meant, before quietly dropping the criterion.

诺贝尔指示"斯德哥尔摩的学院"把文学奖颁给"在理想 [idealisk] 方向上最杰出作品"的作者。瑞典学院花了数十年琢磨"理想"到底是什么意思，之后悄悄放弃了这条标准。

Next Thursday, a door will open in a gilded hall in Stockholm and the Academy's formally dressed permanent secretary will step forward to announce, in Swedish and English, the winner of this year's literature Nobel.

下周四，斯德哥尔摩一间金碧辉煌的大厅里会打开一扇门，学院那位身着正装的常务秘书将走上前，用瑞典语和英语宣布今年诺贝尔文学奖得主。

Now prediction markets favour Indian writer Amitav Ghosh, the Chinese avant-garde author Can Xue or the American novelist Thomas Pynchon to win the SKr12mn (£910,000) in prize money — though nobody really knows, as today's Academy is less leaky than it was before a #MeToo scandal prompted a sweeping overhaul.

如今预测市场看好印度作家阿米塔夫·戈什、中国先锋派作家残雪或美国小说家托马斯·品钦赢得 1200 万瑞典克朗（91 万英镑）的奖金——不过没人真的知道，因为在一场 #MeToo 风波引发全面整改之后，如今的学院已不像过去那样漏风声。

And the pundits usually guess wrong. The prize is ritually mocked. The Nigerian poet Chinweizu Ibekwe once marvelled at "the conceit that a gaggle of Swedes... should pronounce on intellectual excellence for the whole wide world".

而专家们的猜测通常是错的。这个奖照例被嘲笑。尼日利亚诗人钦韦祖·伊贝奎曾惊叹于"一帮瑞典人……竟要对全世界的智识卓越指手画脚，这是何等的自负"。

And yet, says Daniel Medin, professor of comparative literature at the American University of Paris: "The Nobel is the big one for international fiction. There is no comparison."

然而，巴黎美国大学的比较文学教授丹尼尔·梅丁说："对国际小说而言，诺贝尔奖是最大的那个。无可比拟。"

The Nobel shapes the global canon — even if the globe can push back. There has always been a comic discrepancy between the weight of the prize and the parochialism of the judges.

诺贝尔奖塑造着全球的经典谱系——即便世界也会反推回来。这份奖的分量与评委的眼界狭隘之间，始终存在一种滑稽的落差。

The Academy's 18 life members — known as "The Eighteen" — are Swedish writers, literary historians, linguists and a jurist, some of them obscure even in Sweden. There were barely any female members until this century.

学院的 18 位终身院士——人称"十八院士"——是瑞典作家、文学史家、语言学家和一位法学家，其中有些人即便在瑞典也默默无闻。直到本世纪之前，女性院士寥寥无几。

The very first award, in 1901, made international news. Somehow nobody nominated Leo Tolstoy, whereas 17 members of the Academie Francaise endorsed the French poet Sully Prudhomme, and he won.

1901 年的首个奖项成为国际新闻。不知怎的没人提名列夫·托尔斯泰；反倒有 17 位法兰西学术院的院士支持法国诗人苏利·普吕多姆，而他获奖了。

The literature prize has never been as controversial as the frankly political Peace Prize; but because greatness in literature is more subjective than in the sciences, the Academy's choice reliably angers critics.

文学奖从未像坦率政治化的和平奖那样充满争议；但因为文学上的伟大比科学上的伟大更为主观，学院的选择总能可靠地激怒批评者。

They delight in drawing up lists of overlooked greats — James Joyce, Franz Kafka, Virginia Woolf, Marcel Proust, Constantine Cavafy, et cetera — though some of these achieved renown only after death.

他们乐于列出被埋没的大师名单——詹姆斯·乔伊斯、弗兰茨·卡夫卡、弗吉尼亚·伍尔夫、马塞尔·普鲁斯特、康斯坦丁·卡瓦菲斯等等——尽管其中有些人是死后才获得声名的。

The prize was at its most politicised during the cold war, when the Academy chose the unreliable Soviet writer Boris Pasternak (1958) and dissidents Aleksandr Solzhenitsyn (1970) and Joseph Brodsky (1987).

这个奖在冷战期间最为政治化：学院选择了立场摇摆的苏联作家鲍里斯·帕斯捷尔纳克（1958 年），以及异见人士亚历山大·索尔仁尼琴（1970 年）和约瑟夫·布罗茨基（1987 年）。

Pasternak initially accepted the nobelevka, as the award is popularly known in Russia — but then, under pressure from the regime, rejected "this undeserved prize which has been presented to me".

帕斯捷尔纳克起初接受了"诺别列夫卡"（俄语中对这个奖的俗称）——但后来在政权压力下，拒绝了"这个颁给我的、我不配得的奖"。

Jean-Paul Sartre, who refused the Nobel in 1964, noted among his reasons that the prize was "reserved for the writers of the West or the rebels of the East." By the 1980s, the Academy — earlier than most of the Western literary establishment — began looking beyond the west.

1964 年拒绝诺贝尔奖的让-保罗·萨特在理由中提到，这个奖"是留给西方作家或东方叛逆者的"。到 1980 年代，学院——比西方文学界的大多数机构都更早——开始把目光投向西方之外。

A "Nobel committee" of four or five academicians whittles down the nominations to a shortlist of five, which the Eighteen spend the summer reading. They decide in early October. Translation has become central to the award.

一个由四五名院士组成的"诺贝尔委员会"把提名缩减到五人的决选名单，"十八院士"用整个夏天来阅读。他们在 10 月初作出决定。翻译已成为这个奖的核心。

The Nobel has helped make the international canon more inclusive. After going global, the prize has recently become gender-equal. This followed a #MeToo scandal in 2017, when 18 women accused the French husband of Academician Katarina Frostenson of sexual assault and rape.

诺贝尔奖帮助国际经典谱系变得更具包容性。在走向全球之后，这个奖近年已变得性别均衡。这发生在 2017 年的一场 #MeToo 风波之后——当时 18 名女性指控院士卡塔琳娜·弗罗斯滕松的法国籍丈夫性侵和强奸。

He was later jailed. Several members quit — although the body's rules forbid resignation — and the post-scandal "Eighteen" included many more women.

他后来入狱。数名院士辞职——尽管该机构的规定禁止辞职——风波之后的"十八院士"中女性多了不少。

They awarded the 2018 prize, a year late, to Polish writer Olga Tokarczuk, and there have been three female laureates since 2018.

他们把 2018 年的奖（推迟了一年）颁给了波兰作家奥尔加·托卡尔丘克，自 2018 年以来已有三位女性得主。

In fact, notes Kaj Schueler, author of a Swedish book on the prize, there's been a precise pattern: male and female victories alternate.

事实上，写过一本关于该奖的瑞典著作的卡伊·舒勒指出，这里存在一个精确的模式：男女得主交替出现。

If that continues, then after Krasznahorkai's win in 2025, this year's laureate would be a woman, perhaps it will be Xue's year? The winners' reactions are part of the award's lore.

如果这一模式延续，那么在克拉斯诺霍尔卡伊 2025 年获奖之后，今年的得主会是一位女性——也许今年会是残雪之年？得主们的反应是这个奖掌故的一部分。

John Steinbeck in 1962, when asked whether he deserved the Nobel, replied, "Frankly, no." Many critics agreed.

1962 年，约翰·斯坦贝克被问到是否配得上诺贝尔奖时回答："坦白说，不配。"许多批评者表示同意。

Samuel Beckett (1969) was on holiday in Tunisia with his wife Suzanne when a telegram from his French publisher arrived: "In spite of everything, they have given you the Nobel Prize. I advise you to go into hiding."

1969 年，塞缪尔·贝克特正与妻子苏珊在突尼斯度假，法国出版商发来一封电报："尽管如此，他们还是把诺贝尔奖给了你。我建议你躲起来。"

Suzanne, when told the news, exclaimed, "Quelle catastrophe." Beckett dubbed December 10, the annual date of the award ceremony, "Nobloodybeldamday".

苏珊得知消息时惊呼："真是一场灾难。"贝克特把每年颁奖典礼的日期 12 月 10 日称为"该死的诺贝尔日"。

Bob Dylan, winner in 2016, ignored the Academy's phone calls and didn't acknowledge the prize for over a fortnight.

2016 年的得主鲍勃·迪伦对学院的电话置之不理，而且两个多星期都没有对这个奖作出回应。
