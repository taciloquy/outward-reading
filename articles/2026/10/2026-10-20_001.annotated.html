On Saturday, 800,000 people took to the streets according to

organizers, while Interior Minister Laurent Nuñez put the number

The same pattern played out in many cities: On one side, a crowd

determined to clash with the police.

The unrest also revived memories of the Yellow Vest movement, 
    /ʌnˈrest/骚乱/rɪˈvaɪvd/唤起

                       /rɪˈbeljən/反抗           /ˌɪnɪˈkwɒləti/不平等

Behind these different grievances lay a deeper frustration: many

Students, workers and Yellow Vest veterans saw an opportunity to

Responding to a call to action that notably cited the rising

capitalism, the bourgeoisie, the police and the cost of living.
Everyone in his entourage chimed in relentlessly, listing their

In Paris, the situation seemed to at least partially slip out of

Place de la République, the starting point for the official

Some violent and organized protesters began by attacking street

While it might not exactly have been the much-heralded

On one side, large groups of demonstrators marched in dozens of

determined to clash with the police.
In the streets, groups of university and high school students, 
toughened up by weeks of protest, mingled with small groups that

Protesters from working-class neighborhoods were there, as well

All the opulence of Paris's wealthy districts was suddenly

"These neighborhoods are rich here. It's all from the profits
