Are 60-Second Microdramas the Future of Hollywood, or the End?

60 秒微短剧，是好莱坞的未来还是终结？

In 2020, the entertainment mogul Jeffrey Katzenberg brought to life an idea that must have seemed — in the flash of its conception — foolproof: Netflix, but for people addicted to their phones.

2020 年，娱乐业大亨杰弗里·卡岑伯格把一个想法变成了现实——在它被构思出来的那一刻，这个主意想必看起来万无一失：给手机上瘾者的 Netflix。

He called it Quibi: short for "quick bites." It collapsed after eight months in a total void of public interest. And yet, the joke may very well be on us.

他把它叫 Quibi，是"quick bites"（小快餐）的缩写。它在公众完全无感的真空中撑了八个月就垮了。然而，笑话很可能最后落在我们身上。

Because, just six years later, short-form, mobile-first narrative entertainment is the fastest-growing form of streaming worldwide, per Bloomberg. Microdramas, sometimes called "verticals," are TV shows for your phone.

因为仅仅六年后，据彭博社数据，短篇幅、手机优先的叙事娱乐已成为全球增长最快的流媒体形式。微短剧——有时也叫"竖屏剧"——是给你手机看的电视剧。

They are typically described as soap operas for Gen Z: hypersexual, over the top and — most important — short, with episodes rarely exceeding two minutes in length.

它们通常被描述为"给 Z 世代的肥皂剧"：充满性暗示、极度夸张，而最重要的是——短，单集长度很少超过两分钟。

The format was born around 2018 in China, where its revenue now exceeds that of the conventional film industry.

这种形式大约在 2018 年诞生于中国，如今它在中国的收入已超过传统电影产业。

As I write this, the form is on track to take in $1.5 billion in revenue in the United States by the end of the year.

在我写下这些文字时，这一形式在美国有望在年底前实现 15 亿美元的收入。

Each short episode opens with a hook and ends with a twist, dragging viewers, like recalcitrant pets, through entire seasons roughly the length of a feature film.

每一集短剧都以钩子开场、以反转收尾，像拖着不听话的宠物一样把观众拽过整整几季——而总时长大致相当于一部电影。

In the first years of this decade, a number of Chinese companies began to export the form abroad, creating countless U.S. microdrama apps and coordinating U.S.-based microdrama shoots in order to supply those apps with content.

本世纪头几年，一批中国公司开始把这种形式输出海外，做出了无数美国的微短剧 App，并在美国本土组织微短剧拍摄，以便为这些 App 供应内容。

These companies still retain a near monopoly on U.S. production, but that is quickly changing. Hollywood executives — noticing these apps outranking their own streamers on the charts — have started to spin up microdrama platforms of their own.

这些公司至今仍近乎垄断着美国的生产，但这一点正在迅速改变。好莱坞的高管们——注意到这些 App 在排行榜上超过了自家的流媒体——已开始搭建自己的微短剧平台。

I'd managed to wrangle three days of set visits on a show called "Bound by Love," a production of ReelShort.

我设法争取到三天去一部叫《Bound by Love》（《为爱所困》）的剧组探班，这是 ReelShort 的制作。

Culturally, ReelShort is an interesting case: the flagship brand of an American company originally financed — and still partly owned — by a Chinese digital content group.

从文化上看，ReelShort 是个有趣的案例：一家美国公司的旗舰品牌，最初由一家中国数字内容集团出资——且至今仍部分持有。

The 9:16 format that fills a vertical phone screen leaves little room to maneuver, requiring actors to stand unnaturally close together.

填满竖屏手机的 9:16 画幅几乎没有腾挪空间，要求演员们不自然地紧挨着站立。

"Bound by Love," which in its finished form would run more than two and a half hours, was to be shot at a variety of locations over the course of just 11 days.

《Bound by Love》成片时长将超过两个半小时，却要在 11 天里于多个地点拍完。

The key grip had an explanation for this: The scenes they were shooting that day were for an early episode, one that would appear before the viewer hit the paywall.

首席器械师对此有个解释：他们当天拍的场景属于靠前的剧集，是观众撞上付费墙之前会看到的。

As at least a dozen people would subsequently inform me, the platforms are not overly invested in the quality of the episodes that come after — i.e., the episodes a viewer has actually paid to see.

正如随后至少有十几个人告诉我的，这些平台并不太在意付费之后那些集的质量——也就是观众真正掏钱看的那些集。

The goal is to keep them up, which is distinct from telling a good or even coherent story.

目标是把他们留住，这与讲一个好故事、甚至一个连贯的故事是两回事。

What is clear with hindsight is that Quibi failed primarily because it was TV — regular old TV — that happened to be on your phone. The intervening years have taught us that what tends to succeed on phones is phone stuff.

事后看得清楚的是：Quibi 失败主要是因为它就是电视——老掉牙的普通电视——只不过碰巧在你的手机上。这中间几年教会我们：在手机上往往能成功的，是"手机的东西"。

This is the terrain that ReelShort and its peers have precision-engineered their content to compete on. Fox Entertainment has made an equity investment in the microdrama platform Holywater.

这正是 ReelShort 及其同行精心设计内容、准备在上面一较高下的地盘。福克斯娱乐已对微短剧平台 Holywater 进行了股权投资。

Disney+ has begun to produce microdramas of its own, and Paramount+ has a slate scheduled for the fall. Today microdramas are — as more than one person put it to me — the only game in town.

Disney+ 已开始自制微短剧，Paramount+ 也有一批排在今年秋季。今天，微短剧——不止一个人这么跟我说——是"城里唯一的游戏"。

"I was fighting it for a long time, but Old Hollywood is dead." Microdramas, she went on, are commercialized, rule-bound, utterly devoid of artistry.

"我抗拒了很久，但旧好莱坞已经死了。"她接着说，微短剧是商业化的、受规则束缚的、完全谈不上艺术性的。

"And this can't be the end of it for me, so I'm joining the bandwagon." The male leads I spoke to, specifically, seemed uniformly thrilled by this professional windfall. It's different for the women.

"而对我来说这不可能就是终点，所以我加入了这股浪潮。"与我交谈的男主角们，具体而言，似乎一致为这波职业上的横财兴奋。女性这边情况不同。

Apparently, China's short-form video platforms had recently seen a milestone: more than $12 million spent in one day on advertisements for A.I.-generated microdramas. The country's microdrama production hubs, meanwhile — sites of industrial-scale production only months earlier — were emptying out.

据说，中国的短视频平台最近见证了一个里程碑：一天之内为 AI 生成的微短剧投放了超过 1200 万美元的广告。与此同时，中国的微短剧制作基地——几个月前还是工业化规模的生产现场——正在清空。

Actors who were in demand last year, stars of the form, were doing food delivery just to survive.

去年还炙手可热的演员、这一形式的明星，如今靠送外卖维生。

Gelb was seeing the writing on the wall: "Experience shows that, in the microdrama world, what happens in China does not stay in China."

格尔布看到了墙上的字："经验表明，在微短剧的世界里，中国发生的事不会只留在中国。"

It's a preview for what's to come for the rest of the world. " Soon after I returned, it emerged that an average of 470 A.I.-generated microdramas were released on Chinese platforms each day this past January.

这是对世界其他地方即将到来的事情的预演。"我回来后不久，有消息称：今年 1 月，中国平台平均每天发布 470 部 AI 生成的微短剧。

In the same period, COL Group — a part-owner of ReelShort's parent company — said that it planned to replace 75 percent of its actors with A.I. for titles released in China.

同一时期，ReelShort 母公司部分持股的 COL 集团表示，计划在中国发行的作品中，用 AI 替换掉 75% 的演员。

I spoke to an executive at COL, a man named Timothy Oh. He seemed amused, if puzzled, by the continued American resistance to A.I.-generated content. "The reality is, content is content," he told me.

我与 COL 的一位高管、一个叫蒂莫西·吴的人交谈过。对于美国人对 AI 生成内容持续的抵触，他似乎觉得好笑，也有些困惑。"现实是，内容就是内容，"他告诉我。

"People are already watching A.I. slop on Instagram." As I write this, A.I. microdramas are all over the ReelShort homepage, and make up a large percentage of their most-watched charts.

"人们已经在 Instagram 上看 AI 的'泔水'了。"在我写下这些文字时，AI 微短剧已布满 ReelShort 的首页，并在其观看量最高的榜单中占相当大的比例。
