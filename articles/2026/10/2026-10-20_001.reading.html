October 17 French protests: Crowds marched through the streets before violence, looting and chaos broke out
法国爆发近年来最严重的街头骚乱震惊世界！为什么法国人总在抗议？

Paragraph 1
On Saturday, 800,000 people took to the streets according to organizers, while Interior Minister Laurent Nuñez put the number at 550,000. The same pattern played out in many cities: On one side, a crowd of peaceful protesters; on the other, independent groups determined to clash with the police. While turnout across the country was massive, the protests were also marred by unprecedented property damage, violence and looting. In France, several worlds seemed to have collided over one long day.
周六，据组织者统计，约有80万人走上街头，而法国内政部长洛朗·努涅斯则将人数估计为55万。许多城市都上演了相同的场景：一边是和平示威的人群，另一边则是决心与警方发生冲突的独立团体。尽管全国各地的抗议活动参与人数众多，但这些示威也因前所未有的财产破坏、暴力行为和抢劫事件而蒙上阴影。在法国，仿佛几个截然不同的世界，在这漫长的一天里迎面碰撞。

Paragraph 2
The roots of the protests stretched back to September, when calls for demonstrations against rising fuel prices began circulating on social media. Meanwhile, high school students were protesting teacher shortages, overcrowded classrooms and poor school conditions. The unrest also revived memories of the Yellow Vest movement, which began in 2018 as a protest against fuel taxes before growing into a broader rebellion against economic inequality and President Emmanuel Macron’s government. Behind these different grievances lay a deeper frustration: many French people felt squeezed by rising living costs, disappointed by public services and increasingly distrustful of political leaders. Students, workers and Yellow Vest veterans saw an opportunity to unite their separate struggles.
这场抗议活动的根源可以追溯到9月，当时，社交媒体上开始流传反对燃油价格上涨的示威号召。与此同时，高中生也在抗议教师短缺、教室过度拥挤以及学校条件恶劣等问题。这场社会动荡还唤起了人们对“黄背心运动”的记忆。该运动始于2018年，最初是为了抗议燃油税，随后逐渐演变为一场范围更广、反对经济不平等和埃马纽埃尔·马克龙政府的反抗运动。在这些不同的不满诉求背后，隐藏着一种更深层次的挫败感：许多法国人感到生活成本不断上涨，令自己不堪重负；他们对公共服务感到失望，也越来越不信任政治领导人。学生、工人以及曾经参加“黄背心运动”的老成员们，看到了将各自的抗争联合起来的机会。

At exactly 10 am, outside Gare Saint-Lazare train station, one Yellow Vest summed up his twofold ambition for the day: “First, the revolution, but also to sleep in Macron’s bed.” He said this with a mixture of humor and seriousness, going on to denounce capitalism, the bourgeoisie, the police and the cost of living. Everyone in his entourage chimed in relentlessly, listing their demands, frustrations and anger, until their conversation was abruptly cut short when a BRAV-M motorcycle police unit suddenly barged in and descended on the group.
上午10点整，在圣拉扎尔火车站外，一名“黄背心”抗议者概括了自己当天的双重目标：“首先是革命，不过我还想睡到马克龙的床上去。”他说这番话时，语气中既带着幽默，又透着认真。随后，他开始抨击资本主义、资产阶级、警察以及高昂的生活成本。周围的同伴也接连加入讨论，滔滔不绝地列举各自的诉求、不满和愤怒。直到一支BRAV-M摩托车警察部队突然闯入，向这群人逼近，他们的谈话才戛然而止。
