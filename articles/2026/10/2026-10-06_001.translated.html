The AI industry is booming. Women are getting left behind

AI 行业在繁荣，女性却被甩在后面

There's a common fear among people who work in Silicon Valley: snag one of the fast-growing, high-paying jobs in artificial intelligence, or get trapped in the "permanent underclass", a phrase describing the fate of those who won't have upward mobility in the age of AI.

在硅谷工作的人普遍有一种恐惧：要么抓住人工智能领域那批增长快、薪水高的工作，要么就困进"永久下层阶级"——这个说法描述的是那些在 AI 时代失去向上流动机会的人的命运。

The phrase usually describes a future in which AI automates human jobs. But for women in tech, it's starting to look like the present.

这个词组通常用来描述 AI 自动化掉人类工作的未来。但对科技行业的女性来说，它已经开始像是当下。

"A lot of people say: 'Oh, AI is lowering barriers, it's equalizing the playing field for women," said Urvashi Batra, co-founder and CEO of Prioriwise, an AI platform for IT service providers.

"很多人说：'哦，AI 在降低门槛，它在为女性拉平竞争环境，'"面向 IT 服务商的 AI 平台 Prioriwise 联合创始人兼 CEO 乌尔瓦希·巴特拉说。

"I actually think it's the opposite." Batra said people take her less seriously as the founder of an AI company than her male co-founder.

"我其实认为恰恰相反。"巴特拉说，作为一家 AI 公司的创始人，人们对待她不如对待她的男性联合创始人那么认真。

When pitching investors, she and her co-founder have learned that they are more likely to get an investment if he does the pitching.

在向投资人推介时，她和她的联合创始人发现：如果由他来推介，更有可能拿到投资。

Women made up only about a quarter of new hires in AI roles in the last year, compared to 50% of new hires in non-AI roles, according to a recent report from LinkedIn.

根据领英（LinkedIn）最近的一份报告，过去一年女性只占 AI 岗位新员工的约四分之一，而在非 AI 岗位中这一比例为 50%。

In executive roles, that number drops to just 13%.

在高管岗位上，这个数字降到只有 13%。

At the same time, LinkedIn's research shows that women are more likely to work in roles with high exposure to AI disruption, like customer service, meaning they are also at higher risk for job loss due to AI.

与此同时，领英的研究显示，女性更可能从事那些高度暴露于 AI 颠覆风险的岗位，比如客服——这意味着她们因 AI 而失业的风险也更高。

AI jobs, whose postings have doubled since 2023, come with a salary premium, on average paying more than twice as much as roles that don't involve working with AI.

AI 岗位的招聘信息自 2023 年以来翻了一倍，还带有薪资溢价，平均薪酬是不涉及 AI 的岗位的两倍多。

But women who do have jobs in AI are disproportionately concentrated in low-paying roles, like data annotators. Across all AI occupations, men have $45,000 higher median pay than women, partly due to the types of jobs they are likelier to have.

但那些确实在 AI 行业工作的女性，却不成比例地集中在低薪岗位上，比如数据标注员。在所有 AI 职业中，男性的薪酬中位数比女性高 4.5 万美元，部分原因在于他们更可能从事的岗位类型。

The ongoing trend could produce the greatest gender pay gap in generations and leave women out of building a technology that shaped the economy.

这一持续的趋势，可能造成几代人以来最大的性别薪酬差距，并让女性缺席一项塑造了经济的技术。

"What we're witnessing is a sort of backsliding," said Brenda Darden Wilkerson, president of AnitaB.org, a non-profit devoted to advancing women in tech jobs.

"我们正在目睹某种倒退，"致力于推动女性从事科技工作的非营利组织 AnitaB.org 主席布伦达·达登·威尔克森说。

Initiatives to promote women in the tech industry have been ongoing for years, with mixed results. Women now hold about one-third of tech jobs in the US.

推动女性进入科技行业的行动已持续多年，效果参差不齐。如今女性在美国科技岗位中约占三分之一。

But research has found that women leave the tech industry at a much higher rate than their male counterparts, citing factors like non-inclusive cultures and barriers to advancement. Women working in AI may face even greater challenges.

但研究发现，女性离开科技行业的比例远高于男性同行，原因包括不够包容的文化和晋升障碍。从事 AI 工作的女性可能面临更大的挑战。

"AI moves extremely fast, and the pressure to keep up is immense," said Jayeeta Putatunda, an AI engineering lead at investment firm Turing.

"AI 发展极快，跟上的压力巨大，"投资公司 Turing 的 AI 工程负责人贾伊塔·普塔通达说。

"I have seen throughout my career so many amazing women I know kind of fall through the cracks." Putatunda said it's not uncommon to be the only female engineer on a team, and that when women are outnumbered, they have to speak up louder to make their ideas known.

"在我的职业生涯中，我见过太多我认识的优秀女性就这样掉队了。"普塔通达说，在一个团队里成为唯一的女工程师并不罕见；而当女性人数占少数时，她们必须更大声地表达，才能让自己的想法被听见。

Mentorship in the AI field, especially by other women, can be hard to come by. And the breakneck pace of AI advancements means anyone working in the field should expect to put in long hours in order to keep up.

AI 领域的导师指导——尤其是来自其他女性的——可能很难获得。而 AI 进展的惊人速度意味着，任何在这个领域工作的人都应该做好投入长时间工作的准备。

Those kinds of work expectations can make it impossible for everyone to stay in the field.

这类工作预期会让所有人都难以留在行业内。

"I went on maternity leave for four months, and by the time I came back, there were completely different frameworks and levels of models," Putatunda said. Getting caught back up was overwhelming.

"我休了四个月产假，等我回来时，框架和模型层级已经完全不同了，"普塔通达说。重新跟上进度让人不堪重负。

She said it was only possible with the help of supportive co-workers and a husband who equitably split childcare. The AI industry is so new and fast-moving that it should in theory be more meritocratic.

她说，这只有在 supportive 同事和平等分担育儿任务的丈夫的帮助下才可能做到。AI 行业如此新、变化如此快，理论上应当更加任人唯贤。

No one has a decade of experience working with a model developed one year ago, which should mean everyone has a level playing field.

没人能在一年前开发的模型上拥有十年经验，这本该意味着每个人都有平等的起跑线。

But instead the field has indexed more on personal networks and other loose signals, according to some women who work in AI.

但据一些在 AI 领域工作的女性说，这个行业反而更多地依赖人脉圈和其他松散的信号。

The political climate has made it more challenging to get AI companies to support initiatives focused on women, said Felicia Newhouse, founder of AI Powered Women, an organization that promotes women's participation and leadership in AI.

AI Powered Women（一个促进女性参与和领导 AI 的组织）创始人费利西亚·纽豪斯说，政治气候让推动 AI 公司支持女性相关倡议变得更加困难。

Newhouse, a product marketer who has worked in technology companies for two decades, points to the Department of Justice targeting corporations with DEI programs, claiming the initiatives violate anti-discrimination laws.

在科技公司工作了二十年的产品营销人纽豪斯指出，司法部正把设有 DEI（多元、公平、包容）项目的企业列为目标，声称这些举措违反反歧视法。

Still, she said the risk that women are left behind in the AI workforce was something that kept her up at night. "We're talking about who captures a major new source of economic mobility," she said.

尽管如此，她说女性在 AI 劳动力中被甩下的风险，是让她夜不能寐的事。"我们在谈论的是谁将抓住一个重要的新增经济流动来源，"她说。

"The deepest risk is that a participation gap becomes a power gap."

"最深层的风险是：参与度的差距会演变成权力的差距。"
