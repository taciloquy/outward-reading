Musk's Promise to Give SpaceX Stock to Kids Is No Gift

马斯克要给每个孩子发一股 SpaceX：这可不是礼物

One of the great joys, but also miseries, of following business news over the past decade or so has been riding the dodgy roller coaster of the Elon Musk experience.

过去十来年关注商业新闻，最大的乐事之一——也是苦事之一——就是坐马斯克这趟颠簸的过山车。

He has gone from a guy people considered a real-life Tony Stark to something more like a modern-day Henry Ford, in the worst possible way, with countless gut-wrenching twists in between.

他从一个人人眼中的"现实版钢铁侠"，变成了更像是当代亨利·福特的那种角色——而且是以最糟糕的方式，中间还有无数令人肝肠寸断的转折。

At no point, however, did anybody think: "Hey, you know who's missing out on this? Children." Until today.

但从来没有人想过："嘿，你知道谁被漏掉了吗？孩子。"直到今天。

SpaceX Chief Executive Officer Elon Musk pledged on Monday to donate one share of his company to each of America's 72 million children, vowing it would be the first such gift "of many," if "you had just this morning traveled here from another planet, then you might consider this a reasonably generous gesture from a near-trillionaire."

SpaceX 首席执行官埃隆·马斯克周一承诺，向美国 7200 万名儿童每人捐赠一股公司股票，并宣称这将是"众多"此类馈赠中的第一笔。"如果你今天早上刚从另一个星球旅行到这里，你也许会觉得这是一位准万亿富翁相当慷慨的举动。"

The stock has been a bit stagnant since its June IPO, sure, but the company has billions of dollars in US government contracts, and Musk has promised its revenue will soar from less than $19 billion last year to $1 trillion by 2030.

这只股票自 6 月 IPO 以来确实有点停滞，但公司握有数十亿美元的美国政府合同，而马斯克承诺其营收将从去年的不到 190 亿美元，飙升到 2030 年的 1 万亿美元。

Such a gift would barely trouble Musk's vast wealth and power. It would cost about $10.5 billion, shaving Musk's net worth to $889.5 billion.

这样一份礼物几乎动摇不了马斯克庞大的财富与权力。它大约要花 105 亿美元，把马斯克的净资产削到 8895 亿美元。

And as my Bloomberg Opinion colleague Liam Denning points out, it would cut his SpaceX voting power, currently at 82% because of the stock's dual-class nature, by just 0.1 percentage points.

正如我在彭博观点（Bloomberg Opinion）的同事利亚姆·丹宁指出的，由于股票采用双重股权结构，他在 SpaceX 的投票权目前达 82%，而这份礼物只会让这个比例下降 0.1 个百分点。

But even a passing familiarity with Musk's whole vibe should raise many immediate concerns about this "promise." For one thing, it was made on his social media app, X, formerly known as Twitter, on which Musk has promised many things, a large percentage of which have never happened.

但只要对马斯克的整体风格稍有了解，就该对这个"承诺"立刻生出许多疑虑。首先，它是在他的社交媒体应用 X（原名推特）上做出的——马斯克在那上面承诺过很多事，其中很大一部分从未发生。

Most famous was his 2018 declaration that he had "funding secured" to take Tesla Inc. private at $420 a share. It got him in serious legal trouble when it turned out not to be true.

最著名的就是他 2018 年宣称"资金已落实"，要以每股 420 美元将特斯拉私有化。当事实证明并非如此时，他惹上了严重的法律麻烦。

If you have several hours to kill, you can peruse this website for a long list of other unfulfilled Musk promises, including a "cross country summon" feature that would let your Tesla travel the continent by itself, incredible-Journey style to find you; half-hour rocket trips from New York to London; 1 million robotaxis; and seafaring Teslas, to name a few.

如果你有几个小时可以消磨，可以去这个网站浏览一长串其他未兑现的马斯克承诺，包括一项"跨洲召唤"功能——让你的特斯拉独自穿越大陆、《神奇旅程》式地找到你；从纽约到伦敦半小时的火箭旅行；100 万辆无人驾驶出租车；还有能下海航行的特斯拉，仅举几例。

Buying stock in Musk is less an investment than it is subscribing to a streaming service for increasingly ridiculous science fiction. Also, it doesn't seem as if Musk spent long hours conferring with lawyers and accountants planning for this gift.

买马斯克的股票，与其说是投资，不如说是订阅了一个越来越离谱的科幻流媒体服务。而且，马斯克似乎并没有花很长时间与律师和会计师商议、为这份礼物做规划。

Rather, the timing and Musk's track record suggest he saw SpaceX's AI rival, Anthropic PBC, making its own charitable pledge on Sunday and felt the urge to one-up it.

相反，从时机和他的过往记录看，更像是他看到 SpaceX 的 AI 竞争对手 Anthropic 周日做出了自己的慈善承诺，于是产生了好胜心，想压过它一头。

The maker of Claude vowed to donate 5% of its shares, potentially worth $75 billion (if the company is worth $1.5 trillion), to a fund that will benefit the "public good," including AI's destruction of jobs that kids want.

这家 Claude 的开发商誓言捐出 5% 的股份，价值可能高达 750 亿美元（如果公司估值 1.5 万亿美元），投入一个造福"公共利益"的基金，其中也包括应对 AI 对孩子们想要的那些工作岗位的摧毁。

Musk replied to Anthropic's X post making this announcement with a curt "I recommend giving the shares directly to the public," suggesting he's just better at doing charity. His detail-free promise of one share per child followed hours later.

马斯克在 Anthropic 发布这一公告的 X 帖下简短生硬地回复："我建议直接把股票给公众。"言下之意是他做慈善更在行。几个小时后，他那个没有任何细节的"每个孩子一股"的承诺就来了。

How will children receive these shares? Nobody knows, and the odds are very good that nobody will ever have to find out.

孩子们将如何拿到这些股票？没人知道，而且很有可能永远没人需要知道。

Anthropic's offer seems to be an olive branch to AI's large and growing contingent of haters and skeptics, including most of the American public, ahead of its IPO.

在 IPO 之前，Anthropic 的提议看起来像是向 AI 日益庞大、不断增长的憎恶者与怀疑者群体（包括大多数美国公众）递出的一根橄榄枝。

Maybe it will also deflect some of the pressure to impose actual government regulation of the technology. Musk could use some good publicity, too, and not just because SpaceX is a growing AI giant.

也许这还能转移一部分要求政府对这项技术真正加以监管的压力。马斯克也需要一些正面宣传，而且不只是因为 SpaceX 正成长为 AI 巨头。

He has embraced White nationalism and let SpaceX's Starlink service undermine Ukraine's effort to defend itself from Russia's invasion.

他拥抱"白人至上主义"，还任由 SpaceX 的星链服务削弱乌克兰抵御俄罗斯入侵的努力。

He manned the Department of Government Efficiency that slashed government jobs and offices without care for the repercussions, including the 700,000 people who have already died because of DOGE's assault on USAID.

他执掌过"政府效率部"，大刀阔斧地砍掉政府岗位和机构，全然不顾后果——其中包括因 DOGE 对 USAID 的猛攻而已经死亡的 70 万人。

His behavior affects everyone invested in his companies, for better or worse, including those theoretical 72 million children. For a long time, "worse" has been a no-show; his fans have helped Tesla's stock price defy traditional market logic for years.

他的行为影响着每一个投资他公司的人，无论好坏——包括那 7200 万"理论上"的孩子。很长一段时间里，"坏的那一面"始终没有出现；多年来，他的粉丝们一直在帮特斯拉股价违背传统市场逻辑。

How long can the magic last?

这种魔法还能撑多久？

As tough as children have it these days, with the climate in chaos, war in Europe and the Middle East and AI threatening to take all of the jobs, do they really need the headache of finding out?

如今孩子们的处境已经够难了——气候混乱、欧洲和中东有战争、AI 还威胁要抢走所有工作——他们真的还需要为这个问题头疼吗？
