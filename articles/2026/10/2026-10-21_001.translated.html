Paris police issue overnight curfew in 2 areas of French capital to try to prevent further disorder

巴黎警方在首都两区实施宵禁，防骚乱再起

PARIS (AP) — Paris police issued an overnight curfew on Monday in two areas of the French capital, after a weekend marred by France's worst rioting in years that targeted city halls, other public buildings and thousands of businesses across the country.

巴黎（美联社）——周一起，巴黎警方在首都两个区域实施宵禁。此前一个周末，法国遭遇多年来最严重的暴乱，市政厅、其他公共建筑以及全国数千家商户成为攻击目标。

The order aims to protect "people and property from the violence and looting," the Paris police statement said.

巴黎警方的声明说，这道命令旨在保护"人员和财产免遭暴力与抢掠"。

The curfew prohibits anyone from entering or remaining on the Place de la République, where rioting took place Saturday, and the shopping neighborhood of Les Halles, which has been subject to looting.

宵禁禁止任何人进入或停留在共和国广场——周六的暴乱就发生在这里——以及屡遭抢掠的莱阿勒商业区。

Earlier Monday, Prime Minister Sébastien Lecornu had called for "very limited" and "localized" curfews. In northern France, local authorities imposed a curfew for children and teenagers under age 18 in several cities, including Lille.

周一早些时候，总理塞巴斯蒂安·勒科尔尼曾呼吁实施"非常有限"、"局部"的宵禁。在法国北部，地方当局在里尔等数座城市对 18 岁以下的儿童和青少年实施宵禁。

At least 2,400 people were arrested across the country. Authorities estimated Monday that at least 3,000 businesses were damaged or looted in several cities.

全国至少 2400 人被捕。当局周一估计，数座城市至少有 3000 家商户受损或被抢。

Flames engulfed the 19th-century City Hall in Limoges, in central France, causing its roof to collapse and nearly destroying the building.

在法国中部的利摩日，火焰吞没了 19 世纪的市政厅，导致屋顶坍塌，几乎将整座建筑摧毁。

The previous day, at the end of the protest against the rising cost of living, which brought together 5,500 people in Limoges, a group attacked the east wing of the city hall and started a fire.

前一天，在利摩日一场 5500 人参加的反生活成本上涨抗议结束时，一群人袭击了市政厅东翼并纵火。

Within three hours, the roof of the historic building, inaugurated in 1883 and modeled after the Hôtel de Ville in Paris, was completely engulfed.

不到三小时，这座历史建筑（1883 年落成，仿照巴黎市政厅建造）的屋顶就被完全吞没。

"I was horrified, but despite how violent what I saw was, I couldn't bring myself to leave." The day after the arson attack, Anne, a 47-year-old teacher, returned to the site and said she was still "dumbfounded." Marine, 18, a literature student, on the verge of tears, said she felt "heartbroken." "I don't understand how things got to this point." "Usually, our protests are always peaceful," continued Anne, who joined the march at 2 pm.

"我吓坏了，可尽管我看到的场面如此暴力，我还是舍不得走。"纵火后的第二天，47 岁的教师安娜回到现场，说她仍然"惊得说不出话"。18 岁的文学系学生玛丽娜快要哭了，说她"心碎"了。"我不明白事情怎么会发展到这一步。""通常，我们的抗议总是和平的，"下午 2 点加入游行的安娜接着说。

"There was some tension, we got a bit of tear gas, but I never felt unsafe, and then at 3:30 pm, suddenly, things deteriorated." On Sunday evening, a rally to condemn the violence drew 7,000 people.

"是有些紧张，我们沾了点催泪瓦斯，但我从没觉得不安全；然后下午 3 点半，突然间，情况就恶化了。"周日晚，一场谴责暴力的集会吸引了 7000 人。

On Monday, cleanup crews and firefighters cleared shattered glass and other debris from the streets of Paris.

周一，清理队和消防员清扫了巴黎街头的碎玻璃和其他瓦砾。

Ouri Zagoury, owner of a video game shop near Paris' Place de la République, said that he closed the store Saturday afternoon, but then saw looters forcing their way in on surveillance cameras.

乌里·扎古里在巴黎共和国广场附近经营一家电子游戏店。他说自己周六下午关了店，但随后在监控里看到抢劫者破门而入。

"There were so many people coming in and out. And even though I called the police, they said not to intervene, that they would come, but they never did, which is another problem," Zagoury said.

"进进出出的人太多了。而且我虽然报了警，他们说不必干预、他们会来，可他们一直没来——这是另一个问题，"扎古里说。

Interior Minister Laurent Nuñez offered his resignation Sunday. But President Emmanuel Macron refused it "given the need for continued command of law enforcement in the current context," Macron's office said.

内政部长洛朗·努涅斯周日提出辞职。但马克龙办公室说，总统以"当前形势下执法工作需要保持连续指挥"为由拒绝了。

He instructed Nuñez "to take all necessary steps to ensure full transparency about the failures in planning and intelligence-gathering that led to the violence." Nuñez acknowledged that authorities had "lost control" in some major cities on Saturday.

他指示努涅斯"采取一切必要措施，确保对导致暴力的筹划与情报失误保持完全透明"。努涅斯承认，周六当局在一些大城市"失去了控制"。

"You can't look shopkeepers in the eye when hundreds of businesses have been looted and say we haven't lost control in some of these major cities," Nuñez said.

"当数百家商户被抢，你没法直视店主的眼睛说我们在这些大城市里没有失去控制，"努涅斯说。

The protests in France began last month at high schools in Paris'working-class northern suburbs, where anger over teacher shortages and shabby facilities evolved into a broader movement.

法国的抗议上个月始于巴黎北部工薪阶层郊区的高中——对教师短缺和校舍破旧的愤怒，后来演变成一场更广泛的运动。

France's extremely strained public finances mean the government has precious little room for broader measures to address protesters'anger over the rising cost of living. The government has so far not committed to a substantial increase in funding for schools.

法国极度吃紧的公共财政，意味着政府几乎没有余地采取更广泛的措施，来回应抗议者对生活成本上涨的愤怒。政府迄今尚未承诺大幅增加学校经费。

However, it has pledged to send some 3,000 teachers, who are fully qualified but have not yet started work, to fill staffing gaps.

不过，政府已承诺派出约 3000 名已完全取得资格但尚未上岗的教师，填补师资缺口。

The unrest will likely deal a blow to business confidence, which is already fragile due to a fiscal crisis and high energy prices resulting from the Iran war.

这场骚乱很可能打击本已脆弱的商业信心——财政危机和伊朗战争带来的高能源价格已经让它不堪一击。

The minority government is struggling to pass spending cuts in France's deeply divided parliament as part of its 2027 budget. The unrest has weighed on French stocks and the euro and put pressure on French government bonds.

这个少数派政府正艰难地推动在严重分裂的议会通过削减开支——作为其 2027 年预算的一部分。骚乱拖累了法国股市和欧元，也给法国国债带来压力。

"Le Pen no longer needs to spend any money to campaign," Jean-Baptiste Moreau, a former lawmaker from Macron's camp, told Reuters.

"勒庞再也不需要花一分钱去竞选了，"马克龙阵营的前议员让-巴蒂斯特·莫罗对路透社说。

"It's not just a clear path ahead of her, it's a six-lane highway." Some protesters have already called for a new round of demonstrations on Saturday, October 24.

"她面前不只是一条畅通的路，而是一条六车道高速。"一些抗议者已经呼吁在 10 月 24 日（周六）举行新一轮示威。

Unions have called for strikes on November 5 to protest budget cuts and support hiking wages. The weekend violence has largely eclipsed the recent high school student protests.

工会已号召在 11 月 5 日罢工，抗议预算削减并支持加薪。周末的暴力在很大程度上盖过了近期的高中生抗议。

Schools are now on a break for two weeks, but there have been reports of protests at some universities.

学校现已放假两周，但据报道一些大学出现了抗议。

While mayors of France's biggest cities demanded answers from the government over what they say was a lack of preparation, authorities rushed to set up support measures for impacted businesses and bolster security as calm returned to the streets.

在法国各大城市市长就他们所说的"准备不足"要求政府给个说法之际，当局则赶着为受影响商户设立帮扶措施、加强安保，街头秩序逐渐恢复平静。
