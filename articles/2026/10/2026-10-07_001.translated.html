China cultivated high-rolling crime families before turning on them


A Post investigation found that criminal networks in Myanmar enjoyed the protection of Chinese officials as well as the military government in Myanmar.

《华盛顿邮报》的一项调查发现，缅甸的犯罪网络既受到缅甸军政府的庇护，也曾受到中国官员的保护。

For the scion of a crime family linked to human trafficking and enslavement, money laundering and global cyberscams, Wei Qingtao was brazenly public. His Douyin account, the Chinese-language version of TikTok, flaunted the excesses of his life in a remote corner of Myanmar by the border with China: Bentleys and Lamborghinis, rare cigars and private jets. When the 27-year-old partied at the multistory glass-walled nightclub he owned in a region called Kokang, he'd throw crisp Chinese yuan bills into the crowd.

作为一个牵涉人口贩运与奴役、洗钱和全球网络诈骗的犯罪家族的子弟，魏清涛曾肆无忌惮地高调炫耀。他在抖音（TikTok 的中文版）上的账号，炫耀着他在缅甸与中国边境一个偏远角落的奢靡生活：宾利和兰博基尼、稀有雪茄和私人飞机。当这位 27 岁的年轻人在一个叫果敢的地区、自家那栋多层玻璃幕墙夜总会里开派对时，他会向人群抛撒崭新的人民币钞票。

In November, the good times rolled to a stop. Wei's social media presence vanished. He soon appeared in a different kind of video: reading a scripted confession while in Chinese custody. "The money we make from cyberscams comes from the pensions of everyday Chinese people," Wei said. "This time, the Chinese government has made up its mind. ... We must not cling to any illusions." Within weeks, his uncle was in cuffs on a plane to China, flanked by dozens of police officers.

11 月，好日子戛然而止。魏的社交媒体账号消失了。他很快出现在另一类视频里：在中国被羁押期间念着一份写好的认罪书。"我们搞网络诈骗赚的钱，来自中国老百姓的养老金，"魏说，"这一次，中国政府是下了决心的……我们绝不能再抱任何幻想。"几周之内，他的叔叔在数十名警察的押送下戴着手铐上了飞往中国的飞机。

The clans controlled every aspect of life in Kokang, becoming lawmakers, militia heads, local administrators and powerful business executives. But at the heart of the Kokang empire were casinos, underground banking and money laundering, according to U.N. officials and U.S. diplomatic cables released by WikiLeaks. Newly built mega-casinos in the Kokang capital, Laukkaing, opened 24 hours a day.

这些家族控制着果敢生活的方方面面——他们当上立法者、民兵头目、地方行政长官和权势商人。但据联合国官员和维基解密曝光的美国外交电报，果敢这个"帝国"的核心是赌场、地下钱庄和洗钱。在果敢首府老街新建的巨型赌场，24 小时营业。

Behind the vast casino floors, a more sinister industry started to grow. Chinese court documents show that from at least 2018 "criminal syndicates" were smuggling people into the Crouching Tiger Villa, a banquet hall and hotel, and forcing them to work as scammers. Border closures that followed the outbreak of the coronavirus pandemic shut off the cash flow to the casinos, accelerating a shift to this new scam economy.

在巨大的赌场大厅背后，一个更凶险的产业开始生长。中国法庭文件显示，至少从 2018 年起，"犯罪集团"就在把人偷运进"卧虎山庄"——一个宴会厅兼酒店——并强迫他们充当诈骗分子。新冠疫情暴发后的边境封锁切断了赌场的现金流，加速了向这种新型诈骗经济的转向。

Workers, mostly Chinese nationals or ethnic Chinese, were bought, sold and traded; and then beaten, tortured or killed when they didn't reach financial targets or tried to escape, according to firsthand accounts. Many of the compounds for running scam operations were hosted in the same hotels and casino complexes long established by the clans. Others were purpose-built, but had telltale signs like barred windows, high walls and even Myanmar military snipers on the rooftops, witnesses said.

据第一手陈述，这些劳工大多是中国公民或华裔，被买卖和转手；如果完不成经济指标或试图逃跑，就会遭到殴打、酷刑或杀害。许多运营诈骗的园区，就设在各家族长期经营的同一批酒店和赌场综合体里。另一些则是专门建造的，但带着明显的特征：铁栏杆窗户、高墙，甚至屋顶上还有缅甸军方的狙击手——目击者说。

A handyman, hired to build soundproof phone booths within a scam compound in a hotel owned by the Liu family, said he was interrupted by screams as he worked. He said he saw a supervisor beating three men, their hands tied behind their backs, with lead pipes. A Taiwanese woman, who was trafficked into Laukkaing, told The Post she was waterboarded when she tried to escape. A Chinese man, who was forcibly brought into Kokang by knife-wielding men after responding to an acting job online in China, said he saw others in his compound chained and beaten. At one point, four people were shot when they fought back during beatings and tried to seize guns from the guards, he added in an interview.

一名受雇在刘氏家族名下酒店的诈骗园区里建造隔音电话亭的杂工说，他在干活时被尖叫声打断。他说，他看到一名主管用铅管殴打三名双手被反绑的男子。一名被贩运到老街的台湾女性告诉本报，她试图逃跑时遭到了水刑。一名在网上应聘中国境内演艺工作、被持刀男子强行带到果敢的中国男子说，他看到同园区其他人被锁链锁着殴打。他在一次采访中补充说，有一次四人因在挨打时反抗、试图夺取守卫的枪而被枪击。

The Crouching Tiger Villa was among the most brutally run compounds, witnesses said. There, caged lions, tigers and bears were used to threaten workers who stepped out of line, according to a person who led a team of workers there. By 2023, Beijing was coming under growing domestic and international pressure to act against these operations. The United Nations estimated in a report last year that more than 120,000 people were being forced to work in Myanmar. Signs of official Chinese concern burst into the open in August when the Chinese ambassador warned Myanmar authorities to "eradicate the cancer of gambling and scams" that was "deeply loathed by the Chinese people." At the Crouching Tiger Villa on Oct. 20, guards opened fire when more than 100 people tried to leave. The Post could not verify how many died, but four undercover Chinese police officers were among those killed.

目击者说，"卧虎山庄"是管理最残暴的园区之一。据一名在那里带队的负责人说，园区里用笼子养着狮子、老虎和熊，用来威胁那些不听话的劳工。到 2023 年，北京面临着越来越大的国内外压力，要求对这些窝点采取行动。联合国在去年的一份报告中估计，缅甸有超过 12 万人被迫从事这类工作。中国官方关切的迹象在 8 月公开爆发——中国大使警告缅甸当局要"根除赌博和诈骗这一毒瘤"，称其"为中国人民所深恶痛绝"。10 月 20 日，在"卧虎山庄"，当 100 多人试图离开时，守卫开枪了。本报无法核实死亡人数，但死者中有四名卧底的中国警察。

Confession videos featuring Wei Qingtao and two others — individuals from the Liu family and the Ming family — began to appear on Chinese media. Two of the Mings were arrested in Myanmar by local police and handed over to China. In December, China issued arrest warrants for 10 more heads of the Bai, Wei and Liu families, and their children. On Jan. 6, Laukkaing, the capital of Kokang, fell to the MNDAA, returning full control of the region to the rebel group for the first time in 15 years.

以魏清涛和另外两人——来自刘氏家族和明氏家族——为主角的认罪视频开始出现在中文媒体上。明家两人被缅甸当地警方逮捕并移交中国。12 月，中国又对白、魏、刘三家的 10 名头目及其子女发布了逮捕令。1 月 6 日，果敢首府老街落入缅甸民族民主同盟军（MNDAA）之手，15 年来首次把该地区的完全控制权交还给这支反叛武装。

Since last July, 49,000 scam workers have been returned home to China, according to the Ministry of Public Security. The casinos and hotels owned by the Kokang crime families still stand, but have gone dark. Hanley Interstellar, Wei's club, is deserted. So too is the Crouching Tiger Villa, its walls crumbling and pockmarked with bullet holes, verified videos show. But some of the exotic animals — a tiger and five bears — survived the conflict. They still roam their cages, depending on rebel soldiers to bring their daily meal of chicken.

据中国公安部数据，自去年 7 月以来，已有 4.9 万名诈骗劳工被遣返回中国。果敢犯罪家族拥有的赌场和酒店仍在，但已陷入黑暗。魏的夜总会"星际"（Hanley Interstellar）人去楼空。经核实的视频显示，"卧虎山庄"同样如此，墙体剥落、布满弹孔。但其中一些异域动物——一只老虎和五只熊——在冲突中活了下来。它们仍在自己的笼子里踱步，靠着反叛士兵送来的每日一顿鸡肉维生。
