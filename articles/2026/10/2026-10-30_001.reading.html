The Spanish prime minister has declared three days of mourning after at least 68 people were killed and dozens more injured in a collision between two high-speed trains that officials suggested was caused by an “inexplicable” error at the central command centre. The trains, one from Spain’s state-owned Renfe and the other from the private rail company Iryo, were travelling on Wednesday morning in the same direction on the line that stretches from the Spanish capital, Madrid, to the southern region of Andalusia. After the Iryo train came to a halt, the other slammed into its rear, knocking several carriages off the track near the town of Urda.
西班牙首相宣布全国哀悼三天。此前，两列高速列车发生相撞事故，造成至少68人死亡、数十人受伤。官员表示，事故可能是由中央指挥中心一次“令人费解”的失误造成的。周三上午，两列列车沿着连接西班牙首都马德里与南部安达卢西亚地区的铁路线路同向行驶，其中一列属于西班牙国有铁路公司Renfe，另一列属于私营铁路公司Iryo。Iryo列车停下后，另一列列车猛烈撞上了它的尾部，导致数节车厢在乌尔达镇附近脱轨。

Critical Thinking 课后思考
If a tragedy could have been prevented, is it still just an accident?

如果一场悲剧原本可以避免，那么它还能仅仅被称为一场“意外”吗？
On Wednesday morning, a deadly collision between two high-speed trains in Spain claimed at least 68 lives. One train was stationary when another slammed into its rear. The impact sent passengers flying and left many injured. A survivor recounted seeing people covered in blood. Investigators suspected human error after a signalling system malfunction. The command centre had instructed trains to resume their journeys despite a stop signal. This was Spain’s second major deadly train accident in ten months, rattling the country. Opposition leaders called for an immediate investigation and an independent review of the railway network.

周三上午，西班牙两列高速列车发生严重相撞事故，造成至少68人死亡。一列列车停在轨道上时，另一列列车从后方猛烈撞上。巨大的撞击力将乘客甩飞，导致多人受伤。一名幸存者回忆说，他看到许多人浑身是血。信号系统发生故障后，调查人员怀疑事故可能涉及人为失误。尽管停车信号仍然有效，指挥中心却指示列车恢复行驶。这是西班牙十个月内发生的第二起重大致命列车事故，令全国深受震动。反对党领导人呼吁立即展开调查，并对铁路网络进行独立审查。

思考题参考答案（选做）

I don’t think so. An accident is something nobody wants to happen, but that doesn’t mean nobody is responsible. If people ignore safety warnings or make careless decisions, the tragedy may be preventable. In this case, technology failed, but human decisions may have made the situation worse. We cannot prevent every accident, but we can learn from our mistakes. The most important thing is not just to find out what went wrong, but to make sure it never happens again.

我不这么认为。意外是没有人希望发生的事情，但这并不意味着没有人需要承担责任。如果人们忽视安全警告，或者作出草率的决定，那么悲剧原本可能是可以避免的。在这起事故中，技术出现了故障，但人为决定可能让情况变得更加糟糕。我们无法阻止所有意外发生，但可以从错误中吸取教训。最重要的不仅是查清究竟哪里出了问题，更是确保同样的悲剧不再重演。
