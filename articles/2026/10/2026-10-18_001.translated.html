The Tesla Roadster flew, drove upside down, and hit 60mph in under a second

特斯拉 Roadster 会飞、能倒挂行驶，不到 1 秒破 60 英里/时

Elon Musk promised a flying car. And the Tesla Roadster did exactly that.

埃隆·马斯克承诺过要造一辆会飞的车。而特斯拉 Roadster 恰恰做到了。

Tesla demonstrated its next-generation Roadster at an event in Waco, Texas, where the electric supercar drove upside down, briefly flew, and accelerated to 60mph in under a second.

特斯拉在得克萨斯州韦科的一场活动上展示了下一代 Roadster。这台电动超跑倒挂着开了一圈，短暂地飞了起来，并在不到 1 秒内加速到 60 英里/时。

It was an impressive demonstration of a car that likely will come with some heavy caveats and restrictions if it ever goes into production.

这是一场令人印象深刻的演示——但如果这台车真要投产，大概率会附带一大堆严苛的限制条件。

The event was less than 30 minutes and felt more like a stunt show than a product reveal. Indeed, there was no mention of price, trim levels, release date, or other such mundane details.

整场活动不到 30 分钟，与其说是产品发布，不如说更像一场特技表演。事实上，价格、配置等级、上市日期这类常规信息只字未提。

Musk promised the hypercar would go into production in 2028, but after nearly nine years who's counting. The people were here to see a car fly, and that's what Tesla gave them.

马斯克承诺这台超跑将在 2028 年投产，不过在等了近九年之后，谁还在乎这个数。人们来现场是为了看一辆车飞起来，而特斯拉给的正是这个。

First, the Roadster drove up a very tall, multi-story ramp, using its incredible downforce to cling to the vertical roadway.

首先，Roadster 沿着一座多层高的巨大坡道向上行驶，凭借惊人的下压力紧紧吸附在垂直路面上。

When it reached the top of the ramp, it flipped around using its cold-gas thrusters to bring it gently back down to earth.

到达坡顶后，它利用冷气推进器翻身调头，再缓缓落回地面。

The car uses a fan-based suction system, supplied by SpaceX and mounted underneath the vehicle, which is capable of generating up to two Gs of suction force.

这台车采用一套由 SpaceX 提供、安装在车底的风机吸附系统，能产生高达 2G 的吸附力。

Next, the Roadster flew over the rest of Tesla's lineup, including the Model 3, Model Y, Cybertruck, Semi, and Cybercab — doing what Tesla VP for Engineering Lars Moravy called "a kick flip" in the process.

接着，Roadster 从特斯拉其余车型的头顶飞过，包括 Model 3、Model Y、Cybertruck、Semi 和 Cybercab——特斯拉工程副总裁拉尔斯·莫拉维把这个过程称为"一个尖翻"。

Musk explained that it features attitude-control thrusters as well as main thrusters at the rear, giving the Roadster rocket-like capabilities. "So it can basically fly like a rocket," Musk said. "And it could do rolls.

马斯克解释说，它配有姿态控制推进器，以及位于车尾的主推进器，让 Roadster 具备火箭般的能力。"所以它基本上可以像火箭一样飞行，"马斯克说，"它还能做横滚。

It could do, like, crazy gymnastics. Whatever you want, really. Now, I should say that this is not necessarily a safety-optimized car.

它能做各种疯狂的特技动作。真的，想怎么来就怎么来。不过我得说明，这未必是一台以安全为最优目标的车，

But it takes the fun meter to 11 out of 10." Of course, it seems extremely unlikely that this version of the car will ever be available to the average consumer — even as Musk claimed "This will be an actual car that's on the roads." The company would have to satisfy strict regulatory requirements from the Federal Aviation Administration, as well as the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration.

但它把'乐趣指数'拉到了满分 10 分里的 11 分。"当然，这个版本的车几乎不可能面向普通消费者销售——尽管马斯克声称"这会是一台真正在路上跑的车"。公司必须满足美国联邦航空管理局以及国家公路交通安全管理局的严格监管要求。

The Roadster could go into production in 2028, as Musk promised — or it could be another decade of missed deadlines and broken promises. Again, who cares? Car flew.

Roadster 可能会像马斯克承诺的那样在 2028 年投产，也可能再迎来一个充斥着跳票与食言的十年。再说一遍，谁在乎呢？车飞起来了。

Tesla has also said that the Roadster can accelerate from 0-60mph in less than a second. And during the event, the company claimed it did so in 0.81 seconds.

特斯拉还表示，Roadster 的 0-60 英里/时加速可以在 1 秒内完成。在本次活动上，公司宣称实际成绩是 0.81 秒。

Musk explained that increasing the applied force while reducing the vehicle's mass help boost acceleration. "You've got to crack halfway to the speed of sound, I think," Musk teased. "Mach 0.5.

马斯克解释说，增大施加的作用力、同时减轻车身质量，有助于提升加速性能。"我觉得你得冲到音速的一半，"马斯克卖关子说，"0.5 马赫。

Maybe a little higher." The next-gen Roadster certainly took the scenic route in getting here. A prototype version was announced as a "one more thing" style surprise during the 2017 Tesla Semi event.

也许还能再高一点。"下一代 Roadster 走到今天，确实绕了很远的路。它的原型车曾以"还有一件事"式的惊喜，在 2017 年的特斯拉 Semi 发布会上亮相。

The specs sounded ludicrous even back then: 0-60 mph in 1.9 seconds, a top speed over 250 mph, a 200 kWh battery delivering up to 620 miles of range, and a $200,000 starting price tag.

即便在当时，那些参数听起来也荒唐：0-60 英里/时 1.9 秒、极速超过 250 英里/时、200 千瓦时电池带来最高 620 英里的续航，以及 20 万美元的起售价。

Musk said deliveries would start in 2020, which sounded perfectly doable at the time. Early adopters quickly threw down $50,000 deposits (or $250,000 for the limited Founders Series), effectively interest-free loans to help fund Tesla's scaling ambitions.

马斯克说 2020 年开始交付，这在当时听起来完全可行。早期预订者很快砸下 5 万美元定金（限量创始人系列则是 25 万美元），实际上等于给特斯拉的扩张野心提供了无息贷款。

But then Tesla seemed to forget about the Roadster, cementing its status as the ultimate vaporware. Production deadlines slipped as Tesla prioritized mass-market vehicles like the Model Y, while also wrestling with the overly complicated Cybertruck.

但此后特斯拉似乎把 Roadster 忘了，让它坐稳了"终极画饼产品"的位置。随着特斯拉把重心放在 Model Y 这类走量车型上，同时还要应付过于复杂的 Cybertruck，量产期限一拖再拖。

Occasionally, when Musk would remember that the Roadster existed, he would up the ante about its capabilities. This culminated in the "SpaceX package" that would equip the supercar with cold-gas thrusters, bestowing hover powers and a 0-60 time under one second.

偶尔，当马斯克想起 Roadster 的存在时，就会把它的性能吹得更高。最终演变成那个"SpaceX 套件"——给这台超跑装上冷气推进器，赋予它悬停能力和不到 1 秒的 0-60 加速成绩。

Musk has also sought to downplay the Roadster's significance, calling it a "small potato situation" that will not "move the needle in a major way financially." He's framed the supercar more as a joint engineering collaboration between Tesla and SpaceX, even questioning whether it's "even a car anymore" given its experimental nature.

马斯克也试图淡化 Roadster 的重要性，称它是"小土豆一桩"，不会"在财务上带来多大改变"。他更倾向于把这台超跑定位为特斯拉与 SpaceX 的一次联合工程合作，甚至鉴于其实验性质，质疑它"还算不算一辆车"。

Some analysts theorize that the decision to re-release the Roadster is an attempt to set the stage for a rumored Tesla-SpaceX merger.

一些分析人士推测，重新推出 Roadster 是为传闻中的特斯拉与 SpaceX 合并铺路。

Tesla's failure to bring the Roadster to market allowed other automakers to slip past the company with their own hyper-powered EVs.

特斯拉未能把 Roadster 推向市场，让其他车企得以凭借自家的高性能电动车实现反超。

Rimac has smashed performance records with the Nevera, Porsche has refined electric performance with the Taycan, and Chinese giants like BYD are producing track-ready supercars at a fraction of the cost.

Rimac 用 Nevera 打破了多项性能纪录，保时捷用 Taycan 打磨了电动性能，而比亚迪这样的中国巨头正在以零头成本生产可下赛道的超跑。

The Roadster is no longer shocking the world as an impossible prototype; it's playing catch-up to an industry that spent nine years building what Tesla merely rendered. But then again, none of those cars flew.

Roadster 已不再是那个震惊世界的"不可能的原型车"；它现在是在追赶一个用了九年时间造出、而特斯拉只停留在渲染图上的行业。不过话说回来，那些车可都没飞起来。
