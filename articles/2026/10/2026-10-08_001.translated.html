Why Does Anyone Care About the Nobel Prize?

为什么人人在乎诺贝尔奖？

"Just go to sleep," Martin Chalfie's wife told him late one October night in 2008.

"去睡吧，"2008 年 10 月的一个深夜，马丁·查尔菲的妻子对他说。

Chalfie, a Columbia University chemist, had co-authored an influential paper describing a new method for studying cells with green fluorescent protein, and he was anxiously waiting to see whether it would be recognized.

查尔菲是哥伦比亚大学的化学家，曾与人合写一篇有影响力的论文，描述一种用绿色荧光蛋白研究细胞的新方法；他正焦虑地等待这篇论文能否得到承认。

His wife, a fellow academic, tempered his expectations. "It's a wonderful tool, and lots of people use it," he recalls her saying.

他的妻子也是一位学者，她给他降了降温。"这是个很棒的工具，而且很多人在用它，"他回忆她这样说。

"But, to tell you the truth, it will never win the Nobel." So when the call from Sweden came in, Chalfie was asleep.

"可是说实话，它永远不会得诺贝尔奖。"所以当瑞典打来电话时，查尔菲正在睡觉。

This past week, the 2024 Nobel class was introduced to the world, creating a new crop of Olympian figures.

刚刚过去的这一周，2024 年的诺贝尔奖得主们被介绍给全世界，又造就了一批"神一般"的人物。

Chalfie might tell you that the award hasn't changed his day-to-day life all that much, that his grant applications still sometimes get denied. But the medal radiates from his chest even when he's not wearing it.

查尔菲也许会告诉你，这个奖并没有怎么改变他的日常生活，他的科研经费申请有时照样被拒。但那枚奖章即便在他没佩戴的时候，也从胸前散发光芒。

The Nobel is the honor above all honors. The achievements it rewards—in science, literature, and peace—contribute "to the greatest benefit of mankind," a tagline any other respectable award committee would be too shy to claim.

诺贝尔奖是荣誉之上的荣誉。它所表彰的成就——在科学、文学与和平领域——都"为人类最大福祉"作出了贡献，这句口号是其他任何体面的奖项委员会都会羞于说出口的。

How did a single award from the small nation of Sweden become the undisputed highest honor in the world? Establishing a prize is easy. The hard part is getting anyone to care.

一个来自瑞典这个小国的单一奖项，是如何成为世界上无可争议的最高荣誉的？设立一个奖很容易，难的是让人们在意。

Alfred Nobel, a businessman who made his fortune selling dynamite and explosives in the 19th century, was not an obvious candidate to become the namesake of the most prestigious distinction in the world.

阿尔弗雷德·诺贝尔——一位在 19 世纪靠销售炸药和爆炸物致富的商人——怎么看都不像是会成为世界上最高声望荣誉之名祖的人。

For the Nobel to become the Nobel, a few people had to get a few things really right. When rich individuals bequeath all their wealth to something other than their would-be heirs, their would-be heirs tend to resist.

要让诺贝尔奖成为"诺贝尔奖"，有几个人必须把几件事做得非常对。当富人把所有财富遗赠给继承人以外的人或事时，那些准继承人往往会抵制。

The Italian businessman Jerome Ponti, for example, had more or less the same vision as Nobel.

比如意大利商人杰罗姆·蓬蒂，他的愿景与诺贝尔大致相同。

But after his surviving relatives learned that he had donated his fortune to national academies headquartered in London, Paris, and Vienna in 1874, a group of them successfully litigated to void the will.

但在他幸存的亲属得知他于 1874 年把财产捐给了总部设在伦敦、巴黎和维也纳的国家科学院之后，其中一群人成功提起诉讼，使遗嘱无效。

In the 1890s, the executors of Nobel's estate had to be a bit more clever.

到了 1890 年代，诺贝尔遗产的执行人必须更聪明一些。

They preemptively reached an agreement with Nobel's two living nephews, one of whom was particularly gracious and persuaded the rest of the family to honor the late Nobel's wishes.

他们抢先与诺贝尔当时健在的两位侄子达成协议，其中一位格外通情达理，说服了家族其余成员尊重已故诺贝尔的遗愿。

A second reason the Nobel prevailed over prizes of similar ambition was that Sweden turned it into a nationalist project.

诺贝尔奖能压过同等雄心的其他奖项，第二个原因是：瑞典把它变成了一项民族工程。

Nobel himself did not mention anything about Swedish interests, but the government and the press interpreted his bequest as a call to action for the nation as a whole.

诺贝尔本人并没有提到任何关于瑞典利益的内容，但政府和媒体把他的遗赠解读为对全国的行动号召。

"The Swedish scientific society could not have been invested with a more glorious task," a newspaper editorial declared. The effort turned out to be well timed.

"瑞典科学界不可能被赋予比这更光荣的任务，"一篇报纸社论宣称。这项努力来得恰逢其时。

In the early 1900s, a new prize in Sweden might have seemed unlikely to compete in prestige with one given by the French Academy of Sciences or the Royal Society in London.

在 1900 年代初，瑞典的一个新奖项，看上去未必能在声望上与法国科学院或伦敦皇家学会颁发的奖项竞争。

But when World War I struck, neutral little Sweden had a claim to objectivity that the great powers didn't. Finally, both Nobel and the executors of his will designed the rules wisely.

但当第一次世界大战爆发时，中立的小国瑞典拥有列强所不具备的一种"客观性"主张。最后，诺贝尔本人和他的遗嘱执行人都把规则设计得很明智。

Most prestigious prizes at the time were international only in theory; judges tended to favor their countrymen.

当时大多数著名奖项只是理论上"国际化"；评委往往偏袒本国人。

Nobel requested that each award be given to the "worthiest person, whether or not they're Scandinavian." The Nobel Prize committee became the first to systematically solicit nominations from institutions abroad, turning the award into a sort of contest among nations.

诺贝尔要求每个奖项都颁给"最值得的人，无论他们是否为斯堪的纳维亚人"。诺贝尔奖委员会成为第一个系统性地向国外机构征集提名的机构，从而把这个奖变成了某种国家之间的竞赛。

And one of the executors of the will had the idea of rewarding achievements no matter when they had been made, rather than just the work or discoveries of the previous year.

而遗嘱的一位执行人想出了这样一个主意：奖励成就，不论它是何时做出的，而不仅仅是上一年度的工作或发现。

This was a clever move, not just because some discoveries can take time to reveal their worth, but also because it turned winning the Nobel into a more sweeping victory.

这是一步妙棋——不仅因为有些发现需要时间才能显露出价值，也因为它让赢得诺贝尔奖变成一场更彻底的胜利。

Fortified with its elegant design choices, the Nobel quickly established a first mover advantage. The Scottish economist Angus Deaton told me that nothing comes close to the Nobel ceremony.

凭借这些精巧的设计选择，诺贝尔奖迅速确立了先发优势。苏格兰经济学家安格斯·迪顿告诉我，没有任何典礼能比得上诺贝尔颁奖典礼。

On the night after he found out he won, Deaton couldn't keep the news to himself. He told his Uber driver on the way home from dinner in Princeton and watched him become ecstatically happy.

在得知自己获奖的第二天晚上，迪顿忍不住把这个消息说了出去。他在普林斯顿吃完饭回家的路上告诉了优步司机，并看着对方欣喜若狂。

The driver at first assumed that Deaton had won the peace prize; when he learned it was for economics, his enthusiasm dimmed, but only slightly. A Nobel is a Nobel. Prizes aren't all great.

司机起初以为迪顿得的是和平奖；当他得知是经济学奖时，热情稍减，但也只是稍减。诺贝尔奖就是诺贝尔奖。并非所有奖项都了不起。

The main ideology the Nobel Foundation appears to be committed to, however, is the importance of preserving the prestige of the Nobel Prize—and in this it has succeeded.

然而，诺贝尔基金会似乎致力于的主要意识形态，是维护诺贝尔奖声望的重要性——而在这一点上它成功了。

If there were a prize for prizes, the Nobel would win every year.

如果有一个"奖中之奖"，诺贝尔奖每年都会赢。
