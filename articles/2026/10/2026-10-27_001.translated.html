How a Trillion-Dollar Industry Launders Illegal Gold

儿童挖矿、黑帮洗金，伦敦金库照单全收：你买的黄金干净吗？

In an industry riddled with abuse, the world's banks and governments rely on one entity to keep gangsters, warlords and human rights violators from selling gold into the mainstream market.

在一个充斥着侵害行为的行业里，全世界的银行和政府都依赖同一家机构，来阻止黑帮、军阀和侵犯人权者把黄金卖进主流市场。

That group, the London Bullion Market Association, is the self-described "global authority for precious metals." But a New York Times investigation found that the association has repeatedly allowed gold into the market that has been mined by criminals, cartel members and children.

这个机构就是伦敦金银市场协会（L.B.M.A.），它自称是“贵金属的全球权威”。但《纽约时报》的调查发现，该协会屡次放行由罪犯、贩毒集团成员和儿童开采的黄金进入市场。

Refiners lie with impunity about the origins of their gold. And in the case of Madagascar, the L.B.M.A. helped change and backdate key paperwork that certified gold as free from abuse.

精炼商对黄金来源撒谎却不受惩罚。在马达加斯加这个案子里，L.B.M.A. 甚至帮着修改并倒填关键文书——那份文书原本是用来认证这批黄金“未涉侵害”的。

The Times investigation shows that the divide between "clean" and "dirty" gold is an industry fiction. There is just gold.

《纽约时报》的调查显示，“干净”黄金与“肮脏”黄金之间的界线，只是行业编造出来的说法。黄金就是黄金。

And as Wall Street, central banks and investors stockpile and trade the precious metal, they're profiting off a supply that includes gold from illegal sources. The price this year crossed the $5,000 mark.

随着华尔街、各国央行和投资者囤积并交易这种贵金属，他们获利的供给中就有来自非法渠道的黄金。今年金价突破了 5000 美元大关。

That has made it profitable to mine in inefficient, destructive and politically destabilizing ways. The industry works like this: Miners and brokers sell raw gold to refiners, which melt it, remove impurities, and form it into bars.

这让以低效、破坏性且在政治上制造动荡的方式采矿变得有利可图。这个行业是这样运作的：矿工和经纪人把原金卖给精炼厂，后者将其熔化、去除杂质，铸成金条。

The major international marketplace accepts bars only from the 67 L.B.M.A.-certified refineries. Those refiners must document the source of every ounce of gold in every bar.

主要的国际市场只接受由 67 家 L.B.M.A. 认证精炼厂生产的金条。这些精炼厂必须为每根金条中每一盎司黄金记录来源。

But for all its talk about being a global authority, the L.B.M.A. is mostly a paperwork collector. The association relies on refiners to inspect the mines and to self-report the details.

但尽管口口声声说自己是全球权威，L.B.M.A. 基本上只是个收文书的机构。协会依赖精炼商自己去检查矿区、自行上报细节。

Madagascar's central bank decided to capitalize on booming prices by buying and stockpiling gold, including from illegal mines that employ children. To see how it happened, I began at the mines themselves.

马达加斯加央行决定借价格暴涨之机买入并囤积黄金，其中就包括来自雇用童工的非法矿区的黄金。为了弄清事情是怎么发生的，我从矿区本身开始走访。

I took a propeller plane from the capital, Antananarivo, and drove three hours through farmland to a mine called Madagold.

我从首都塔那那利佛乘一架螺旋桨飞机，再驱车三小时穿过农田，来到一座名为 Madagold 的矿场。

The mine sits in a dry valley in the northern part of the island, surrounded by a busy market fueled by gold-rush cash. Everywhere I looked, at Madagold and at mines across the country, children were in danger.

这座矿位于该岛北部一处干燥的谷地，四周是一个靠淘金热现金撑起来的繁忙市集。我目之所及，无论是 Madagold 还是全国各地的矿场，孩子们都身处危险之中。

Children in Madagascar's mines crushed rocks and hauled buckets of ore. Toddlers wandered between 100-foot-deep shafts. In the gold-rich northern region of Diana, miners follow no laws, the government's regional mining supervisor, Firmin Razafindratondra, said.

马达加斯加矿场里的儿童砸碎石块、搬运一桶桶矿石。幼童在深达 100 英尺的竖井之间游荡。在盛产黄金的北部迪亚纳地区，矿工们不守任何法律，政府区域矿业监督员 Firmin Razafindratondra 说。

All of the area's gold mining, he said, is illegal. Outside Mananjary, on Madagascar's east coast, miners operated despite government shutdown orders. Excavators turned rice paddies into a giant pit.

他说，这一地区的所有采金活动都是非法的。在马达加斯加东海岸的马南扎里城外，矿工们无视政府的关停令继续作业。挖掘机把稻田变成了一个巨大的坑。

For that gold to be worth anything, it needed to be "certified L.B.M.A.," Madagascar's central bank governor, Aivo Andrianarivelo, said. So he dispatched his gold chief to Switzerland to find a refiner.

这些黄金要想有任何价值，就必须“获得 L.B.M.A. 认证”，马达加斯加央行行长 Aivo Andrianarivelo 说。于是他派他的黄金主管去瑞士寻找精炼商。

Refiners questioned the gold's origins and legality. "Nobody wanted to touch this gold," said Marc Ummel, a gold-supply-chain specialist at the nonprofit group SwissAid. Small-scale mines are considered red flags.

精炼商质疑这批黄金的来源与合法性。“没人想碰这批金子，”非营利组织瑞士援助组织（SwissAid）的黄金供应链专家 Marc Ummel 说。小规模矿场被视为危险信号。

The L.B.M.A.'s rules say that any refiner accepting this gold would be required to conduct mine inspections. "It's impossible," Mr. Ummel remembers telling Madagascar's banker.

L.B.M.A. 的规则规定，任何接收这批黄金的精炼商都必须进行矿区检查。“这不可能，”Ummel 先生记得自己这样告诉马达加斯加的这位央行官员。

"You will never be able to find an L.B.M.A. refiner." On that point, he was wrong.

“你永远找不到一家 L.B.M.A. 精炼商。”在这一点上，他说错了。

The central bank cut a deal in 2023 with the Istanbul Gold Refinery, one of the world's biggest L.B.M.A.-certified refiners.

2023 年，该央行与伊斯坦布尔黄金精炼厂达成交易，后者是全球最大的 L.B.M.A. 认证精炼商之一。

The Turkish refiner's auditor filed paperwork with the L.B.M.A. claiming that it "does not do any business" with mines that raise red flags. That claim freed the company from having to conduct inspections.

这家土耳其精炼厂的审计员向 L.B.M.A. 提交文书，声称该公司“与亮起红灯的矿场没有任何业务往来”。这一说法使该公司免于进行检查。

The raw gold was melted into 12 kg bars at a plant next to Madagascar's international airport. From there, tens of millions of dollars in gold was shipped to Turkey to enter the legitimate supply.

原金在马达加斯加国际机场旁的一家工厂被熔铸成 12 公斤的金条。随后，价值数千万美元的黄金被运往土耳其，进入合法供应链。

It was only by chance that anyone at the L.B.M.A. took a look at the Madagascar gold. The European Union happened to be considering certifying the association for a new oversight role.

完全是偶然，L.B.M.A. 才有人看了一眼这批马达加斯加黄金。当时欧盟恰好在考虑认证该协会，准备赋予它一项新的监管职能。

An E.U. inspector noticed the Madagascar deal and began scrutinizing it. How, the inspector asked, could a refinery process gold from Madagascar yet claim no red flags?

一位欧盟检查员注意到了这笔马达加斯加交易，并开始仔细审查。这名检查员问：一家精炼厂怎么可能加工来自马达加斯加的黄金，却声称没有红灯？

L.B.M.A. staff escalated the problem to a "compliance panel" that included the chief executive, Ms. Crowell. The association could have punished the refiner. Instead, the L.B.M.A. helped cover up the problem.

L.B.M.A. 的工作人员把问题上报给一个“合规小组”，其成员包括首席执行官 Crowell 女士。协会本可以惩罚这家精炼商。但 L.B.M.A. 反而帮着掩盖了问题。

Association officials worked with the Istanbul Gold Refinery's auditor to create a new report that made it seem like the refiner had disclosed the red flags from the beginning and had taken steps to scrutinize the supply chain.

协会官员与伊斯坦布尔黄金精炼厂的审计员合作，炮制了一份新报告，让事情看起来像是这家精炼商从一开始就披露了红灯，并已采取措施审查供应链。

The report was backdated, as if it had been filed in real time. That backdated report remains on the L.B.M.A.'s website, with no indication of any alterations. The matter was closed.

这份报告被倒填了日期，仿佛它是实时提交的。那份倒填日期的报告至今仍挂在 L.B.M.A. 网站上，看不出任何改动痕迹。事情就此了结。

The gold was certified as meeting the world's most rigorous standards.

这批黄金被认证为符合全球最严格的标准。

Now those same refiners who had turned Madagascar away are calling looking to buy gold, Mr. Andrianarivelo said, because the L.B.M.A. had declared that "there was no issue" with it.

Andrianarivelo 先生说，如今那些曾经把马达加斯加拒之门外的精炼商，正打电话来想买黄金，因为 L.B.M.A. 已宣布这批黄金“没有问题”。
