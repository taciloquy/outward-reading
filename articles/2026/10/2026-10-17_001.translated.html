Mark Carney shows middle powers how to handle a dangerous world

马克·卡尼向中等强国示范如何应对危险世界

Entitled Canada has been feeding off America for decades. Like a parasitic bug, it enjoys the benefits of being America's cherished 51st state without giving anything back.

自视特殊的加拿大几十年来一直在"啃"美国。像一只寄生虫，它享受着作为美国宠爱的"第 51 个州"的好处，却不给任何回报。

Canadians know they couldn't live without America's protection, yet their ungrateful prime minister, Mark Carney, refuses to defer to the great and beautiful country over the border.

加拿大人知道没有美国的保护他们活不下去，可他们那位不知感恩的总理马克·卡尼，却拒绝向边境以南那个伟大而美丽的国家低头。

That is how Donald Trump sees Canada, and it leaves no room for mutual benefit between the two allies. The bullying president is ratcheting up tariffs. Canadian consumers, in response, are spitting out their Kentucky bourbon and leaving Las Vegas.

唐纳德·特朗普就是这样看加拿大的——这种看法让两个盟国之间毫无互利可言。这位霸凌成性的总统正在不断加码关税；作为回应，加拿大消费者倒掉了肯塔基波本威士忌，也不再光顾拉斯维加斯。

Mr Carney, in office for 18 months, remains defiant, the star of an extended, dramatic and riveting stand-off. By now you might have expected Mr Carney to have caved in.

上任 18 个月的卡尼依然不肯屈服，成为这场漫长、戏剧性又引人入胜的对峙中的主角。按说到这个时候，你大概会以为卡尼早就服软了。

Canada is only a mid size power with a so-so economy and puny armed forces. It has vulnerabilities aplenty.

加拿大只是一个中等规模的国家，经济平平，军力孱弱。它的软肋多得很。

One will be evident on October 19th, when the province of Alberta holds a referendum asking if it may one day secede, taking with it more oil in the ground than in the entire United States.

其中一个软肋将在 10 月 19 日显现——艾伯塔省将举行公投，询问是否有一天可以脱离加拿大；而该省地下的石油储量比整个美国还多。

In fact Mr Carney is riding high. Polls suggest that those Albertans will probably vote to stay. And under him, Canada is doing better than you might expect.

事实上，卡尼如今风头正劲。民调显示，那些艾伯塔人很可能会投票选择留下。而在他的领导下，加拿大的表现比你预想的要好。

In January it was Mr Carney, at the annual jamboree in Davos, who argued that what he called the "middle powers" must find ways to take on threatening ones.

今年 1 月，正是在达沃斯的那场年度盛会上，卡尼提出：他所谓的"中等强国"必须找到办法去应对那些具有威胁性的大国。

Nine months on, his country offers lessons for what these middle powers should do when the hegemonic giant pulls the mat from under them. Those lessons start with a bracing dose of realism.

九个月过去了。当那个霸权巨人突然抽走脚下的地毯时，中等强国该怎么做——他的国家给出了答案。这些经验，首先是一剂令人清醒的现实主义。

As we set out, middle powers will never replace the hegemons as the force that sustains powerful permanent institutions such as the UN.

正如我们此前阐述的，中等强国永远不会取代霸权国家，成为支撑联合国这类强大常设机构的力量。

The world has grown more dangerous because both America and China are exploiting or undermining arrangements and norms in which power was shared. Emboldened, wreckers like Russia and Iran are becoming even more predatory.

世界变得更加危险，因为美国和中国都在利用或破坏那些原本共享权力的安排与规范。受到鼓舞的俄罗斯、伊朗这类"破坏者"正变得更加贪婪。

Co-ordinating the more herbivorous middle powers is often too hard to get much done—as you can see in even the most developed middle-power club, the European Union.

要把这些"食草型"的中等强国协调起来往往难上加难，难有作为——这一点即便在最成熟的中等强国俱乐部欧盟身上也能看到。

Yet middle powers can still mitigate some of the damage, as Canada and its allies show.

然而，中等强国仍可以减轻部分损害——加拿大及其盟友就是例证。

Mr Carney has zipped around the world making new trade deals as well as drumming up symbolic agreements, such as talk of associate EU membership.

卡尼马不停蹄地奔走世界各地，谈成新的贸易协议，也张罗着一些象征性的安排，比如讨论成为欧盟的"准成员国"。

In the realm of defence, Canada is trimming its old reliance on America, and is considering placing an order for Swedish Gripen fighter jets. Canada's most important lesson, however, emerges from what the pragmatic Mr Carney is doing at home.

在国防领域，加拿大正削减对美国的长期依赖，并考虑订购瑞典的"鹰狮"战斗机。不过，加拿大最重要的一条经验，来自务实的卡尼在国内所做的事。

A few years ago the country's woeful politics and ailing government seemed broken. Income per person had drifted from 90% of America's in the 1980s to just 71% in 2022, much of it following a recent burst of low-skilled immigration.

几年前，这个国家糟糕的政治和积弊丛生的政府看起来已经失灵。人均收入从 1980 年代相当于美国的 90%，滑落到 2022 年仅有 71%，其中很大一部分源于近年一波低技能移民的涌入。

Today Mr Carney is pushing through overdue measures to boost productivity, open the economy and lift defence spending from a pitifully low 1.3% of GDP to 2%.

如今，卡尼正在推动一系列早该出台的措施：提升生产率、开放经济，并把国防开支从低得可怜的占 GDP 1.3% 提高到 2%。

Among his first steps, he scrapped an unpopular consumer carbon tax and vowed to build a pipeline to carry oil from Alberta to the west coast.

作为最初的举措之一，他取消了一项不受欢迎的消费者碳税，并承诺修建一条把石油从艾伯塔输往西海岸的管道。

He has started to cut internal barriers to trade, called off a hefty rise in capital-gains tax, cut the lowest rate of income tax and set out plans to trim federal spending.

他已着手削减国内贸易壁垒，取消了大幅提高资本利得税的计划，降低了最低档所得税税率，并提出了压缩联邦开支的方案。

Mr Carney has so far managed to resist what is known as the "Juncker curse", named after a president of the European Commission, Jean-Claude Juncker.

到目前为止，卡尼成功抵御了所谓的"容克魔咒"——它以欧盟委员会前主席让-克洛德·容克的名字命名。

Speaking of reform, Mr Juncker once said: "We all know what to do, we just don't know how to get re-elected after we've done it." Yet Mr Carney's popularity has been surging even as he reforms.

谈到改革，容克曾说过："我们都知道该做什么，只是不知道做完之后该怎么连任。"然而卡尼的支持率却随着改革推进不断攀升。

Today a maple-leaf love-in is under way: polls show the prime minister's approval rating nearing 70%. Internationally, he has replaced France's beleaguered Emmanuel Macron as the centrists' pin-up. The contrast with Mr Trump, unloved by his own voters, is stark.

如今加拿大正上演一场"枫叶之恋"：民调显示总理的支持率接近 70%。在国际上，他取代了焦头烂额的法国总统埃马纽埃尔·马克龙，成为中间派的新宠。与自己选民都不喜欢的特朗普相比，反差鲜明。

True, Canada must go further and faster with its liberalising changes. There is every chance that relations with America will improve when Mr Trump leaves office in 2029, but it is not certain.

诚然，加拿大的自由化改革还必须走得更远、更快。特朗普 2029 年卸任后，加美关系很有可能会改善，但这并非板上钉钉。

It should therefore work on keeping its options open. Other middle powers can learn from Canada. And they can fortify themselves abroad by harnessing the need for change at home.

因此加拿大应努力保持选择的开放性。其他中等强国可以向加拿大学习，还可以通过借助国内改革的必要性来增强自己在海外的底气。

In a way, Mr Trump has done all democracies a favour, by giving them a reason to tackle long-standing problems that seemed too hard. The middle powers need to prepare for a dangerous world.

从某种意义上说，特朗普帮了所有民主国家一个忙——他给了它们一个理由，去解决那些看似过于棘手的长期难题。中等强国需要为一个危险的世界做好准备。

They should look to Mr Carney and Canada for an answer on what to do.

至于该怎么做，它们应该看看卡尼和加拿大给出的答案。
