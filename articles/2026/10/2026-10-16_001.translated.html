Video Cameras Are Everywhere-and Now They Are Being Hacked for War

摄像头无处不在——如今它们正被黑进战争

As U.S. and Israeli forces bombed Iran early this year, a poultry farmer in northern Israel decided to check on his chickens.

今年年初，美国和以色列军队轰炸伊朗之际，以色列北部一位家禽养殖户决定去看看他的鸡。

When he launched the app on his phone for the webcam outside his coop, he was alarmed to see that its language had been switched to Arabic.

当他打开手机里那个连接鸡舍外网络摄像头的应用时，惊恐地发现界面语言已经被切换成了阿拉伯语。

He contacted Israel's National Cyber Directorate, which found that hackers had taken control of the Chinese-made camera. They weren't interested in chickens.

他联系了以色列国家网络局，对方发现黑客已经控制了这台中国制造的摄像头。他们对鸡没兴趣。

The camera's orientation on the coop offered it a line of sight to the movements of Israeli security forces along the nearby border with Lebanon.

摄像头在鸡舍上的朝向，使它能望见附近黎巴嫩边境一带以色列安全部队的动向。

The cyber directorate said it helped the farmer harden the camera's security and adjust its viewing angle to stop capturing military operations. The farmer's webcam was one of an estimated 1.2 billion internet-connected cameras worldwide, according to cybersecurity consultants S-RM.

该网络局表示，他们帮助这位养殖户加固了摄像头的安全设置，并调整了拍摄角度，使其不再拍到军事行动。据网络安全咨询公司 S-RM 估计，这位农户的摄像头是全球约 12 亿台联网摄像头中的一台。

The devices have become a background fixture of daily life in the form of security systems, doorbells, traffic controls and even baby monitors—and potentially any of them are vulnerable to snooping, say cybersecurity experts and hackers.

这些设备以安防系统、门铃、交通监控乃至婴儿监视器的形式，已成为日常生活中习以为常的背景装置——网络安全专家和黑客都表示，其中任何一台都可能遭到窥探。

"We are now seeing the conversion of civilian surveillance into wartime intelligence collection," said Alexander Leslie, a national-security expert at threat-intelligence consultants Recorded Future, who sees a global "soft underbelly" in omnipresent digital eyeballs.

"我们现在正看到民用监控被转化为战时情报搜集，"威胁情报咨询机构 Recorded Future 的国家安全专家亚历山大·莱斯利说。他认为，无处不在的数字"眼睛"构成了全球性的"软肋"。

Intelligence agencies scour the internet for vulnerable cameras and robotically tap in to them by testing millions of passwords.

情报机构在互联网上搜罗存在漏洞的摄像头，并通过试错数以百万计的密码自动侵入它们。

Now, AI has made it even easier to hack into them and analyze their data at scale, transforming cameras that were designed to boost safety into tools for combatants to track, surveil and attack the enemy—often surpassing the abilities of human intelligence analysts.

如今，人工智能让入侵摄像头、大规模分析其数据变得更加容易——这些原本为提升安全而设计的摄像头，被变成了作战人员追踪、监视和打击敌人的工具，其能力往往超过人类情报分析员。

Algorithms digest endless reams of footage to find hidden gems and patterns for agents to monitor.

算法消化着无穷无尽的影像素材，从中找出隐藏的"珍宝"和规律，供情报人员盯梢。

Russia and Ukraine have ramped up camera-hacking in their war, aiming to spy on each other, find new targets and assess damage from their attacks.

俄罗斯和乌克兰在战争中加大了摄像头入侵的力度，目的是互相刺探、寻找新目标，并评估己方打击造成的破坏。

Israel, long a master of digital spycraft, hacked into Iranian traffic cameras to help forces collect intelligence used in the assassination of Iranian Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei earlier this year.

以色列素来是数字间谍技术的大师，它入侵了伊朗的交通摄像头，协助部队搜集情报——这些情报被用于今年早些时候对伊朗最高领袖阿里·哈梅内伊的暗杀。

Iran and its proxies have struck back by breaking into hundreds of Israeli webcams and security cameras that Israeli officials consider sensitive to national security, like the one on the chicken coop.

伊朗及其代理人展开了反击，入侵了数百个以色列网络摄像头和安防摄像头——以色列官员认为这些摄像头（比如鸡舍上那个）涉及国家安全敏感信息。

In 2024, Russian hackers commandeered residential webcams in Kyiv, including one installed by a condominium association to monitor the neighborhood.

2024 年，俄罗斯黑客劫持了基辅的住宅网络摄像头，其中包括一个由业主委员会安装、用于监控社区情况的摄像头。

Ukraine's security service, the SBU, said that Russia intended to use the hacked video feeds to find targets in the capital and "to adjust missile attacks on Ukraine," refining their aim for subsequent strikes.

乌克兰安全局（SBU）表示，俄罗斯打算利用这些被劫持的视频画面在首都寻找目标，并"调整对乌克兰的导弹袭击"，为后续打击校准目标。

The SBU said it has identified and secured, reoriented or blocked roughly 40,000 video cameras that posed potential security risks since Russia's 2022 invasion.

SBU 称，自俄罗斯 2022 年入侵以来，已识别并对大约 4 万台存在安全隐患的摄像头采取了加固、调整朝向或屏蔽措施。

Hacked cameras are taking over roles in targeting, damage assessments and other combat intelligence-gathering that were previously supplied by aerial reconnaissance or on-the-ground spies.

被入侵的摄像头正在接管目标定位、毁伤评估以及其他战场情报搜集工作——这些此前由空中侦察或地面间谍承担。

Penetrating a well-placed camera can get analysts closer to targets and carries less risk than infiltrating even the canniest secret agent. Video cameras operate silently, often in restricted areas.

攻入一个位置得当的摄像头，能让分析员更贴近目标，而且比渗透哪怕最精明的特工风险更小。摄像头静默运行，且常常位于禁区之内。

While private individuals and enterprise-network managers are increasingly attuned to cybersecurity, web-connected devices like cameras may have fewer built-in protections and not be consistently monitored, patched or updated, said Xu Zou, senior vice president for cloud security services at cybersecurity company Palo Alto Networks.

网络安全公司 Palo Alto Networks 云安全服务高级副总裁徐邹表示，尽管个人和企业网络管理者对网络安全的意识越来越强，但摄像头这类联网设备的内置防护可能更少，也未必被持续监控、打补丁或更新。

Information-security specialists inside companies and governments often aren't aware of connected cameras, which are frequently installed and operated by facilities managers or other non-IT teams, he said.

他说，企业和政府内部的信息安全专家往往并不知道这些联网摄像头的存在，因为它们通常由设施管理人员或其他非 IT 团队安装和运维。

Ondrej Svoboda in Prague was reading to his young children one evening in 2023 when the baby monitor in their room started emitting strange sounds of people talking and background music, as if from a bar.

2023 年的一个晚上，布拉格的翁德雷·斯沃博达正在给年幼的孩子读故事，房间里那台婴儿监视器突然传出奇怪的声音——有人说话声和背景音乐，像是从酒吧里传来的。

At first he thought his wife was using the two-way function but quickly realized that hackers had gotten into his system. "I didn't think this was something that could happen to me," recalled Svoboda, who worked at a cybersecurity company.

起初他以为是妻子在用双向通话功能，但很快意识到黑客已经侵入了他的系统。"我从没想过这种事会发生在我身上，"斯沃博达回忆说，他当时在一家网络安全公司工作。

"I was really scared-my heart was racing." Trying to avoid frightening his children, he silently stared into the camera to show the hackers he knew about them and then unplugged the camera.

"我真的吓坏了——心跳得厉害。"为了不吓到孩子，他默默盯着摄像头，向黑客表明自己已经发现了他们，然后拔掉了电源。

For days he suffered nightmares as he struggled to understand how he got hacked. Eventually he remembered he had tried connecting his webcams to another app, which likely was insecure.

接连几天他都做噩梦，苦苦思索自己是怎么被黑的。最终他想起来，自己曾尝试把摄像头连接到另一个应用，而那个应用很可能不安全。

Vulnerable accessories linked into a network are among hackers' prime targets, expanding what technicians call the attack surface. Often, cameras aren't the target, but serve as the weak link in a larger hacking attempt.

接入网络的有漏洞的配件是黑客的主要目标之一，它扩大了技术人员所说的"攻击面"。摄像头常常不是目标本身，而是在一次更大规模的入侵中充当薄弱环节。

Ransomware group Akira tapped an unprotected webcam to get around one organization's network-security systems and extort it, S-RM reported last year.

S-RM 去年报告称，勒索软件团伙 Akira 利用一台毫无防护的网络摄像头，绕过某机构的网络安全系统并对其进行勒索。

Once hackers are inside a local network, they can usually probe stored files, email accounts and other devices, including cameras.

黑客一旦进入本地网络，通常就能探查存储的文件、邮箱账号以及包括摄像头在内的其他设备。

"The Internet of Things is such a wild area that everything-whether you realize it or not—is an extension of your attack surface," said Paul Caron, S-RM head of cyber for the Americas.

"物联网是一片如此失控的领域——无论你意识到与否，每一样东西都是你攻击面的延伸，"S-RM 美洲区网络安全主管保罗·卡龙说。
