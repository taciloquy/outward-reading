Critical Thinking 课后思考
In this instance, an unforeseen situation arose in which protocols designed for elite competition and mass participation became blurred, creating ambiguity in how they were applied and understood.

但在本次事件中，出现了突发情况，使原本为精英竞赛和大规模群众参与设计的规程界限变得模糊，导致其在适用和理解上产生歧义。
