US Strikes Deal Over Greenland to End Row That Threatened NATO

美丹格陵兰达成安全协议，结束威胁北约的争端

The US, Denmark and Greenland struck a security deal that expands Washington's role on the Arctic island, ending for now a long-running diplomatic spat without ceding land to the US.

美国、丹麦和格陵兰达成一项安全协议，扩大华盛顿在这座北极岛屿上的作用，暂时结束了这场旷日持久的外交争端——且没有把土地割让给美国。

President Donald Trump said Friday that his administration had reached an agreement handing the US "permanent control over security" in Greenland. Leaders of Denmark and Greenland confirmed that an accord is expected to be signed next week at the United Nations General Assembly in New York.

特朗普总统周五表示，他的政府已达成协议，让美国获得格陵兰"安全事务上的永久控制权"。丹麦和格陵兰领导人确认，协议预计将于下周在纽约的联合国大会上签署。

Full details have yet to be released. Denmark's Prime Minister Mette Frederiksen said the pact "recognizes the sovereignty and territorial integrity" of the Danish kingdom and the Greenlandic people's right to self-determination, indicating that Trump gains no ownership or sovereignty over the island.

完整细节尚未公布。丹麦首相梅特·弗雷泽里克森表示，该协议"承认丹麦王国的主权和领土完整"，以及格陵兰人民的自决权——这意味着特朗普并未获得该岛的所有权或主权。

That marks a significant pullback from Trump's earlier push to control Greenland or make it part of the US. His repeated demands to acquire the territory had fueled distrust between Washington and its European allies, presenting one of the most serious strains on the North Atlantic Treaty Organization in decades.

相较特朗普此前试图控制格陵兰、或将其并入美国的努力，这是一次显著退让。他一再要求取得这片领土，加剧了华盛顿与欧洲盟友之间的互不信任，构成数十年来对北约最严重的压力之一。

For Denmark and Greenland, the agreement is significant because it appears to bring an end to a nearly two-year standoff without ceding land to the US, Marc Jacobsen, associate professor at the Institute for Strategy and War Studies at the Royal Danish Defence College. "Donald Trump needs to be seen as a winner," he said in an interview. "So a new agreement was necessary, allowing him to say: look, my pressure worked. " But when it comes to Trump's ambition to acquire or politically control Greenland, "he did not get his way on this," Jacobsen said.

丹麦皇家国防学院战略与战争研究所副教授马克·雅各布森说，对丹麦和格陵兰而言，这项协议意义重大，因为它似乎在不割让土地的前提下终结了近两年的对峙。"特朗普需要被看成赢家，"他在一次采访中说，"所以有必要搞一个新协议，让他可以说：看，我的施压奏效了。"但说到特朗普获取或政治上控制格陵兰的野心，"他在这件事上没能如愿，"雅各布森说。

Trump announced the arrangement on social media on Friday, saying it would give Washington the power to block US adversaries from establishing bases or a military presence in Greenland, as well as from making sensitive investments there. Trump also said the US would immediately begin expanding its military footprint on the island and work with Greenlanders on developing and building the facilities, all at "NO COST to the United States. "

特朗普周五在社交媒体上宣布了这一安排，称它将赋予华盛顿权力，阻止美国的对手在格陵兰建立基地或军事存在，以及进行敏感投资。特朗普还表示，美国将立即开始扩大在该岛的军事存在，并与格陵兰人合作开发和建设相关设施，而这一切对"美国零成本"。

Based on what Trump has disclosed so far, the new pact appears to grant Washington many of the same rights it already enjoys under the existing security arrangement, Jacobsen said. A 1951 agreement with Denmark, updated in 2004 and still in force, gives the US broad latitude to station forces in Greenland. Washington is obliged to consult and inform Copenhagen about its plans, but that was seen as a largely pro forma condition that required little more than notification.

雅各布森说，从特朗普目前披露的内容看，新协议似乎赋予了华盛顿许多它在现有安全安排下已经享有的同等权利。1951 年与丹麦签署、2004 年更新且仍然有效的一项协议，给予美国在格陵兰驻军的广泛自由度。华盛顿有义务就计划与哥本哈根磋商并通报，但这在很大程度被视为走过场的条件，几乎只需通知即可。

The deal includes some elements that appear to be new, said Jacobsen. One is a US veto over whether other countries can establish a military presence in Greenland. Another is that the arrangement would be permanent. The 1951 agreement is explicitly tied to a key NATO accord and therefore the alliance's existence. "This one is about the US and is not linked to NATO's lifespan," he said.

雅各布森说，协议中包含一些看似全新的内容。其一是美国对他国能否在格陵兰建立军事存在拥有否决权。其二是该安排将是永久性的。1951 年的那项协议明确与一项关键的北约协定挂钩，因而与联盟的存在绑定。"这份协议只关乎美国，与北约的存续无关，"他说。

Several important questions remain unanswered, Jacobsen said, including where any new bases would be located, what capabilities and personnel they would host, and who would pay for them. Another question is what Greenland receives in return, beyond the evident removal of the threat of US annexation. Greenland's Prime Minister Jens-Frederik Nielsen, who has previously ruled out ceding sovereignty, said he was encouraged by the accord, which "recognizes Greenland's interests and our place in the international cooperation. "

雅各布森说，仍有几个重要问题没有答案，包括任何新基地将选址何处、容纳何种能力与人员、由谁来买单。另一个问题是格陵兰能得到什么回报——除了显而易见的"美国吞并威胁解除"之外。此前明确排除出让主权的格陵兰首相延斯-弗雷德里克·尼尔森表示，他对这项协议感到鼓舞，它"承认了格陵兰的利益，以及我们在国际合作中的位置"。

Since returning to office last year, Trump had repeatedly pressured Denmark, a NATO ally, to give up Greenland. Tensions escalated in January after he refused to rule out military action, prompting Denmark and other NATO allies to send troops to the island. While the deployment was publicly presented as an exercise, Copenhagen sent forces with live ammunition who were prepared to blow up airport runways to prevent US troops from landing if Trump attempted to seize Greenland by force. The confrontation began to ease after Trump held talks with NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte at the World Economic Forum in Davos, Switzerland. There, the two agreed on the contours of a framework agreement regarding Greenland.

自去年重返执政以来，特朗普一再对这个北约盟友丹麦施压，要求其交出格陵兰。今年 1 月，在他拒绝排除动用武力之后，紧张局势升级，促使丹麦和其他北约盟国向该岛派兵。虽然这次部署对外被包装成演习，但哥本哈根派出的是携带实弹的部队，他们做好了在特朗普试图武力夺取格陵兰时炸毁机场跑道、阻止美军降落的准备。在特朗普于瑞士达沃斯世界经济论坛与北约秘书长马克·吕特会谈后，这场对抗开始缓和。双方在那里就格陵兰问题框架协议的轮廓达成了一致。

Trump's interest in taking over Greenland dates back to his first term in the White House. He has warned that Russia desired a foothold there and that only the US could keep them out. But Danish and Greenlandic leaders have long ruled out the idea of giving up control of the island.

特朗普想拿下格陵兰的兴趣，可以追溯到的他的第一个白宫任期。他警告说俄罗斯渴望在那里获得立足点，而只有美国能把他们挡在外面。但丹麦和格陵兰领导人长期以来一直排除放弃对该岛控制的可能。

"Next week could be a good week. For Greenland, Denmark and the United States alike," Danish Foreign Minister Lars Løkke Rasmussen said in a post on Instagram after the deal was struck. "Hopefully, a long period of uncertainty will be replaced by a binding agreement that strengthens security in the Arctic and North Atlantic, and thereby our shared security in NATO and Europe — while respecting the Kingdom's red lines. "

"下周可能是个好日子。对格陵兰、丹麦和美国都一样，"丹麦外交大臣拉尔斯·勒克·拉斯穆森在协议达成后于 Instagram 上发帖说，"希望这段长时间的不确定性能被一份有约束力的协议取代，加强北极和北大西洋的安全，进而加强我们在北约和欧洲的共同安全——同时尊重王国的红线。"
