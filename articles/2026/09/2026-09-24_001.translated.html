Wall Street expects Meta’s AI agent to shape into a new revenue engine

华尔街押注 Meta 的 AI 智能体成为新收入引擎

Wall Street has turned bullish on Meta Platforms, betting that its new AI agent Muse could open a new revenue stream for the social media giant and reshape the company's AI monetization prospects.

华尔街已经转而看多 Meta 平台公司，押注其新的 AI 智能体 Muse 能为这家社交媒体巨头开辟新的收入来源，并重塑公司的 AI 变现前景。

Muse, launched on September 8, is an AI assistant that can send emails, book travel and complete transactions such as selling a car on behalf of a user. Internally code named Hatch, the agent is built on the open-source framework OpenClaw and is designed to plug into a person's apps across categories including email, calendar, payments, health, shopping and the smart home. Meta has said users choose which apps to connect and can revoke that access at any time, and each agent runs on its own cloud-based virtual machine so it can keep working on tasks even when the user isn't actively engaged with the app. The basic version is free, with subscriptions priced at $20 and $100 a month for heavier use.

9 月 8 日推出的 Muse 是一款 AI 助手，可以发邮件、订行程，并完成诸如代用户卖车之类的交易。该智能体内部代号 Hatch，基于开源框架 OpenClaw 构建，设计上可接入个人在邮件、日历、支付、健康、购物和智能家居等各类应用。Meta 表示，用户自行选择连接哪些应用，并可随时撤销授权；每个智能体都运行在自己的云端虚拟机上，因此即使用户没有主动使用应用，它也能继续处理任务。基础版免费，订阅版分别为每月 20 美元和 100 美元，面向更重度使用。

Meta executives have said the product was originally slated for an April release but was held back for additional months of security work before the company judged it ready to launch. The rollout is currently limited to the US and Canada, available through a standalone app, on iOS and Android, and through WhatsApp, with Meta saying it plans to bring the agent to its Ray-Ban smart glasses in the future.

Meta 高管表示，该产品原定于 4 月发布，但为了多做几个月的安全工作而推迟，直到公司认为可以推出。目前发布范围限于美国和加拿大，可通过独立应用（iOS 和 Android）以及 WhatsApp 使用；Meta 称计划未来把该智能体带到其 Ray-Ban 智能眼镜上。

Since its launch, shares of Meta Platforms, have surged more than 20%, outpacing the "Magnificent 7" group of US tech companies during the period. Shares have added over $200 billion in market capitalization to touch their highest level in more than seven months as of last close. Shares were up 1% by noon on Tuesday, after surging more than 11% in the previous session. "While it is still early, we believe that Muse has the potential to become the most widely used consumer AI application since ChatGPT," J.P. Morgan analysts said in a note, crediting the app's early traction to strong product-market fit, Meta's distribution reach and a free tier that lowers the barrier to trial and repeat use.

自发布以来，Meta 平台公司的股价已上涨超过 20%，同期跑赢美国科技股"七巨头"组合。截至上一交易日收盘，其市值增加超过 2000 亿美元，触及七个多月来的最高水平。周二中午前股价上涨 1%，此前一个交易日暴涨逾 11%。"虽然还为时尚早，但我们认为 Muse 有潜力成为自 ChatGPT 以来使用最广泛的消费级 AI 应用，"摩根大通分析师在一份报告中说，并把该应用的早期 traction 归功于强劲的产品—市场契合度、Meta 的分发触达，以及降低试用和重复使用门槛的免费层级。

If compared on similar metrics to the first 12 days of OpenAI's ChatGPT, Muse has 1.8 million downloads versus 1.3 million for ChatGPT. The app briefly overtook ChatGPT as the top free app on Apple's App Store.

若以与 OpenAI 的 ChatGPT 上线头 12 天的同类指标相比，Muse 有 180 万次下载，而 ChatGPT 为 130 万次。该应用一度在苹果 App Store 免费榜上超过 ChatGPT，登顶榜首。

Early reviews suggest "functionality is resonating with users, particularly agent's ability to execute multi-step tasks and workflows with limited user intervention," BofA Global Research analysts said. A New York Times reporter who spent two weeks testing Muse described the agent successfully navigating a call with a dental insurer, flagging and canceling duplicate subscriptions after being linked to a bank account, and negotiating down the price of ceramics on Facebook Marketplace — though it struggled with tasks like signing into some third-party loyalty accounts.

早期评测显示，"功能正在引起用户共鸣，尤其是该智能体在用户介入极少的情况下执行多步骤任务和流程的能力，"美银全球研究部的分析师说。《纽约时报》一位记者用两周时间测试 Muse，说该智能体成功应对了与牙科保险公司的通话、在关联银行账户后标记并取消了重复订阅，还在 Facebook Marketplace 上把陶瓷用品的价格砍了下来——不过它在登录某些第三方忠诚计划账户这类任务上表现吃力。

Enthusiasm around Muse sparked a Wall Street tech rally on Monday, lifting Meta shares more than 11% and boosting chipmakers, including AMD, and Intel, with AMD crossing the $1 trillion valuation mark for the first time. "The market has interpreted Muse as a boon for the already significantly bottlenecked CPU market," said Michael O'Rourke, chief market strategist at JonesTrading.

围绕 Muse 的热情周一在华尔街点燃了一轮科技股行情：Meta 股价大涨逾 11%，并带动芯片商走高，包括 AMD 和英特尔；AMD 市值首次突破 1 万亿美元。"市场把 Muse 解读为对已经严重瓶颈化的 CPU 市场的一大利好，"JonesTrading 首席市场策略师迈克尔·奥罗克说。

Brokerage Jefferies estimated that if Muse could see 1 billion users by end of 2027 and at least 3% transition to a paid version, it could result in $10.8 billion in annualized revenue, as the brokerage raised its price target on Meta's stock to $875. UBS Global Wealth Management analysts said in a note Meta's Muse provides "early evidence that consumer AI agents could achieve broad adoption, offering new AI monetization opportunities beyond enterprise application."

券商杰富瑞估计，如果 Muse 到 2027 年底能拥有 10 亿用户，且至少有 3% 转化为付费版，就可能带来 108 亿美元的年化收入；该券商同时把 Meta 的目标价上调至 875 美元。瑞银全球财富管理的分析师在报告中说，Meta 的 Muse 提供了"消费级 AI 智能体能够实现广泛采用的早期证据，带来企业级应用之外的新 AI 变现机会"。

Of the 64 brokerages that cover Meta's stock, 57 rate it "buy" or higher with a median price target of $760, according to data compiled by LSEG. "Prior to Muse, investors had completely dismissed the idea that Meta would be able to release a personalized consumer AI agent, favoring (Alphabet) instead," Jefferies analysts led by Brent Thill said.

根据伦敦证券交易所集团（LSEG）汇编的数据，在覆盖 Meta 股票的 64 家券商中，有 57 家给予"买入"或更高评级，目标价中位数为 760 美元。"在 Muse 之前，投资者完全否定了 Meta 能发布个性化消费级 AI 智能体的想法，转而看好（Alphabet），"由布伦特·蒂尔领衔的杰富瑞分析师说。

Muse's rise hasn't gone unchallenged. Amazon has blocked the agent from accessing its online store, arguing that unauthorized AI agents violate the terms customers agree to, and Meta separately announced a Shopify partnership to cover some of Muse's shopping functions instead. The standoff echoes a similar fight Amazon waged last year against Perplexity's Comet browser agent, a case in which a federal appeals court sided against Amazon's attempt to block bot-driven purchases. Meta's Muse follows a series of setbacks with its Llama 4 models last year, including criticism over the benchmark results reported for early versions.

Muse 的崛起并非没有阻力。亚马逊已阻止该智能体访问其线上商店，理由是未经授权的 AI 智能体违反了客户同意的条款；Meta 则另外宣布与 Shopify 合作，覆盖 Muse 的部分购物功能。这一对峙让人想起亚马逊去年对 Perplexity 的 Comet 浏览器智能体发起的类似争斗——在那起案件中，一家联邦上诉法院否决了亚马逊阻止机器人代客下单的企图。Muse 的推出，也接续在 Meta 去年 Llama 4 模型遭遇的一系列挫折之后，其中包括对其早期版本所报告基准测试结果的批评。
