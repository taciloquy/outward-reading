The Great Stuff Transfer Has a $750 Billion Golden Lining

"物品大转移"背后藏着 7500 亿美元的黄金

When Andrew Stinger's mother, Nancy, died of cancer in 2024, her grief-stricken husband and sons began the journey of managing her estate in probate court — a process that would stretch almost two years. Then, in January 2025, Stinger's father, Bill, died unexpectedly. And yet, there was one happy spot amid the heartbreak: the jewelry.

2024 年安德鲁·斯廷格的母亲南希因癌症去世后，悲痛欲绝的丈夫和儿子们开始了在遗嘱认证法庭处理她遗产的历程——这个过程前后拖了近两年。接着在 2025 年 1 月，斯廷格的父亲比尔又意外离世。然而在这一片心碎之中，有一处让人欣慰的地方：那些珠宝。

"There were pieces that my mom inherited from her mother that it made sense for my mom's sisters to have, and to be able to pass down to their daughters," Stinger says.

"有些首饰是我母亲从她母亲那里继承来的，由我母亲的姐妹们来保管、再传给她们自己的女儿，是合情合理的，"斯廷格说。

"My mom's engagement ring will go to her only granddaughter when she grows up.

"我母亲的订婚戒指，等她唯一的孙女长大后会传给她。"

These things are part of our family lineage. "

"这些东西是我们家族血脉的一部分。"

In the next 20 years, some $93 trillion in assets in the US will be passed down from baby boomers and the Silent Generation to their heirs. Along with that will come a whole lot of physical items: the china, silver, coin and stamp collections, and millions and millions of baseball cards that make up what's known as the Great Stuff Transfer.

未来 20 年里，美国将有约 93 万亿美元资产从婴儿潮一代和"沉默的一代"传给继承人。随之而来的还有大量实物：瓷器、银器、硬币和邮票收藏，以及数以千万计的棒球卡——它们构成了所谓"物品大转移"。

Jewelry is the rare exception. The reasons are both obvious and intimate: To begin with, silver, gold, platinum and gemstones have material value. With gold prices climbing back toward their January high (when the spot price was $5,589.38 per ounce, five times what it was 10 years earlier) and silver on a similar trajectory, it's a good time to come into some precious metal. According to a 2026 report from the jewelry tracking startup Unvault Inc., the average US household is sitting on $5,700 in unappraised gold jewelry, with roughly $750 billion in gold weight in drawers across the country. Add to that silver, platinum and pieces with artistic merit that can command high premiums.

珠宝是个少见的例外。原因既显而易见又带着私人情感：首先，白银、黄金、铂金和宝石本身就有材质价值。随着金价回升逼近 1 月的高点（当时现货价格为每盎司 5589.38 美元，是十年前的五倍），白银也走出类似轨迹，现在正是接手一些贵金属的好时候。据珠宝追踪初创公司 Unvault 2026 年的一份报告，美国家庭平均持有 5700 美元的未经估价黄金首饰，全国抽屉里的黄金总重量价值约 7500 亿美元。此外还有白银、铂金，以及那些能卖出高溢价、具有艺术价值的物件。

Unlike art, old furniture or Dickens houses, the infrastructure for offloading jewelry at almost any price point is expansive and well established. From estate-sale specialists, pawnshops and local jewelers to high-end auction houses to eBay and the RealReal, there are options for pretty much everything you could inherit.

与艺术品、旧家具或狄更斯主题小屋不同，出手珠宝的基础设施在任何价位上都广泛而成熟。从遗产拍卖专家、当铺、本地珠宝商，到高端拍卖行，再到 eBay 和 The RealReal——你继承到的几乎任何东西都有出路。

If you've come into some jewelry or even expect to, it's crucial to know its value — for insurance, for deciding who in a family will get what and for potential division of cash outcomes, says Nidhi Singhvi. A former hedge funder who later worked in crypto, Singhvi started Unvault last October to bring transparency to the secondhand jewelry market. "I'm Indian. We do gold very differently in India," she says. Gold jewelry, like 22-karat bracelets and necklaces, are "transferred from generation to generation with the idea that it can be used for anything," she says. "My mom used gold to send me for a school exchange program. The market is very transparent, and there is very good financial infrastructure for selling it. "

Unvault 创始人妮迪·辛格维说，如果你已经继承了、或预计会继承一些珠宝，了解它的价值至关重要——这关系到保险、决定家族里谁分到什么，以及可能如何分配现金结果。辛格维曾做对冲基金，后在加密行业工作，去年 10 月创办 Unvault，为二手珠宝市场带来透明度。"我是印度人。在印度，我们对待黄金的方式很不一样，"她说。黄金首饰，比如 22K 的手镯和项链，是"代代相传的，理念是它可以拿来应付任何用途"。"我母亲曾用黄金送我去参加学校交换项目。那里的市场非常透明，出售黄金的金融基础设施也很好。"

To Singhvi, the American system of aggressive storefronts with big "WE BUY GOLD" posters in the window was a turnoff. She knew a lot of women who wouldn't set foot in a pawnshop. Instead, her platform uses artificial intelligence to assess the jewelry in clients' collections and assign it a clear value based on the weight of the metal and the carats and quality of the gemstones. With Unvault, you can build a digital portfolio of what you own by sending in photographs and watch its value rise and fall with the price of gold. When you're ready to offload it, you can sell directly to Unvault, which will deduct a 10% to 20% fee and then pass the interesting pieces and stones to a network of estate jewelers, and melt down the rest.

在辛格维看来，美国那种橱窗上贴着大大的"我们收购黄金"海报、咄咄逼人的门店让人反感。她认识很多女性根本不会踏进当铺。相反，她的平台用人工智能评估客户收藏中的珠宝，并根据金属重量、宝石的克拉与品质给出清晰定价。通过 Unvault，你可以把自己的藏品建立成数字档案——发照片进去，然后看着它的价值随金价起伏。当你准备出手时，可以直接卖给 Unvault，它会扣除 10% 到 20% 的费用，再把有意思的款式和宝石交给一个遗产珠宝商网络，其余的则熔掉。

"For me, a key part of such intergenerational transitions is not being afraid to repurpose, reset or recast things," says Andrew James Hamilton, a curator at the Art Institute of Chicago. "My grandmother had a pair of opal earrings that my mother inherited and turned into two tie tacks for her gay grandsons, my brother and me," he says. "Earrings can become cuff links, rings, pendants. And with the price of precious metals as high as it is, things can be melted down and recast as something else equally precious. "

"对我来说，这类代际传承的关键一环，是不要害怕把东西改作他用、重新镶嵌或重铸，"芝加哥艺术博物馆策展人安德鲁·詹姆斯·汉密尔顿说，"我祖母有一对蛋白石耳环，我母亲继承后把它改成了两枚领带夹，给她两个同性恋外孙——我哥哥和我，"他说，"耳环可以变成袖扣、戒指、吊坠。而在贵金属价格这么高的当下，东西可以熔掉重铸成同样珍贵的其他物件。"

"After the Holocaust, my grandparents immigrated to Israel, and my grandmother always wore a gold Magen David Jewish star and an infinity necklace that looked like an 'S' to honor her roots and love of my grandfather," says Ronnit Hershowitz, a marketing director in New York. "It was passed down to me. My grandfather always got choked up seeing it because it was more than just a necklace. For them, it represented eternal strength and love. "

"大屠杀之后，我的祖父母移民到以色列。我祖母一直戴着一枚金的大卫之星和一个看起来像'S'的无限符号项链，以纪念她的根脉和对祖父的爱，"纽约一位市场总监罗尼特·赫肖维茨说，"它传给了我。我祖父每次看到它都会哽咽，因为那不只是一串项链。对他们来说，它代表着永恒的力量与爱。"
