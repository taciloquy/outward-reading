The Washington Post · 标题
Venezuela vowed not to ‘hand over’ its oil. Trump aims to grab a windfall. — The deal cements the United States’ long-term dominance over Venezuela, which has become a neo-colony since the U.S. military’s capture of then-President Nicolás Maduro in January.

vow /vaʊ/ v. & n.
英文释义：vow (v.) means to make a serious and formal promise to do something or to behave in a particular way
中文释义：郑重宣誓、公开立誓

例句一：The chairman vowed to restore profitability within two years, staking his reputation on the promise. 董事长立誓要在两年内恢复盈利，把自己的声誉都押在了这个承诺上。
例句二：Having vowed never to return to the industry, she surprised everyone by launching a rival firm. 她曾发誓再不涉足这一行，却出人意料地创办了一家竞争公司。
