The threat of annihilation from nuclear weapons is simple and easy to explain. For one part, a single warhead can level an entire city. For another, the mere launch of a single missile all but assures the release of all of them. Likewise, the AI superintelligence of the Terminator films is both straightforward and obviously threatening. Skeleton-like metal robots want only to destroy humanity. Real AI is not so straightforward. I used it on the morning of Coxon’s post to help me nail down the quantity of various sugars I would use in a gelato recipe. By the afternoon, it had undergone a costume change, into the angel of death.
核武器带来的毁灭威胁，简单，好解释。一来，一枚弹头就能把整座城市抹平；二来，只要一枚导弹升空，剩下的几乎注定倾巢而出。同样，《终结者》里的AI超级智能也是直白得一目了然的威胁——骷髅一样的金属机器人，脑子里只装着一件事：灭掉人类。可现实中的AI没这么直白。考克森发帖那天上午，我还在用它帮我敲定一份意式冰淇淋配方里几种糖该放多少；到了下午，它换了身行头，摇身一变成了死神。

冷战最紧张那些年里，我们曾对威胁有过一种清清楚楚的感觉——那种清晰，我们大概再也找不回来了，如果"找回"这个词用得还算贴切的话。
