Ukraine hits Moscow with ‘largest ever’ drone attack

乌克兰对莫斯科发动"史上最大规模"无人机袭击

Ukraine has hit Moscow with what local authorities said was the largest ever drone attack on the capital, setting a major oil refinery ablaze and killing at least two people, as voting in Russia's stage-managed parliamentary elections entered its final day.

乌克兰对莫斯科发动了当地当局所称的"史上最大规模"无人机袭击：首都一座大型炼油厂被点燃，至少两人死亡。此时，俄罗斯这场走过场式的议会选举投票正进入最后一天。

Moscow's mayor Sergei Sobyanin said air defences shot down 450 drones as they approached the capital on Saturday night, calling the attack "unprecedented". Sobyanin claimed the attack was "clearly planned to disrupt the elections", adding that "the enemy did not succeed". Several drones hit the Moscow Refinery in the south of the capital, one of Russia's largest.

莫斯科市长谢尔盖·索比亚宁说，周六夜间防空系统在无人机逼近首都时击落了 450 架，称这次袭击"前所未有"。索比亚宁声称，袭击"显然是为了干扰选举而策划的"，并补充说"敌人没有得逞"。数架无人机击中位于首都南部、俄罗斯最大的炼油厂之一的莫斯科炼油厂。

Videos on social media showed a drone exploding into the refinery, with plumes of black smoke shrouding the sunrise, and several areas of the facility on fire. A nearby power station, warehouse and market were also hit. Drones also hit residential buildings near the refinery and at least 11 suburban towns in the surrounding region, authorities said. Two people died in the attacks and a further 20 people were injured, including three children, according to Andrei Vorobyov, governor of the Moscow region.

社交媒体上的视频显示，一架无人机在炼油厂内爆炸，黑色烟柱遮蔽了日出，厂区内多处起火。附近的一座电站、一处仓库和一个市场也被击中。当局说，无人机还击中了炼油厂附近的住宅楼，以及周边地区至少 11 个郊区城镇。莫斯科州州长安德烈·沃罗比约夫说，袭击造成两人死亡、另有 20 人受伤，其中包括三名儿童。

The consequences of the Moscow attack extended beyond the initial fires. Russia's Moscow refinery halted crude processing after both of its main crude distillation units were damaged, according to industry sources. One accounts for 53 per cent of the refinery's capacity and the other for the remaining 47 per cent. Repairs could take several weeks. The plant processed 11.6mn tonnes of crude in 2024 and produced 2.9mn tonnes of gasoline and 3.2mn tonnes of diesel.

莫斯科袭击的后果超出了最初的火情。据业内消息人士称，在该厂两套主要原油蒸馏装置双双受损后，莫斯科炼油厂已停止原油加工。其中一套占该厂产能的 53%，另一套占剩余的 47%。维修可能需要数周时间。该厂 2024 年加工了 1160 万吨原油，产出 290 万吨汽油和 320 万吨柴油。

Flights were grounded at all three of Moscow's main airports. Russia's defence ministry claimed to have shot down a total of 1,110 drones across the country overnight. Kyiv and Moscow have continued striking each other's energy infrastructure throughout the week despite Donald Trump's claim on Monday that the warring sides had agreed to halt such attacks, which the US president blamed for the surge in global diesel prices. Trump has not commented on the attacks, which Russia and Ukraine continued within hours of his statement.

莫斯科三大主要机场的航班全部停飞。俄罗斯国防部称，全国范围内一夜共击落 1110 架无人机。尽管特朗普周一声称交战双方已同意停止此类袭击（他还称这些袭击是全球柴油价格飙升的元凶），基辅和莫斯科整周仍在持续打击对方的能源基础设施。特朗普对这些袭击未作评论——在他表态后数小时内，俄乌双方仍在继续攻击。

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy posted on social media that Ukraine had used domestically produced drones and cruise missiles to target facilities he said earned "billions of dollars that support the war machine". Zelenskyy said a plan to de-escalate the strikes on energy and food export infrastructure was "on the table" but required "political will".

乌克兰总统弗拉基米尔·泽连斯基在社交媒体上发帖说，乌克兰使用了国产无人机和巡航导弹，打击那些他称为赚取"数十亿美元、支撑战争机器"的设施。泽连斯基表示，一项为打击能源和粮食出口基础设施"降温"的计划"摆在桌面上"，但需要"政治意愿"。

The damage has added urgency to diplomatic efforts to halt attacks on energy infrastructure. Zelenskyy is due to meet Trump at the United Nations, where an energy truce with Russia is expected to be discussed. Ukraine says it is prepared to take matching steps if Russia stops targeting its energy sector, critical infrastructure and food exports. Russia has significantly ramped up the use of jet-powered drones to attack Ukraine in recent months and in July fired the largest number of ballistic missiles at Ukraine in a single month since the start of the war. The latter is a huge problem for Ukraine as they can only be intercepted by Patriot air defence missiles that are in short supply. Kyiv also fears Moscow could intensify attacks on heating and power facilities this winter and is seeking additional US-made Patriot air-defence missiles.

这些破坏为制止袭击能源基础设施的外交努力增添了紧迫性。泽连斯基将在联合国与特朗普会面，预计双方将讨论与俄罗斯的能源休战。乌克兰表示，如果俄罗斯停止针对其能源部门、关键基础设施和粮食出口，它准备采取对等的措施。近几个月俄罗斯大幅增加了喷气动力无人机攻击乌克兰的使用；7 月，其向乌克兰发射的弹道导弹数量创下开战以来单月最高。后者对乌克兰是个大麻烦，因为它们只能由供应短缺的"爱国者"防空导弹拦截。基辅还担心莫斯科可能在今冬加强对供热和电力设施的攻击，正寻求更多美制"爱国者"防空导弹。

Russia has rejected calls for a ceasefire of attacks on energy and other infrastructure ahead of what is expected to be a major campaign of air strikes against Ukraine this winter. Russian President Vladimir Putin expects the strikes to strengthen Moscow's position and help extract concessions from Ukraine, according to people familiar with his thinking.

在预计今冬将对乌克兰发动大规模空袭战役之前，俄罗斯已拒绝停止袭击能源和其他基础设施的呼吁。据熟悉普京想法的人士说，俄罗斯总统弗拉基米尔·普京指望这些打击能强化莫斯科的地位，并帮助从乌克兰榨取让步。

Polls in the three-day parliamentary elections in Russia, the first since Putin ordered the invasion of Ukraine in 2022, were set to close on Sunday evening. Turnout at noon local time on Sunday was 48 per cent, according to the central election commission. The vote has proceeded without drama even by Russian standards as an array of Kremlin-run, pro-war parties compete for seats in parliament. Russia's supreme court removed Yabloko, the only party that had expressed even mild opposition to the war, from the ballot last month.

俄罗斯这场为期三天的议会选举投票（这是 2022 年普京下令入侵乌克兰以来的首次）定于周日傍晚结束。据中央选举委员会数据，周日当地时间中午的投票率为 48%。即便以俄罗斯的标准衡量，这次投票也进行得毫无波澜——一批由克里姆林宫操控、支持战争的政党在争夺议会席位。俄罗斯最高法院上个月把唯一曾对战争表达过哪怕温和反对的政党"亚博卢"从选票上移除。
