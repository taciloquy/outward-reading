Soaring diesel prices are clobbering Trump's MAGA faithful, exposing rift

柴油价格飙升重创特朗普的 MAGA 基本盘，暴露党内裂痕

Pain at the pump for rural Republican is shifting the political map

农村共和党人在加油站承受的痛苦，正在改变政治版图。

Diesel prices soaring to record highs this past week have caused Republican candidates in solid-red parts of the country to call for an end to President Donald Trump's war with Iran and for a ban on diesel exports, as the issue rattles the party's MAGA base in a way that few have in this midterm election year. "Michigan families are struggling with the price of everyday life," Mike Rogers, the state's GOP nominee for U.S. Senate, said in an ad this past week.

上周柴油价格飙升至历史新高，促使全国深红选区的共和党候选人呼吁结束特朗普总统与伊朗的战争，并要求禁止柴油出口——在中期选举年，很少有议题能像这样动摇该党的"让美国再次伟大"（MAGA）基本盘。"密歇根的家庭正在为日常生活开支苦苦挣扎，"该州共和党联邦参议员候选人迈克·罗杰斯在上周的一则广告中说。

The war has sent oil prices surging, with disruptions to shipping through the Strait of Hormuz, a vital route for global energy supplies, reverberating through fuel markets. But while poll after poll has shown voters concerned about the cost of gasoline, it's the price of diesel — a far less obvious consumer product — that has riled up the industrial and agricultural interests that have been among the most faithful Republican supporters.

这场战争推高了油价：经由霍尔木兹海峡（全球能源供应的关键通道）的航运受阻，冲击波传导至燃料市场。然而，尽管一项又一项民调显示选民担心汽油价格，真正激怒工业与农业利益集团——他们一直是共和党最忠实的支持者——的却是柴油价格，一种远不那么显眼的消费品。

The political fallout is landing in deep-red voting districts the hardest. Federal data shows these tend to be the places where diesel makes up the largest proportion of the overall fuel mix. This is especially the case in farming communities, where diesel powers the fuel-thirsty tractors and other machinery that the agriculture industry relies on.

政治后果在深红选区落地最重。联邦数据显示，这些地方往往是柴油在整体燃料结构中占比最高的地区。农业社区尤其如此——柴油驱动着农业所依赖的那些"喝油"的拖拉机和其他机械。

That has led lawmakers to demand what was unthinkable in the GOP only a couple of months ago: a ban on diesel exports from the United States. The proposal seems to make sense on its face: restrict the flow of the fuel out of the U.S. to boost the supply domestically, quickly lowering prices. The problem is that almost nobody who understands oil markets — including Energy Secretary Chris Wright — believes it would work out that way. Analysts who have been modeling the impact of a diesel export ban are consistently warning it would create chaos and hurt consumers.

这导致议员们提出了几个月前在共和党内还不可想象的要求：禁止美国出口柴油。这个提议表面上说得通：限制燃料流出美国以增加国内供应，从而迅速压低价格。问题在于，几乎所有懂石油市场的人——包括能源部长克里斯·赖特——都不相信事情会这样发展。一直在建模推演柴油出口禁令影响的分析师们反复警告，这将造成混乱并伤害消费者。

The Gulf Coast refineries that typically export diesel abroad would get overloaded with a glut of crude that would have to be diverted to storage. The diesel they make would mostly be stranded in their region, because there are no pipelines to get it across the country.

通常向海外出口柴油的墨西哥湾沿岸炼厂，会被一股过剩原油压垮，不得不将其转入储罐。它们生产的柴油大部分会滞留在本地区，因为美国没有把它们运往全国各地的管道。

Parts of the country that rely on imports of diesel could then see prices soar even higher than they are now. "It is not at all clear that the national price of diesel would fall," the Energy Policy Research Foundation said Thursday in a report.

那些依赖进口柴油的地区，价格随后可能涨到比现在还高。"柴油的全国价格是否会下降，完全不清楚，"能源政策研究基金会周四在一份报告中说。

In Iowa, where farmer anger over the price of diesel has reached a boiling point and endangered the GOP's hold over a Senate seat in a state Trump won by 13 percentage points in 2024, conservative lawmakers are rushing to embrace an export ban. The Iowa Corn Growers Association, which has endorsed all Republicans in this year's Iowa congressional races, wrote to the state's delegation in Washington this past week pleading for action to lower diesel prices.

在艾奥瓦州，农民对柴油价格的愤怒已达沸点，危及共和党对这个参议院席位的掌控——2024 年特朗普曾在该州以 13 个百分点胜出。保守派议员正急于拥抱出口禁令。今年艾奥瓦国会选举中背书了全部共和党候选人的艾奥瓦玉米种植者协会，上周致信该州驻华盛顿代表团，恳请采取行动压低柴油价格。

The record highs are especially ill-timed because they hit right at harvest, the association noted. "This time of year should be positive for Iowa farmers, but the concern of affordability looms large as diesel prices go above $6 per gallon," the letter said. "This is accelerating the economic and mental stress farmers are feeling."

协会指出，创纪录的高价来得尤其不是时候，因为它正好撞上收割季。"每年这个时候对艾奥瓦农民本该是积极的，但随着柴油价格突破每加仑 6 美元，对可负担性的担忧沉重压来，"信中写道，"这正在加剧农民所承受的经济和精神压力。"

Steve Kuiper, a farmer and head of the association, said that diesel is indispensable to his work. As the harvest gets in gear, the tractor-trailers that deliver equipment to farms and haul away harvested crops, as well as the tractors and combines and harvesters that do the work, all run on diesel, Kuiper said. His own operation uses some 500 gallons of diesel per day, he said. Fuel he contracted for last year is running at about half the cost of fuel purchased now; topping off one truck Thursday cost $500, Kuiper said. "Farming is not a huge-margin business. This quickly cuts into it," he said.

农民、该协会负责人史蒂夫·凯珀说，柴油对他的工作不可或缺。随着收割全面展开，向农场运送设备、把收获的作物拉走的牵引挂车，以及干活的拖拉机、联合收割机，全都烧柴油。他说，他自己的农场每天大约要用 500 加仑柴油。他去年签约锁定的燃料，成本约为现在采购的一半；周四给一辆卡车加满油花了 500 美元。"农业不是高利润的生意。这很快就吃掉了利润，"他说。

The price of fertilizer is going up at the same time, he said, and all of those costs will be passed on to consumers. Kuiper does not specifically blame Trump or other Republicans for the situation, which he said has been building for years and could be mitigated by steps such as an embargo on diesel exports or allowing more ethanol to be mixed in with petroleum products. But the situation puts all politicians in a negative light. "All our elected officials in D.C. need to do their job and get things taken care of," Kuiper said.

他说，化肥价格同时也在上涨，而这些成本最终都会转嫁给消费者。凯珀并没有特别指责特朗普或其他共和党人，他说这种局面是多年累积的，可以通过诸如禁止柴油出口、或允许在石油产品中掺入更多乙醇等措施来缓解。但这一局面让所有政客都显得难看。"我们在华盛顿的所有民选官员都需要做好本职工作，把事情处理好，"凯珀说。
