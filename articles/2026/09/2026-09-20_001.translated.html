Donald Trump fails to bend the Federal Reserve to his will

特朗普没能让美联储屈从于他的意志

Kevin Warsh has rebuffed Donald Trump’s demands for low borrowing costs, as the president’s pick to run the Federal Reserve led it to the first rate rise since 2023 less than four months into the job. The central bank chair on Wednesday offered the strongest evidence yet that he is prepared to disregard Trump when he corralled all 12 rate setters on the Federal Open Market Committee into backing a quarter-point rate rise. “The plain fact is that inflation is too high and has been for too long,” Warsh said, as he repeatedly told journalists that he would be “hard pressed” to describe current interest rates as so high that they would slow the economy.

凯文·沃什拒绝了唐纳德·特朗普关于压低借贷成本的要求——这位总统亲自挑选的美联储掌门人上任不到四个月，就带领美联储做出了 2023 年以来的首次加息。这位央行主席周三拿出了迄今最有力的证据，表明他准备无视特朗普：他把联邦公开市场委员会全部 12 位利率决策者拧成一股绳，一致支持加息 25 个基点。"明摆着的事实是，通胀太高了，而且已经太高太久了，"沃什说；他反复对记者表示，他"很难"把当前利率形容为高到会拖慢经济的水平。

Warsh’s signal to Wall Street--just weeks before pivotal midterm elections--that he is ready to take further measures to tame the inflation pulsing across the US economy contrasts sharply with the president’s understanding that he was choosing a Fed boss who “certainly wants to cut rates”.

沃什在关键的中期选举前几周向华尔街发出的信号——他准备采取进一步措施，驯服在美国经济中涌动的通胀——与总统的理解形成鲜明反差。特朗普此前以为，自己选的是一位"当然想降息"的美联储掌门人。

Trump was quick to hit out at the Fed on Wednesday, saying on his Truth Social platform that “Interest Rates in the United States should be 1%, or less because we are the Best Credit in the World--BY FAR”. Trump later said he had confidence in Warsh, with whom he has a longstanding personal relationship, and deflected blame on to the Fed board, which includes former chair Jay Powell, whom the president has previously called a “moron” for not lowering rates. It also includes governor Lisa Cook, whom Trump tried to sack before he was stopped by the Supreme Court. “I told Kevin, I said, you might as well vote with the board because it’s just not going to matter. The board is very hostile,” Trump told reporters.

周三特朗普迅速对美联储开火，在自己的 Truth Social 平台上说："美国的利率应该在 1% 或更低，因为我们是世界上信用最好的国家——远远好过别人。"特朗普后来表示他信任沃什（两人有长期私交），并把责任推给美联储理事会——其中包含前主席杰伊·鲍威尔，总统曾因他不降息而骂其"蠢货"；也包括理事丽莎·库克，特朗普曾试图将她解职，但被最高法院阻止。"我告诉凯文，我说，你还不如跟着理事会投票，因为这根本无所谓。理事会非常敌对，"特朗普对记者说。

Trump’s apparent concession that he is unable to bend the Fed to his will comes as his tariffs and the Middle East conflict have conspired to inflame an affordability crisis that he has dismissed as a “hoax”. “There’s a lot of very clear evidence that the policies of the White House--tariffs and the conflict in the Middle East--have pushed up inflation,” said Claudia Sahm, a former Fed official who is now chief economist at New Century Advisors.

特朗普这番话等于承认自己无法让美联储屈从于他的意志。此时，他的关税和中东冲突共同加剧了一场他斥为"骗局"的生活成本危机。"有大量非常清楚的证据表明，白宫的政策——关税和中东冲突——推高了通胀，"前美联储官员、现为新世纪顾问公司首席经济学家的克劳迪娅·萨姆说。

Since Warsh’s nomination in January, the big inflation driver has been a rise in petrol and diesel prices--an increase that has cost American households an extra $108bn since the outbreak of the president’s war with Iran, according to a Brown University tracker. “Trump’s tariffs and the war in Iran are driving prices up,” said Jeff Colgan, professor at Brown University. “To a large extent, the inflation that the Fed is fighting is a problem of President Trump’s own making.”

自沃什 1 月获得提名以来，通胀的主要推手是汽油和柴油价格上涨——据布朗大学的一项追踪，自总统对伊朗开战以来，这一涨幅已让美国家庭多支出 1080 亿美元。"特朗普的关税和伊朗战争正在推高价格，"布朗大学教授杰夫·科尔根说，"在很大程度上，美联储正在对抗的通胀是特朗普总统自己造成的问题。"

Kush Desai, Trump’s spokesperson on economic matters, described the Fed’s move as “unfortunate”, claiming it would “punish businesses who are trying to expand and grow” ”To the extent that we still do have inflation, as the president and others have noted, it’s entirely driven by an energy supply shock, by what’s going on with oil prices in the Middle East,” Desai told Fox News.

特朗普的经济事务发言人库什·德赛称美联储此举"令人遗憾"，声称它会"惩罚那些试图扩张和成长的企业"。"就我们确实仍存在通胀而言——正如总统和其他人指出的——它完全是由能源供应冲击、由中东油价的情况造成的，"德赛对福克斯新闻说。

While another rate rise at the next Fed vote--set for late October, days before the midterms--is seen by some as unlikely, markets are pricing in at least one more increase before the end of the year. Short-term US Treasury yields, which reflect monetary policy expectation, rose sharply in response to Wednesday’s decision as traders interpreted Warsh’s remarks as hawkish.

尽管有人认为在下一次美联储议息会议（定于 10 月下旬、中期选举前几天）再度加息不太可能，但市场已计入年底前至少还会再加一次。反映货币政策预期的美国短期国债收益率周三应声大涨，因为交易员把沃什的言论解读为鹰派。

But many said Wednesday’s increase had helped allay those concerns. “Fed credibility is already on the line because we’ve had more than five years of high inflation--the longest bout of inflation since the late1970s, early 1980s, and it has compounded,” said Diane Swonk, chief economist at KPMG US. “Nobody necessarily wants to be at the helm at a pivotal moment, but Kevin Warsh is there. And he did his job. “

但许多人表示，周三的加息有助于缓解这些担忧。"美联储的信誉已经悬于一线，因为我们已经历五年多的高通胀——这是自 1970 年代末、1980 年代初以来最长的一轮通胀，而且还在累积，"毕马威美国首席经济学家黛安·斯旺克说，"没人一定想在关键时刻掌舵，但凯文·沃什就在那个位置上。而他做了他该做的事。"

When quizzed about the president’s likely response to the decision earlier in the day, Warsh offered only a wry smile. “What the president does and doesn’t like is his prerogative,” said Joe LaVorgna, a former economic counsellor to US Treasury secretary Scott Bessent who is now chief economist for the Americas at SMBC Nikko Securities. “But his views aren’t going to persuade the committee if they’re inconsistent with the fundamentals.”

当天早些时候被问及总统可能对这一决定作何反应时，沃什只是露出了一丝苦笑。"总统喜欢什么、不喜欢什么，是他的特权，"曾任美国财政部长斯科特·贝森特经济顾问、现任 SMBC 日兴证券美洲首席经济学家的乔·拉沃格纳说，"但如果他的看法与基本面不一致，就说服不了委员会。"
